Fyld is hiring: DevOps Engineer

At Fyld, we believe the future is built with people, technology, and strong values.

We are a Portuguese consulting company that operates with transparency, respect, and a focus on everyone's growth.

Here, every project is an opportunity to build reliable, scalable and automated technology solutions.

Our philosophy?

Code for Big Solutions

— because we don't just build technology to make things work, we build solutions that enable teams to deliver better and faster.

We're looking for an experienced

DevOps Engineer

with strong expertise in automation, CI/CD and infrastructure, and a passion for improving the way software is built, deployed and operated.

By joining Fyld, you'll be part of a team where every member is challenged to innovate, collaborate and grow.

Your Profile

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology or a related field

Proven experience as a

DevOps Engineer, DevOps Specialist or similar role

Strong understanding of

CI/CD principles

and software delivery lifecycle

Hands-on experience with CI/CD platforms such as

GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD, Azure DevOps, Jenkins or equivalent

Experience with

Infrastructure as Code (IaC)

using Terraform, OpenTofu, Ansible or similar technologies

Strong experience with

Docker

and containerized application environments

Experience with

Kubernetes

and related technologies such as Helm

Experience Working With At Least One Major Cloud Platform

AWS, Azure or GCP

Strong scripting and automation skills using

Python, Bash, PowerShell or similar

Experience with

Git

, branching strategies and version control best practices

Knowledge of

cloud infrastructure, networking and security fundamentals

Experience implementing automation across development, testing and production environments

Familiarity with

monitoring, logging and observability

practices

Knowledge of security best practices and

DevSecOps

principles

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, including the ability to investigate issues in production environments

Ability to collaborate closely with software developers, infrastructure engineers, security teams and other stakeholders

Fluency in English

Nice To Have

Experience with

GitOps

and tools such as Argo CD or Flux

Experience with Kubernetes in production environments

Knowledge of

OpenTelemetry, Prometheus and Grafana

Experience with cloud-native architectures and microservices

Experience with policy-as-code or secrets management solutions

Experience implementing automated security and compliance checks in CI/CD pipelines

Relevant certifications such as

CKA, CKAD, AWS Certified DevOps Engineer or Microsoft Certified: DevOps Engineer Expert

Our Values

Critical thinking and autonomy

Focus on meaningful solutions and real results

Respect, transparency, and continuous growth

What You'll Find At Fyld

A collaborative team that values individual progress

Access to continuous training and certifications

A culture of proximity, respect, and recognition

Challenging, purpose-driven projects where your talent as a

DevOps Engineer

has real impact
