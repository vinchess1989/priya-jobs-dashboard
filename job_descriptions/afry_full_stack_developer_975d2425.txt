Making Future

Job Description

We are seeking a highly skilled Full Stack Developer to work together with AFRY Finland Industry Division Project Delivery Tools team and to further develop, maintain, and support AFRY’s internally developed engineering tools, integrations, and digital solutions. 

Your primary place of work can be any AFRY Finland location. AFRY supports a flexible hybrid work model.

To ensure continuity and further development of AFRY’s engineering tool platform, your key responsibilities will include: 


Collaboration in cross-functional teams including developers, architects, QA, infrastructure, and data/AI specialists
Prepare and manage technical and functional requirements and documentation
Participating in backlog management, prioritization, risk management, and transparent reporting through Azure DevOps
Collaborate with IT and security teams to ensure compliant and secure solution delivery
Promote modern engineering practices including Agile, DevOps, CI/CD, automation, and AI-assisted ways of working (like GitHub Copilot or Claude Code)
Identify opportunities and contribute to improving efficiency, scalability, innovation, and collaboration across the engineering tools landscape



Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should have experience with: 


Proven experience in a broad range of software development modalities in cross-functional and international environments 
Strong understanding of software development lifecycle and Agile delivery methodologies 
Solid technical background enabling effective communication with engineering and IT teams 
Experience with cloud platforms, preferably Microsoft Azure 
Understanding of system integrations, APIs, enterprise applications, and application architecture 
Experience with Azure DevOps/Jira, GitLab, CI/CD, and modern development practices 
Familiarity with AI/LLM-driven development approaches and modern collaboration tools 
Understanding of plant engineering tools, workflows, and digital engineering environments 
Strong communication and problem-solving skills and a consultive mindset 



 We are not looking only for candidates who are limited to a single framework or programming language. We are looking for adaptable engineers with strong fundamentals, a modern technology mindset, and the ability to bridge business and technology in a rapidly evolving engineering landscape.
