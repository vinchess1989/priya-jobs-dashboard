Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

FINNPLAY Technologies Oy is a Finnish software development company specialized in applications for the iGaming industry. We have been over 15 years in the market and are recognised as one of the leading platform technology providers. Our customers include some of the top online casino operators in regulated markets in Europe and elsewhere around the world. Our technology and services provide the technical framework on which iGaming operators build their successful businesses. The industry is dynamic and fast growing, especially as more jurisdictions legalize iGaming and provide a regulatory process for online gambling. Finnplay is also strongly committed to helping players enjoy the iGaming experience safely using technology to provide a safer and more responsible online gambling environment.




Our team consists of close to 150 talented professionals in four offices, with headquarters in Espoo. We offer a flexible working environment and plenty of opportunities for career growth. Our company is also a part of Entain Group PLC - one of the largest businesses in the industry - stock-listed in London and employing over 30,000 people around the world. So our employees get the benefit of being part of a large international network of like-minded professionals. Learn more about Finnplay and our team at http://www.finnplay.com. Here you can learn more about our platform: https://www.finnplay.com/titan-platform.




Now we are looking for a Release Manager to join our team in Espoo, Finland. In this role, you will collaborate with our clients and internal teams to manage the release of Finnplay’s platform to different environments. This role requires a proactive approach to problem solving, and a commitment to continuous improvement. The ideal candidate will thrive in a fast paced environment and be dedicated to ensuring the smooth and timely delivery of software products and services. We work in a hybrid model and hope you can join us at the office twice a week. Salary for this role will be 4000-4500€/month gross.




YOUR JOB IN A NUTSHELL:

Participate in release planning
Execute the release plan
Collaborate with DevOps, QA and Development teams
Configure the system in different environments
Perform preliminary testing
Track the found issues with different teams and clients
Support compliance team for auditing




IN ORDER TO SUCCEED IN THIS ROLE, THESE ARE SOME OF THE MUST-HAVES:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science
2+ years of experience in release management position or similar roles like Developer or DevOps Engineer
Good understanding of CI/CD
Good understanding and experience with web based systems
Good understanding of version control system (Git)
Familiar with Linux
Familiar with Docker or Kubernetes
Strong analytical, problem solving, and communication skills
