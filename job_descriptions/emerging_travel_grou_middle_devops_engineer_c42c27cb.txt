We are looking for two Middle DevOps Engineers to join our team working on a large-scale international IT product. You will be part of engineering team, with opportunities for professional growth, working with a modern technology stack and real high-load infrastructures.

There are two positions with different technical focuses:


DevOps Engineer with a focus on databases;
DevOps Engineer with a focus on CI/CD & Kubernetes


Responsibilities:


Building resilient, fault-tolerant infrastructures
Scaling infrastructure to handle high loads
Setting up build and code delivery processes
Automating and optimizing routine tasks
Implementing new tools and technologies
Supporting and developing database clusters
Managing and troubleshooting production Kubernetes clusters, writing Helm charts
Participating in on-call duties


Requirements


4+ years of commercial experience in DevOps
Experience managing Linux servers and infrastructure using IaC tools (Ansible, Terraform, or equivalents)
GitLab CI: configuring CI/CD pipelines for seamless delivery of Docker containers with Python or Go code
Setting up and maintaining database clusters (such as PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, Aerospike, Consul)
Working with monitoring and logging systems (e.g., Zabbix, Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics, Grafana, ELK, Sentry)
Working with containerization: Docker, docker-compose
Experience managing and troubleshooting production Kubernetes clusters, writing Helm charts
Experience supporting and developing PostgreSQL clusters, understanding of high availability, backups, and replication principles
Conversational English at B1 level or above (Intermediate)


Benefits


Flexible schedules and opportunity to work remotely
Ambitious and supportive team who love what they do, appreciate each other, and grow together
Internal programs for adaptation and training, development of soft skills, and leadership abilities
Partial compensation for participating in external training and conferences
Corporate English school: Group and individual lessons, speaking clubs with colleagues from all over the world
Corporate prices on hotels and travel services
MyTime Day Off - an extra non-working day without loss of compensation
