Job description
Founded in 1910, KONE is a global leader that provides elevators, escalators and automatic building doors, as well as solutions for maintenance and modernization that add value to buildings throughout their life cycle. Our mission is to improve the flow of urban life and make our world's cities better places to live by providing innovative solutions that help make people's journeys safe, convenient and reliable. Our operations in over 60 countries around the world has helped us achieve our position as an innovation and sustainability leader with repeated recognitions by Forbes , Corporate Knights for clean capitalism and others.

KONE's vision is to create the Best People Flow experience by providing ease, effectiveness and experience to our customers and users. In line with our strategy, Sustainable Success with Customers, we will focus on increasing the value we create for customers with new intelligent solutions and embed sustainability even deeper across all of our operations. By closer collaboration with customers and partners, KONE will increase the speed of bringing new services and solutions to the market.

At KONE Technology and Innovation (KTI), we are imagining and creating the future of People Flow, combining the physical and digital worlds to make cities smarter and everyday journeys better.

We are looking for a

Test Specialist, Embedded SW

to join our embedded System Testing- team in Hyvinkää, Finland. The work is performed on-site at KONE's Reliability Laboratories in Hyvinkää, Finland where the teams work every day. Selected candidate is required to live permanently in Finland.

Our teams play a key role in ensuring the quality and reliability of software systems used in KONE solutions. In this role, you will be part of a collaborative testing team working closely with software developers, system experts, and other test professionals to deliver high-quality products.

This position offers an excellent opportunity for a test professional who enjoys hands-on testing, solving technical challenges, and learning new technologies in a real-world environment.

This role focuses primarily on hands-on manual system testing in our on-site laboratory environment in Hyvinkää. The position is not focused on test automation.

What you will do

Execute and develop test plans and test cases in global testing team
Document findings and collaborate with development teams to resolve issues
Participate in test design and continuous improvement of testing practices
Contribute to ensuring that delivered solutions meet quality and reliability expectations

What we are looking for

Experience in software testing, system testing, or a related technical field. Whether you are earlier in your career or already an experienced specialist, we value the right attitude, learning mindset, and ability to contribute to the team
Genuine interest in quality assurance and problem solving
A practical, hands-on approach and willingness to learn
Experience using AI tools to improve productivity, software testing, or engineering workflows
Prompt engineering or practical experience applying generative AI in daily work
Good communication skills in English
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Positive attitude, curiosity, and energy to drive things forward

Additional assets

The following experience is considered a plus:

Embedded software or system-level testing
Linux environments
Python or other scripting/programming skills
Test automation experience
Electrical or electronics control systems
Understanding of elevator, escalator, or people flow solutions
Experience working with real hardware, devices, or integrated systems

What we offer

A challenging and meaningful role in a global technology environment
Opportunities to learn and grow professionally
Collaboration with experienced experts across multiple disciplines
Modern testing facilities and real-world systems to work with
A supportive team culture focused on quality and continuous improvement

For additional information, please contact Chapter Lead & Test Manager, SW System Testing jyri.vesalainen@kone.com
