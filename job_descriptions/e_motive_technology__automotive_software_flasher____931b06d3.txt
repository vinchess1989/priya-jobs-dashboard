Automotive Software Flasher - Muonio, Lapland
Hae paikkaa

We are an exciting automotive testing company, with several premium automakers as our main clients. And now have a new exciting opportunity at our operation in Finnish Lapland!

We are seeking a detail-oriented and technically skilled Automotive Software Flasher to support our vehicle testing and development activities. In this role, you will be responsible for preparing test vehicles by flashing (programming) Electronic Control Units (ECUs) with the required software versions, calibrations, and updates. You will collaborate closely with Automotive analysts, vehicle technicians, and OEM engineering teams to ensure the correct implementation of software prior to, during, and after testing programs.

Qualifications & Skills

Essential:

Vocational or bachelor’s degree in Automotive Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, IT, or a related field.
Strong attention to detail and ability to follow structured procedures.
Good documentation and reporting skills in English.

Desirable:

Experience with vehicle diagnostics and fault tracing.
Some understanding of vehicle networks (CAN, LIN, FlexRay, Ethernet).
Knowledge of cybersecurity and secure flashing procedures.
Hands-on experience with ECU flashing tools (OEM-specific tools) such as Idex, Odis and CP-Tool.
Familiarity with automotive software development life cycle (ASPICE, ISO 26262 awareness).
Previous experience in an automotive testing environment.

What We Offer:

Opportunity to work with leading automotive OEMs on next-generation vehicles.
A dynamic, international working environment with modern test facilities.
Professional growth and technical training opportunities.
Competitive salary.

Our company provides automotive testing operations and services for the automotive and e-mobility industry. 

e-motive Technology was established in 2018, and has carried out testing and evaluation since then. We are based in Oslo, Norway and Muonio, Finland.

Focus areas for the testing station in Finland are cold climate testing and evaluation work on tracks and road for development of powertrain, brakes and stability systems, charging and driving assistance systems (ADAS).

In Norway we focus on city, mountain, infotainment, ADAS, durability, user interface and vehicle quality assurance in a diverse climate.
