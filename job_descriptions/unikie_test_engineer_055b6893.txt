WE ARE LOOKING FOR TEST ENGINEER

We’re looking for a Test Engineer to join Unikie in an on-site customer project in Oulu. You’ll help the team build confidence in its software by developing automated tests, finding the cause of failures, and making test results useful throughout development.

What You’ll Do


Develop and maintain test automation using Python.
Design test cases and improve regression coverage.
Investigate failures, report findings, and work with developers to resolve issues.
Use Linux, Git, and CI/CD workflows as part of everyday testing.


You’ll work on a technically engaging customer project with colleagues who enjoy solving difficult problems together. At Unikie, you’ll have room to develop your expertise in a supportive, low-hierarchy engineering community. The role is based on-site in Oulu.

Required Qualifications


Hands-on experience in software testing and test automation.
Good Python scripting skills.
Confidence working in Linux environments and with Git.
An understanding of how automated tests fit into CI/CD pipelines.
A practical approach to troubleshooting and working with a team.


Experience with Robot Framework, test case refactoring, or manual functional testing is an advantage.
