Junior Software Tester
Hae paikkaa

We are looking for a motivated and creative Junior Software Tester to join our team.

Why join us?

We operate across the Nordic market, primarily in Finland and Sweden, developing and maintaining our own IT products alongside client projects. As a growing company, we continuously strive to improve our software and services.

Our work environment is open and low-hierarchy—we value collaboration, creativity, and a good sense of humor. Most importantly, we appreciate talent and innovative thinking.

About the role

We offer a challenging and rewarding position where you will play an important role in improving and developing our software. Youll be involved in a variety of projects and have the opportunity to bring your own ideas and innovations into practice.

What youll be working with

Designing and executing effective testing strategies

Creating testing methodologies and scenarios

Gaining a deep understanding of existing applications

Contributing to the development of automation practices

Thinking outside the box to improve quality and processes

What we offer

A skilled and supportive team

Opportunities for professional growth and development

An open and low-hierarchy work culture

A flexible and positive work environment

Your profile

We hope you have some familiarity with back-end technologies, but above all, you bring the right mindset and a can-do attitude. You take pride in ensuring systems are secure, reliable, and high-performing.

We will fill the position as soon as we find the right candidate.
