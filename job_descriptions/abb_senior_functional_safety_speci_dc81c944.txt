Your Role And Responsibilities

Are you passionate about functional safety and want to contribute to the development of safe and reliable products with experienced drives site level Functional Safety group and relevant interest groups within Drives Service network?

We are looking for a Functional Safety Specialist to join Motion Service, Business Line Low Voltage Drives in Helsinki, Finland. You will be working in our quality team, and you will ensure that products and systems comply with applicable functional safety standards throughout the product lifecycle. You will work closely with R&D, product management, service, and quality functions to drive product safety, compliance, and continuous improvement.

The ideal candidate has a strong technical understanding of ABB Low Voltage Drives, product development, and product maintenance processes. You have knowledge of service portfolios and component sales practices, combined with a structured approach to quality management systems and regulatory compliance. Strong collaboration skills are essential, as you will be expected to influence stakeholders and drive improvements in a constructive, supportive, and determined manner.

You Will Be Mainly Accountable For


Leading and support functional safety activities across product development projects
Developing, maintaining, and reviewing safety plans, safety concepts, and functional management system
Defining and managing functional safety requirements and ensure traceability
Collaborating with engineering teams to ensure compliance with relevant safety standards
Participating in relevant audits, assessments, and certification activities
Providing functional safety guidance and training and promote functional safety awareness
Supporting continuous improvement of functional safety processes and methodologies


Qualifications For The Role


Work permit for Finland
Understanding of technology and customer applications related to variable frequency converter products
Customer focused and innovative mindset along with excellent team-working and communication skills
Fluent written and spoken English required, Finnish is an advantage
B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Engineering or equivalent
Experience conducting risk assessments and safety analyses
Knowledge of systems engineering principles and product development processes
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills


Working Style and Collaboration


Ability to work independently and collaboratively in multidisciplinary teams
Effective stakeholder management and communication skills
Continuous learning mindset and passion for safety engineering


More About Us

ABB Motion Services helps customers around the world maximize uptime, extend equipment lifetime and improve the performance of their operations. Within Drives Service, our engineering teams sup-port customers throughout the lifecycle of their drive systems, including engineered and customer-specific solutions.

Apply today and help us build and maintain safer products for the future.
