Do you want to help develop product data management and change management processes in one of the world's leading quantum technology companies?

We are looking for a Manufacturing Data Engineer to join our client Bluefors Oy's Manufacturing Engineering team. In this role, you will work at the interface of engineering, production, and product data management, ensuring that product structures, revisions, and technical data are maintained accurately and released to production in a controlled manner.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys working with technical data, systems, and processes while collaborating closely with engineering and manufacturing teams in a growing high-tech environment.

Job Description

As a Manufacturing Data Engineer, you will be responsible for maintaining and managing product data while supporting change management processes across the organization.

Your responsibilities will include:

Creating, maintaining, and managing Bills of Materials (BOMs)

Maintaining product data in ERP and PLM systems

Supporting and coordinating engineering change processes (ECO/ECM)

Managing the release and scheduling of revisions and engineering changes

Ensuring data consistency between ERP and PLM systems

Collaborating closely with Engineering, Manufacturing, and Change Management teams

Supporting product data quality and continuous process improvement

Assisting the Manufacturing Engineering team with product data management related activities

We Expect You To Have

A relevant technical degree

A few years of relevant work experience in a manufacturing or engineering environment

Experience working with ERP and PLM/PDM systems (Experience with Lean ERP is considered an advantage)

Experience managing and maintaining Bills of Materials (BOMs)

Understanding of engineering change management processes (ECO/ECM) and product lifecycle management

Strong English communication skills, as English is the primary working language

A structured, detail-oriented, and proactive way of working

Good collaboration and communication skills

We Offer

An opportunity to work for a global leader in quantum technology

A key role at the intersection of Manufacturing Engineering and Product Data Management

A supportive and highly skilled team environment

A thorough onboarding process

Opportunities to grow your responsibilities and develop professionally

Flexible working hours

Partial remote work possibilities depending on business needs

A modern and international working environment

At Bluefors, you will have the opportunity to contribute to the development of scalable processes in a rapidly growing organization while building expertise in product lifecycle and manufacturing data management.
