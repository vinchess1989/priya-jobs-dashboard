Job Title: MES Business Analyst

Location: Finland

Employment Type: Permanent, Full‑Time

Experience: 5+




Job Summary




We are seeking an experienced MES Business Analyst / Functional Consultant to bridge the gap between manufacturing operations and technology solutions. The ideal candidate will work closely with plant stakeholders, production teams, quality, maintenance, inventory functions, and technical teams to define, design, and implement Manufacturing Execution System (MES) solutions that drive operational excellence and digital transformation across manufacturing facilities.




Key Responsibilities




Understand end-to-end manufacturing operations, including Production, Quality, Maintenance, and Inventory Management processes.
Analyze current-state (AS-IS) and future-state (TO-BE) business processes across shop-floor systems and MES applications.
Conduct workshops and requirement-gathering sessions with plant users and business stakeholders to capture functional, operational, and reporting requirements.
Define process flows, use cases, business rules, and functional specifications for MES implementations and enhancements.
Act as the primary point of contact (SPOC) between manufacturing business stakeholders and technical delivery teams.
Gather, analyze, document, and validate business requirements for MES solutions and manufacturing digitalization initiatives.
Translate shop-floor operations and manufacturing requirements into user stories, functional requirements, and technical specifications.
Collaborate with solution architects, developers, and integration teams to ensure successful MES solution delivery.
Support testing activities including SIT, UAT, defect management, and user training.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives by identifying process optimization opportunities and supporting standardization across manufacturing sites.
Ensure alignment of MES solutions with manufacturing best practices, operational KPIs, and business objectives.




Required Qualifications




Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Information Technology, or related discipline.
5+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, Functional Consultant, or MES Consultant in manufacturing environments.
Strong understanding of manufacturing processes including Production, Quality Management, Maintenance, and Inventory operations.
Experience with MES platforms such as Siemens Opcenter, Rockwell FactoryTalk, AVEVA/Wonderware, Dassault Apriso, SAP ME/MII, Camstar, or similar solutions.
Expertise in business process mapping, requirement gathering, and functional documentation.
Experience creating user stories, process flows, use cases, and business requirement documents (BRDs).
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.




Preferred Qualifications




Knowledge of ISA-95, Industry 4.0, Smart Manufacturing, and Digital Manufacturing concepts.
Experience with ERP-MES integrations, particularly SAP.
Familiarity with Agile and Waterfall project delivery methodologies.
Experience supporting global manufacturing deployments and multi-site standardization initiatives.
