Your Role And Responsibilities

As new industries turn to electrification, it is more vital than ever that we can support ABB’s customers with their new challenges and ensure that our Low Voltage System Drives products meet emerging requirements. That is why we are establishing a new role of Product Lifecycle Manager in our Quality & Lifecycle organization.

In this role, you will ensure that learnings from field performance and reliability testing reach all needed stakeholders for example in product management, R&D and sales. You will also analyze the effects of planned changes or new products on the existing technical offering and customer installed base. The target is to fully leverage accumulated product performance data to support business growth.

This role is contributing to ABB’s Low Voltage System Drives business line in Motion High Power division. You will work in close collaboration with stakeholders from division’s Product Management, Product Engineering, R&D and Service.

This is a global role but location is set to be one of these: Finland (Helsinki), China (Beijing), Germany (Mannheim), USA (New Berlin, WI) or India (Bangalore). The work model for the role is: hybrid,

You Will Be Mainly Accountable For


Initial phase: Developing a new process, related documentation (reports, dashboards) and meeting practices to support efficient product lifecycle decision making and a learning organization
Overseeing the process and ensuring execution of related tasks with stakeholders
Coordinating and conducting change impact analysis of additions, enhancements or simplifications of the existing product portfolio
Facilitating continuous improvements along with the execution of lifecycle changes
Managing global projects, involving stakeholders from various functions and local units


Qualifications For The Role


10+ years of experience on product engineering, product management, portfolio management, R&D or similar area.
5+ years experience in leading people with or without line manager authority
M.Sc. or B.Sc. degree in electrical /electronics engineering, mechanical engineering or equivalent
Experience on process development is a benefit
Experience on frequency converters is an asset
You enjoy taking on new challenges and working with people across different functions, perspectives, and backgrounds to move ideas forward.
Readiness for international travel, estimated 15% of work time


More About Us

ABB High Power division is the market leader in high-power, high-performance drives, drive systems and packages for industrial process and large infrastructure applications, and a leading supplier of power conversion equipment for renewable energy and other applications. The division offers global support to help customers, partners and equipment manufacturers with asset reliability, performance improvement and energy efficiency in mission critical applications.
