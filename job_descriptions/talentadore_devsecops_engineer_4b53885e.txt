📍 Hybrid – Office in Pasila, Helsinki




At TalentAdore, we are building one of Europe's leading human-centred AI companies. Every year, more than 1.5 million people experience recruitment through TalentAdore. AI has been a fundamental part of what we do for more than a decade – long before the rise of LLMs.




We are now looking for a DevSecOps Engineer to become the dedicated internal owner of our infrastructure reliability and technical security. This is not a role where you inherit a fully established function. It is a role where you build one.




🚀 What you will own

Full DevOps ownership – Infrastructure reliability, CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes and system performance, taking over from an external provider and establishing lasting internal ownership
Technical security leadership – Security scanning in pipelines, secrets management, vulnerability management, access control and container security. You are the person who makes sure that what our ISMS says we do, we actually do
Audit-ready technical documentation – Architecture diagrams, data flows, access control documentation and security scan results – maintained continuously, not only at audit time
DevSecOps culture – Embedding security-first thinking into R&D's everyday ways of working, acting as a trusted technical partner rather than a gatekeeper




💰 Fixed salary: 4,600 – 5,400 EUR/month + benefits + opportunity to become a shareholder




🎯 We are looking for someone with

6+ years of hands-on DevOps or SRE experience in a cloud environment
Proven experience embedding security tooling into CI/CD pipelines (SAST, dependency scanning, secrets detection, container security)
Solid understanding of information security principles applied at infrastructure level
ISO 27001 operational experience – audit preparation, ISMS documentation, technical controls
Strong technical documentation skills and the ability to influence without authority
Full fluency in English




We work with GCP, Kubernetes, Kustomize, GitLab, Docker, Prometheus/Grafana and security scanning tools such as Snyk or SonarQube – we are tool-agnostic where the fundamentals are strong.




❤️ Why TalentAdore?

A meaningful mission, an amazing team, hybrid flexibility, a brand-new office in Pasila, comprehensive benefits, and a real opportunity to take ownership of a critical domain from day one.
