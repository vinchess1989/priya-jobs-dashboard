Manual QA Analyst (Shopify) at BairesDev

We are looking for a Manual QA Analyst to ensure e-commerce experiences work flawlessly for shoppers and merchants alike. You will test storefronts and workflows with a sharp eye for detail.

What You'll Do


Plan and execute test scenarios that cover the full customer purchase journey.
Validate storefront features, themes, and checkout flows across browsers and devices.
Report defects clearly and follow them through to resolution.
Run regression cycles before each release to protect existing functionality.
Test integrations with payments, shipping, and third-party apps.
Work with developers and merchants to clarify expectations and improve quality.


What We Are Looking For


4+ years of experience in manual software testing.
Hands-on experience testing online stores and admin workflows on Shopify.
Solid knowledge of test design techniques, exploratory testing, and bug tracking tools.
Strong attention to detail with clear written communication.
Advanced proficiency in English.
