Are you interested in owning the technical integrity of complex customer solutions?




We are developing our System Planning capability and are now looking for a System Architect to join our team in Ulvila, Finland. In this role, you will help ensure that our customer solutions are technically coherent, clearly defined and successfully carried through the entire project lifecycle, from concept and technical approval to delivery, verification, customer acceptance and Service handover.




As System Architect, you will connect expertise from Software, Physical Engineering, Product Management, Service, Simulation, Delivery and other specialist functions into one integrated customer solution. You will also participate in developing system planning processes, the planning and development of tools and work guides.




The role does not replace the technical authority of specialist functions. Sales retains customer and commercial ownership, while Project Management remains responsible for cost, schedule, contract and delivery. Your responsibility is to integrate the total solution and make system-level decisions within the agreed governance model.




What you need to succeed




We are looking for a solution-oriented professional who understands how complex technical systems come together. You may have experience in industrial automation, intralogistics, systems engineering or a comparable technical environment.




We expect you to have:

Several years of experience working with complex technical solutions.
A relevant engineering degree or equivalent practical experience.
Strong system-level thinking and structured analytical skills.
The ability to understand how different technical domains, interfaces and dependencies affect one another.
Confidence in making, documenting and communicating technical decisions.
Experience facilitating discussions across functions and organizational boundaries.
The ability to communicate confidently and professionally with customers.
Professional English communication skills.




Experience in project delivery, technical requirements, risk management, verification, acceptance or lifecycle planning will support your success in this role.




Why join Cimcorp?




This is an opportunity to help shape a new capability within Cimcorp and influence how complex customer solutions are defined, approved and delivered.




You will work with experienced specialists across the organization and have a visible impact on:

Solution quality and technical integrity.
Delivery readiness and customer acceptance.
Clearer technical decision-making.
Cross-functional collaboration.
Learning from complex projects and improving our ways of working.




The first appointments will support selected complex projects, and the role model will develop further based on practical experience.




Location and working model




The role is based at Cimcorp’s headquarters in Ulvila, Satakunta, Finland. We offer a hybrid working model.




You will report to Mikko Kumpulainen, System Planning Development Manager.




Interested?




For more information about the role, please contact Mikko Kumpulainen or Jarno Honkanen, VP, Solutions and Technology (firstname.lastname@cimcorp.com)




Please submit your application through our recruitment system as soon as possible, but no later than 30 November 2026. The position will be filled once the right candidate is found.
