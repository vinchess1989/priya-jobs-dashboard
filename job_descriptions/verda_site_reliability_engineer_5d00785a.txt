Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
At Verda, we're building a full-stack AI cloud, covering everything from data centers and hardware to our own cloud platform that the world's leading AI teams use to do serious AI work.

About The Role

As a Site Reliability Engineer you keep Verda's GPU cloud running and make it more reliable every quarter. This role is for engineers with a few years of production experience. You start with well-scoped work alongside senior SREs and grow into owning parts of the platform from build to production.

You work hands-on across the systems that keep our data centres serving customers: deployments, Kubernetes platforms, secrets, observability, and the tooling that connects our bare-metal GPU servers to the services our customers use.

At Verda, reliability is part of the product. You measure it in SLOs, incidents, pages and customer credits, and you write the code and automation that bring those numbers down. You work closely with our automation, networking, Linux, hardware and data-centre operations teams. Once you're ready, you join our paid primary/secondary on-call rotation, paired with experienced engineers while you learn.

Your Responsibilities


Run and improve the services that keep our data centres operating, from deployment pipelines to secret provisioning
Contribute to SLIs and SLOs for our core platform services
Take part in on-call: respond to incidents, lead smaller ones as you gain experience, contribute to blameless reviews and follow through on the fixes you own
Diagnose failures methodically across the stack, from GPU and server hardware through Linux and networking to Kubernetes and the services on top
Build automation that removes repetitive operational work, including AI-assisted and agent-based tooling where it helps
Make deployments safer with release criteria, readiness checks, progressive rollouts and fast rollback
Build monitoring into the work you ship and extend our observability (metrics, logs, traces and alerts), so we find problems before customers report them
Contribute to capacity planning, failover and redundancy design for a fast-growing GPU fleet
Raise reliability risks early with clear options, and log them in the infrastructure risk register
Write runbooks and documentation, and help your peers through code reviews


Your key competencies

The ideal candidate has a solid Linux and systems foundation, writes real code, and is curious to learn the rest of the stack. You don't need to tick every box to apply.


3+ years in SRE, infrastructure, platform or systems engineering, or equivalent hands-on experience from internships
Solid Linux fundamentals and hands-on troubleshooting experience
Hands-on Kubernetes experience, ideally in production
Some experience with infrastructure automation or GitOps (for example Ansible, ArgoCD, Terraform, CI/CD) and observability tools (for example Prometheus, Grafana, VictoriaMetrics, OpenTelemetry)
Experience writing tooling and automation in Go or Python
Solid networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, DNS and load balancing
You debug methodically in Linux, networking or distributed systems, and you're curious to learn the rest of the stack
Ownership and a data-led approach: you follow an incident through to the lasting fix, and you can show the effect of your work
Good judgement about when to automate, when to fix by hand and when to escalate
Clear communication and a quick learner: you share progress, ask for help early, act on feedback and work well across teams


Nice to have


GPU infrastructure: NVIDIA drivers, DCGM, InfiniBand or RoCE, and common GPU failure modes
Bare-metal provisioning and hardware lifecycle: BMC/IPMI, Redfish and firmware management
Linux performance analysis, or driver and kernel-level troubleshooting
Tools from our stack: Rancher, kubeadm, Cilium, containerd, Harbor, Kyverno
Building AI or agent-based tools for operations
Experience at a cloud provider or running a large data-centre fleet
Previous on-call or incident response experience for production systems
