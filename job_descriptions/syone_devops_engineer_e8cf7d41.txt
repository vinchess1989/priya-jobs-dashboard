DevOps Engineer (Hybrid – Porto or Lisbon)

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to join our team.

What you'll do:

Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines to support automated software delivery;

Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, and deployment processes;

Manage and optimize cloud infrastructure and containerized environments;

Implement Infrastructure as Code practices using tools such as Terraform;

Monitor system performance, availability, and reliability, proactively identifying and resolving issues;

Collaborate closely with Development, Infrastructure, and Operations teams to improve deployment processes and system reliability;

Implement and maintain automation and configuration management solutions;

Contribute to the continuous improvement of DevOps practices, processes, and tools;

Ensure security, scalability, and high availability across environments.

What you will need to bring:

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field;

Professional experience as a DevOps Engineer or in a similar role;

Solid experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Azure DevOps;

Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes;

Experience with Infrastructure as Code, particularly Terraform;

Knowledge of at least one major Cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP);

Scripting/programming knowledge, preferably Python, Bash, or PowerShell;

Experience with monitoring and logging tools is a plus;

Good understanding of Git and version control practices;

Autonomous, proactive, and solution-oriented mindset;

Strong communication and teamwork skills;

Fluent English (mandatory).

What can Syone offer:

Integration in an organization with profound and sustained growth and involvement in pioneering projects with innovative technological solutions;

Strong IT training plans;

Professional evolution with intervention in ambitious technological projects, both national and internationally.
