Sumitomo SHI FW is looking for a




Product Coordinator/Manager




The Product Manager or Coordinator in the Digital Innovation Hub supports the lifecycle management of products and services, with a primary focus on digital products while also contributing to broader product and service business development initiatives. Coordinates collaboration and activities across business, technology, and external partners to support successful product delivery, operation, and continuous improvement. Supports product management activities throughout the product lifecycle to maximize business value.




Your main responsibilities will be:

Support the lifecycle management of products and services, including delivery, operation, and continuous improvement activities.
Coordinate collaboration across business, technology, and external stakeholders, including facilitating meetings, workshops, and reviews.
Act as a driving force in product-related initiatives by proactively coordinating stakeholders, identifying dependencies and issues, and ensuring agreed actions are progressed to completion.
Support product portfolio, roadmap, and governance processes, including related documentation, communications, and reporting.
Facilitate the collection, documentation, and communication of business needs and requirements.
Monitor and report product-related performance metrics and status information.
Support the adoption of product management practices, tools, and ways of working.
Contribute to the continuous development of digital products, services, and related processes.
Lead and coordinate nominated NZEE projects, ensuring project objectives, schedules, budgets, and deliverables are achieved.




You will bring with you:

Studies in Industrial Engineering and Management or a related field, with an understanding of business processes, digital solutions, and product management.
Understanding of product portfolio, roadmap, and governance processes.
Experience in coordinating activities across business and technology stakeholders.
Knowledge of product lifecycle management and agile ways of working.
Ability to gather, analyze, and document business requirements.
Strong communication, collaboration, and stakeholder management skills.
Experience with modern product management, collaboration, and digital service management tools and practices.




For more information, please contact: Sampo Pihkala, Manager, Digital Innovation Hub, sampo.pihkala@shi-g.com




About Us




Powering a decarbonized world for everyone

Sumitomo SHI FW (SFW) has been providing innovative and environmentally sound energy solutions to our customers for over 100 years. Our mission is to enable the energy transition to a carbon-free future through decarbonization, decentralization and digitalization.

Our solutions expand from the world-leading circulating fluidized bed (CFB) technology to long-duration energy storage, waste-to-energy, waste-to-value, carbon capture and services for the global power and industrial markets. Through co-creation, partnerships, and ecosystem collaboration SFW aim to accelerate the speed of decarbonization and create more value to our customers.

We are global energy experts with more than 1800 talented staff, across multiple locations, covering the globe from North America through Europe to Asia.

For more information, please visit: https://www.shi-fw.com/
