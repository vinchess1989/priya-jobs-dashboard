Senior Test Engineer (AI-Native Product Team) | Contract




We're supporting a fast-growing technology team that is scaling ahead of a major product launch and looking for an experienced Senior Test Engineer to help ensure quality across a modern AI-native software platform.




The team is looking for someone who combines strong test automation expertise with modern AI-assisted testing practices. You'll play a key role in shaping test strategy, identifying risk, improving quality processes, and helping the engineering team release quickly and confidently.




What You'll Be Doing

Designing and executing testing strategies for modern cloud-native applications
Building and maintaining automated test frameworks
Testing APIs, backend services and integrations
Performing risk-based test analysis and prioritisation
Working closely with engineers to improve quality throughout the development lifecycle
Leveraging AI tools to improve testing efficiency and coverage
Contributing to quality standards across a fast-moving product team




What We're Looking For

Several years of commercial software testing experience
Strong background in test automation
Experience testing APIs and distributed systems
JavaScript or TypeScript experience
Excellent understanding of test design techniques and risk-based testing
Ability to work independently and take ownership of quality outcomes
Strong communication skills in English




Highly Desirable

Hands-on use of AI tools within testing workflows
Experience testing AI-powered products or machine learning systems
Experience creating AI-assisted test cases, automation or validation processes
Cloud-native application testing
Performance, integration or end-to-end testing experience




Technical Environment

TypeScript / JavaScript
Modern API-driven architectures
Cloud-native applications
Azure-based environment
Automated CI/CD pipelines
AI-assisted development and testing practices




The team operates with a startup mindset, moving quickly and placing a strong emphasis on ownership and accountability. Engineers and testers are trusted to make decisions, solve problems independently and continuously improve the way software is delivered.




This is a highly collaborative environment where quality is viewed as a shared responsibility, not something that happens at the end of the development cycle.




Contract Details

Fully remote
Initial 6-month contract
Strong extension potential
Full-time engagement (approximately 160 hours per month)
Preference for consultants based in the Nordics, although other European locations may be considered
English-speaking international team




If you're a senior automation-focused test professional who is already exploring how AI is reshaping software testing and quality engineering, we'd be keen to discuss the opportunity further.
