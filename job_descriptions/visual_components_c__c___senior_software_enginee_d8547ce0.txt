Get to know Visual Components




At Visual Components, we build simulation technology that powers the future of manufacturing. More than 2,400 professionals worldwide, from small factories to major global manufacturers, use our software to design, visualize, and optimize tomorrow’s production lines.

With over 20 nationalities represented in our Espoo HQ and offices in the US, Germany, and Japan, we’re truly international. We grow together, we enjoy our work, and we inspire one another. Join us and experience the difference of working with a team that’s passionate about building high‑quality engineering solutions.




The role and the team




We are looking for a Software Engineer to expand the Product Infra team. You will contribute to the architecture of our products and improve the infrastructure around it. You will ensure that our product customization, dependency management, deployment pipelines, and test infrastructures are streamlined and modernized. Your focus will be on creating reliable, scalable solutions that support a growing number of customers and use cases.

The Product Infra team collaborates closely with DevOps and Quality Assurance teams while focusing on product-specific needs. This includes improving our products to support a growing number of platform customers.

We work in a hybrid model and expect team members to be onsite a few days a week to support collaboration, onboarding, and team cohesion.




Your impact

As a software engineer on the Product Infra team, you will:

Work closely with Product Owner and your Engineering Lead towards a common goal
Streamline and modernize our codebase and the infrastructure around it
Report and take ownership of issues and bring them to a successful conclusion




Key Responsibilities

Contribute to improvements in:
Product customization and variation for our desktop products
Product deployment - improve product distribution, software update mechanism
Desktop application build system - improve build speed, modularity and reliability together with DevOps
Dependency management - maintain up‑to‑date and well‑organized dependencies, being aware of CVEs and best practices
Test infrastructure – work with QA team to improve our custom test infrastructure for the 3D simulation platforms
Code base modernization - identify and deliver code modernization, tools and methodologies
Improve performance, stability, and overall system quality
Champion continuous improvement in development process, tooling, and product quality




TECH STACK

Languages: C++, C++/CLI, C#, WPF, Python
Build & Deployment: PowerShell scripting, AzureDevOps Pipelines, nuget and vcpkg, Advanced Installer
Tools: Visual Studio, GIT, Azure DevOps, GitHub Copilot




The skills you will bring

Hands-on experience with C# and/or C++; Python is a bonus
Deep understanding of algorithms, data structures, design patterns, and software architecture
Experience working with a Microsoft Technology Stack such as Visual Studio, Azure, Azure DevOps, etc
Strong understanding of build and dependency management
Experience with Git, CI/CD pipelines, and modern development workflows
Hands-on experience with Desktop deployment solutions
Experience on modernizing legacy monolithic software projects
Excellent communication and people skills
Familiarity with Agile and Scrum processes and the ability to effectively navigate and work in an agile environment.
Experience working with AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot) to improve coding efficiency, code quality, and developer productivity.
Experience with robotics, simulation systems, or data analysis is highly beneficial
Ability to work independently, make technical decisions, and collaborate effectively across teams
Proficient English communication skills (spoken and written)
