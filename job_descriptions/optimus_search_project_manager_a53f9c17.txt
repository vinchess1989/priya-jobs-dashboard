Senior eQMS Implementation Project Manager – Pharmaceutical

Location: Kuopio, Finland (Hybrid)

Contract: Freelance | 6–9 Months

Start Date: November 2026 / Flexible

Sector: Pharmaceutical / Life Sciences




The Opportunity

We are currently supporting a pharmaceutical manufacturing company in Finland with an upcoming Quality Management System (QMS) transformation project.

We are looking for an experienced Senior eQMS Implementation Project Manager to lead the implementation of a new or upgraded electronic Quality Management System, working directly on behalf of the pharmaceutical manufacturer.




This is a client-side position requiring someone who has successfully managed pharmaceutical QMS implementation projects from start to finish.




Key Responsibilities

Lead the implementation and upgrade of an electronic Quality Management System (eQMS).
Manage project delivery, timelines, milestones and stakeholder coordination.
Act as the client-side Project Manager, overseeing external software vendors and implementation partners.
Coordinate internal Quality Assurance, IT and business teams.
Support the integration and improvement of pharmaceutical quality processes, including CAPA, deviations, change control and document management.
Ensure the implementation aligns with GMP requirements and business objectives.
Identify project risks and ensure successful system adoption.




Requirements

Proven experience managing eQMS implementation projects within pharmaceutical or life sciences environments.
Experience with Veeva Vault QMS, MasterControl, TrackWise or equivalent platforms.
Strong understanding of GMP and pharmaceutical quality management processes.
Previous experience working client-side on QMS implementation projects.
Excellent project management and stakeholder coordination skills.
Experience managing external software vendors and implementation partners.
Ability to work independently and lead complex implementation projects.




Contract Details

Hybrid working arrangement, with approximately 1–2 days onsite per week in Kuopio (potentially more during initial implementation).







Interested?

If you have previously led an electronic Quality Management System implementation within a pharmaceutical environment, I would be interested in hearing from you.




Please apply with your latest CV or get in touch directly to discuss the opportunity.
