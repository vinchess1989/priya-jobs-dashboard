GROUP IT & BPI · CITYCON FINLAND · ONSITE
Systems Specialist
Citycon is seeking a proactive Systems Specialist to support and develop business-critical systems, collaborate with stakeholders, and drive operational excellence across the Nordics.
Apply now!

Citycon is the leading owner, developer and manager of urban hubs in the Nordics. Citycon transforms unique locations into sustainable communities and cities full of life, serving 125 million people each year and delivering long-term share value. Citycon aims for carbon neutrality by 2030. Citycon owns 28 centres: 9 in Finland, 1 in Estonia, 10 in Norway, 6 in Sweden and 2 in Denmark. Citycon has been listed on Nasdaq Helsinki (CTY1S) since 1988 and is a member of EPRA (European Public Real Estate Association). 

Citycon is looking for proactive Systems Specialist to join our Group IT & BPI team in a permanent position. In this role, you will be you will be responsible for the administration, configuration, support, and continuous improvement of our commercial and data platforms. You will work closely with business stakeholders such as leasing, asset management, marketing, and finance across all our business units to ensure reliable, scalable, and user-friendly solutions that support our business objectives.

Employment Type: Permanent

Start of the Employment: Starting flexibly in November or December 2026

Location: On-site at Citycon’s HQ in Iso Omena, Espoo

As a Systems Specialist, you will be responsible for

Acting as the technical Super User and main point of contact for appointed business application platforms such as Salesforce and Power BI. Agreed group wide service responsibilities may change from time to time.

Providing user training, support and feedback as well as advising business and support functions on technology and how it can help them perform better in the agreed area of responsibility.

Administration, configuration, and optimization of CMR solutions and related dependencies, such as Salesforce Sales Cloud and Experience Cloud environments.

Developing and supporting reporting and analytics capabilities using business intelligence and data pipeline/integration solutions such as Power BI, Snowflake, Matillion and Boomi.

Managing user access, security roles, workflows, reports, dashboards, and system configurations in accordance with established controls and policies.

Gathering business- and end-user requirements and translating them into functional solutions and system/process enhancements.

Participating in systems implementation and development projects as a key technical specialist and supporting testing, and release- and change management activities.

Creating and maintaining system-, process- and user guide documentation.

What are we looking for

Several years of relevant experience working with business applications, data platforms, analytics solutions, or related technologies

Hands-on experience administering and configuring Salesforce Sales Cloud and/or Experience Cloud solutions.

Experience developing reports, dashboards, and analytics solutions using Power BI, or similar technologies.

Experience in working with business stakeholders to capture requirements and deliver practical solutions.

Good understanding of system integrations, data flows, APIs, and data management principles.

Proven track record in supporting business-critical applications in a multi-system environment.

A proactive, service-minded, and quality-focused approach with a curiosity for technology and continuous improvement.

A bachelor’s or master’s degree in Business, Information Systems, or a related field.

Excellent communication and presentation skills in English.

What we offer

A competitive salary enhanced with a lunch benefit provided on top of your base pay, a sports and cultural benefit, comprehensive occupational healthcare, and extensive insurance coverage that also protects you during your leisure time.

A supportive and encouraging work community, where everyone’s contribution matters.

An environment where you can learn, make an impact and work in a role with a broad perspective on the real estate industry.

Shared activities, ranging from small sports moments to larger gatherings, that bring our people together.

Please submit your application by Sunday 25th October 2026. We encourage you to apply as soon as possible, since we are going through the applications already during the application period and will proceed as soon as we find a suitable candidate for the Group IT & BPI team.

For more information about the role and the team, please contact Robert Gustafsson, Senior Systems Manager, at +358 20 766 4486 or robert.gustafsson@citycon.com
