Job description
About the job

We are looking for software test engineers for our team working with protocol stacks for wireless IoT, especially with Cellular IoT using radio protocols defined by 3GPP. As a test engineer, you are a critical part of the software development team, verifying the asset and bringing your insight into the design process. In this position, you will be working as part of our experienced and motivated development team with software protocol stacks designed to enable wireless connectivity for very low-power IoT applications. Based on your skills and experience, your role may include anything from test design for large entities, development of in-house test cases and test automation system, to ensuring our SW quality with certified test systems. Seniority and level of responsibility of the role will be defined based on your previous experience.

Key responsibilities
Verification planning as part of software development
Design, development and maintenance of test cases and environments
Planning and maintenance of continuous integration and automation
Analysis of test results, debugging and problem solving
Collaboration within the team, with other Nordic teams, and with test equipment vendors
Key Qualifications
M.Sc or B.Sc in Telecommunications, ICT, or equivalent education
Knowledge of software verification processes
Experience in continuous integration and test automation
Good Python programming skills, C skills are beneficial
Experience of wireless communication protocols is an advantage, e.g. 3GPP LTE and NR, eRedCap, or IEEE 802.15.4 based systems.
Knowledge of internet protocols is beneficial, i.e. TCP/IP, TLS and socket interface for applications
Comfortable in using modern AI-assisted development tools in day-to-day work
Personal Skills

The ideal candidate is curious, motivated and persistent when hunting for a reason for badly behaving device. You should enjoy delivering high-quality solutions for anything you are working on. You communicate clearly in English, both in writing and in person, and you collaborate comfortably across disciplines. We succeed as a team so you should enjoy working with other people towards a common goal.

Working for Nordic

Working at Nordic, you will be inspired and supported to develop yourself. Our teams enjoy a professional and informal working environment. We value and encourage the continuous development of skills and expertise to the highest levels. We are proud of our Norwegian heritage, our highly skilled international workforce, and our world-leading innovation.

We offer a variety of tasks and projects, and the possibility to work alongside some of the world's most renowned experts within their field. We encourage our employees to question the established and innovate while expecting professionalism, commitment, and the will to learn.

Benefits
Competitive salary with short- and long-term incentive plan
Flexible working hours
Medical insurance
Family-friendly policies, insurances, and benefits
