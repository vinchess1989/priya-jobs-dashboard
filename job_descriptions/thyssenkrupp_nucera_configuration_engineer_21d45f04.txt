Your responsibilities

Aim of the position

In this role you will be a vital part in developing our thyssenkrupp nucera global organization & business. You will be able to help us define new ways of conducting our business while also improving our current processes & operations. You will be at the center of laying the foundation for the coming decades of our scale up and growth.

Main Responsibilities And Contents


Configure serialized and customized systems in Teamcenter
Ensure compliance with defined product variants and options while integrating Engineer-to-Order (ETO) requirements
Develop, maintain, and govern configurator rules and frameworks for bid and order configuration
Define and assess the impact of ETO requirements and prepare project-specific configuration structures for efficient implementation
Maintain and enrich the product and variant catalogue, including the implementation of new variants from development or change initiatives
Analyze the variant portfolio to ensure economic efficiency and eliminate underutilized variants
Manage product changes across the full product lifecycle in accordance with the NCA Product Change Management process (acting as Auditor / Change Specialist 2)
Perform change impact and cost assessments, create Change Requests / Orders in Teamcenter, and align with Product Cost Estimation
Prepare and moderate Change Control Board (CCB) meetings
Provide advisory support to projects, sales, and operations during ETO execution



Your profile

Skills, Qualifications And Abilities


Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering or a comparable technical qualification
Willingness to understand and live product configuration, variant management, and change management
Knowledge of PLM systems, ideally Teamcenter is a plus
Understanding of serialized systems and Engineer-to-Order processes
Experience managing product changes throughout the product lifecycle, including cost impact evaluation
Structured and analytical working style with the ability to assess complex technical interdependencies
Strong communication and moderation skills in interdisciplinary environments
Fluent in English; German language skills are an advantage



Your benefits

international work environment in interdisciplinary and multinational teams;
flat hierarchies and short decision-making routes;
flexible working time (flexible working hours at office and smartworking);
welfare plan;
agreements with special rates for local transportation means and fitness studio membership;

availability of public transportation infrastructure: Metrò green line M2, stop Crescenzago, as well as speedway tangenziale est, exit Cascina Gobba.



Company

thyssenkrupp nucera Italy is the Italian branch located in Milano, specialized on design and supply of electrochemical plants for the production of green hydrogen as well as chlorine and caustic soda. With more than 20 years experiences the company is offering new-build and service solutions in many regions of the world.
