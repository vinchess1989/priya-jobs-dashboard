The role is about:

As a DevOps Engineer at payabl., you will play a key role in building and scaling our infrastructure while collaborating across multiple product teams. Your primary focus will be supporting the Data engineering team, ensuring robust, automated, and secure delivery of data-driven solutions. At the same time, you'll work closely with Banking, Payments, and payabl.one teams to enable faster, more reliable releases and provide the foundations for innovation across our global payments platform. This role is highly cross-functional, giving you the opportunity to influence how teams build, deploy, and operate products at scale.

Location: Portugal, Poland fully remote. Cyprus Hybrid in Limassol Office.

What you will do:


Building and maintaining our Kubernetes clusters, (not just creating tools and also deploying apps with Helm), but the emphasis is on being able to do this in a code-first way: infrastructure as code
Work closely with other teams to identify pain points and problems around the platform
Develop new tools and frameworks to improve data platforms
Manage CI/CD. Design, develop, and implement CI/CD pipelines using tools like Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab
Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration management using tools like Ansible, Terraform, or AWS CloudFormation
Monitor and troubleshoot infrastructure issues using tools like Prometheus, Grafana
Implement security best practices and ensure compliance with internal and external regulations
Participate in code reviews and help teammates with constructive feedback


What we need:


At least 5 years of experience with containerization technology (Kubernetes, Docker) and cloud platforms
Proficiency in provisioning infrastructure and managing configurations with tools such as Ansible, Terraform, or AWS CloudFormation
Strong knowledge of Data lake services on AWS (Glue, Athena)
Linux experience: troubleshooting, Bash/Python/Perl scripting, familiarity with multiple distributions (Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL)
Experience developing automation for CI/CD pipelines, especially Jenkins
Good to have Kafka knowledge
Desire to learn and participate in coaching junior members of the team
Excellent communication and presentation skills
Outstanding attention to details
Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
A customer-centric, empathetic mindset with excellent listening skills
An ability to focus on what counts, creating simple solutions to deliver fast results

Hiring Process:


Step 1 - Thinking in Action (40 minutes) Your first conversation will be with our Talent Acquisition team. We'll explore your background, career journey, motivations, and overall fit for the role. As part of this discussion, you'll also complete a short technical screening that will be reviewed by our engineering team. This stage helps us understand both your experience and how you approach technical challenges
Step 2 - Build in Production (60-90 minutes) Meet with the Team Lead and technical experts for a practical assessment. Depending on the role, this may involve a live coding exercise or a real-world scenario designed to evaluate your technical skills, problem-solving approach, and ability to work through challenges similar to those faced by our teams
Step 3 - Final Interview (45 minutes) The final stage is a group interview with senior members of our Technology squad, which may include the CTO, CPO, and Team Lead. Together, we'll discuss team fit, collaboration style, expectations from both sides, and any remaining questions about the role, team, or technology domain. This is also an opportunity for you to learn more about our culture and ways of working
