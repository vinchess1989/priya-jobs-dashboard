Senior DevOps Engineer - Application Management (permanent position)

Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

Would you like to be our expert in operating and monitoring our data pipelines for Default Management Engine in High Risk Service Data Domain? We are now looking for DevOps engineers to operate, monitor and automate our data pipelines/applications.

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We're not just a bank, we're a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About our team

Meet Default Management team in SD High Risk and SD C & I. Our role is to support the lending business by providing data solutions for ongoing loan servicing and monitoring.

Main responsibilities in this role:

Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines to automate software deployment and release processes

Automate monitoring and reconciliation

Release deployment, verification and follow-up and Incident investigations and management

Application provider, governance and compliance

Operate and monitor system performance and troubleshoot issues to ensure high availability and reliability 

Work closely with development teams to integrate DataOps/ BizDevOps best practices into the software development lifecycle and to enhance collaboration, automation, and efficiency across the organization

Work closely with development teams to help support our Nordea BizDevOps ambitions with architects and hardware/infrastructure engineers within Nordea

Who you are

To succeed in this role, we believe that you have:

Expert knowledge Dev Ops ways of working

Expertise in operating and automating E2E data pipelines

Strong communication skills to teach and convey messages in a professional manner

Skills in coaching team members in the areas of performance, standards and work methods

Ability to resolve conflicts (mediation skills)

Ability to manage challenging conversations e.g. performance assessment

Your background and skills include: 

Multiple years’ experience within application management/operations/monitoring

5+ years’ experience in DW, BI & analytics within engineering, test, development or architecture

Some team/technical leader experience preferred

ELT/ETL development using GUI-based tool e.g. IBM Datastage, ADF, Stitch, Matillion

Data / Software Engineering skills (Java/Scala/Python, SQL)

Continuous Integration and Deployment experience, utilizing tools like Jenkins, Ansible, GIT/BitbucketExperience with monitoring tools such as Splunk, Grafana or similar
