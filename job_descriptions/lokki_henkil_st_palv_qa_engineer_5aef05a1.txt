QA Engineer
Hae paikkaa

For our client in the banking sector, we are looking for a QA Engineer to join a project focused on modernizing the user interface of the clients mobile banking application and web platform.

Note! This application is only for individuals already residing in Finland, and relocation is not possible.

Assignment:
The project aims to give the existing netbank application and website a modernized look and feel. The work will initially focus on updating UI components across Android, iOS, and web platforms.

As the existing components will remain in production during the uplift, testing will play an important role in ensuring both the quality of the new functionality and the stability of existing features. As a QA Engineer, you will be responsible for testing new and updated UI components, performing regression testing, and ensuring that existing functionality continues to work as expected throughout the modernization.

Required Skills:
- Minimum 3 years of professional frontend testing/QA experience
- Experience with mobile application testing
- Experience with web application testing
- Experience with non-functional testing, including accessibility testing
- Good understanding of relevant testing methodologies, standards, technologies, and tools
- Ability to work independently and take ownership of assigned testing activities
- Proactive approach to identifying, communicating, and resolving potential issues Preferred

Skills:
- UI component testing
- Regression testing
- Test automation
- Previous experience testing IT solutions within the financial or banking sector

Answer these questions separately in a few sentences and attach them to your application! (Mandatory!)
- Do you have at least 3 years of professional experience in software testing/QA?*
- Do you have hands-on experience with both mobile application testing and web application testing?*
- Do you have experience with non-functional testing, including accessibility testing?*
- Do you have experience with regression testing and/or UI component testing?*
- Do you have previous experience testing IT solutions within the banking or financial sector?

Please apply with your updated CV, that clearly demonstrates your skills and experience relevant to this role.
