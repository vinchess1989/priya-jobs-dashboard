Quality Process Specialist, Fab processes

Are you interested in working as a Quality Process Specialist for semiconductor wafer processing in the field of microelectronics and quantum technologies with the possibility to make an impact? VTT might be just the right place for you! 

VTT is one of the leading applied research organisations in Europe. What unites us are curiosity, a passion for learning, and a purpose: to solve the biggest challenges of our time and turn them into sustainable growth and well-being. Our cross-disciplinary community of over 2000 brilliant minds from 55+ different nationalities focuses on creating impact through science-based innovation. In recent years, we have created ground-breaking innovations such as the world's fastest and most accurate radiation detector, a cancer-sniffing sensor device for smartphones, and a headphone set entirely made of microbial materials. Get to know us further by exploring VTT's tech & infra. 

 We are now looking for a Quality Process Specialist to join our Micronova FAB organization in Espoo! 

About Fab:  

The semiconductor wafer fab located in Micronova facilitates the whole chain of research, development and small-volume production. The total area of the cleanroom is 2 600 m2, and there is an installed equipment base of 200 processing and measurement tools, enabling fabrication of devices for silicon photonics, radio, MEMS, quantum and hyperspectral applications. Micronova is a vibrant ecosystem, where more than twenty companies have access to the fab for the fabrication of  

their own products, and Aalto University operates independently in part of the facility. The fab operates at 200 mm wafer size and its operations are ISO9001 certified. In addition to the existing toolbase, numerous investments into new equipment are currently in progress to enhance the capabilities. New business models, growing demands and VTT’s involvement in EU Chips JU Pilot Line activities drive a change in operational model. The fab is in a transformation process towards more repeated process offering and advanced process control and data management. 

As a Quality Process Specialist, you will: 
Own the quality processes and governance in the Micronova fab 
Maintaining and continuously developing the Quality Handbook for cleanroom operations, including document and change management. 
Define environmental targets, monitor key performance indicators, and prepare the annual environmental report 
Own the training set for quality system 
Facilitate and support quality related audits to the fab 
Audit material vendors  
Leading the quality and environmental training for VTT personnel using the cleanroom 
Facilitate continuous improvement activities 
Own, report and develop quality and environmental data, metrics and KPIs 

You will work closely within the fab core operations unit, which consists of highly qualified Maintenance and Process Engineering and data experts, cleanroom operators, and manufacturing process quality experts. Further key stakeholders include research teams and external companies that utilize the Micronova cleanrooms. 

Who we are looking for:   
You have a relevant university or university of applied sciences degree in a technical, technological, scientific, or other related field 
Good knowledge and experience of quality standards 
Background and work experience in semiconductor processing 
Knowledge and experience in problem solving methods 
Team working skills, good communication capabilities 
Hands-on attitude and positive drive to implement new processes 
Fluent spoken and writing in English is mandatory, fluent Finnish is seen as an asset  
As a person, we hope that you are: 

A person with good problem-solving and communication skills. We hope that you have a hands-on mentality, good collaboration skills, and are able to create strong relationships with all cleanroom users. You are proactive, methodical, and willing to take responsibility.
