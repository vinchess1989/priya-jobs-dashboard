Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

At ALTEN Finland, we are looking for a Test Automation Engineer to strengthen our team. This is a fully remote position, but candidates must be located in Finland.

In this role, you will develop and maintain automated testing solutions, enhance existing automation capabilities, perform software verification and validation activities, evaluate test outcomes, and collaborate closely with development teams. You will also be involved in test planning, test design, defect resolution, reporting, and the ongoing enhancement of testing practices.




We expect you to have:

Hands-on experience in test automation and software testing
Experience with technologies such as Python, Robot Framework, Ansible, Bash or similar tools
Experience with software testing methodologies and quality assurance practices
Familiarity with CI/CD environments and tools such as Jira and Confluence
Experience working in agile development environments
Strong problem-solving skills and a structured way of working
Ability to work independently and proactively in an international environment
Strong communication skills in English
A quality-focused mindset and enthusiasm for automation and continuous improvement




We offer:

ALTEN Finland provides a clear and encouraging work environment where you can develop your skills in software development and test automation. The team atmosphere is friendly, and support is available whenever you need it.

You will also enjoy benefits such as lunch and sports/culture vouchers, leisure-time accident insurance, and the option for a company phone and bicycle to support your everyday well-being.

How to apply

Send your CV and application by 23.10.2026, and tell us about your skills and interest in software development and test automation. We review applications during the application period and the position can be filled immediately upon finding a suitable candidate.
