pawaTech is looking for a Senior Infrastructure DevOps Engineer to join our Infrastructure team. In this role, you will help build and scale modern infrastructure across on-premise and cloud environments, improve Infrastructure as Code practices, automate operational processes, and contribute to the development of internal platform services.




You will join a highly technical team working on long-term infrastructure initiatives, including datacenter, development, Kubernetes platforms, infrastructure automation, and large-scale Terraform improvements.




Compensation range: €5800 - €6300 monthly gross




About us:

pawaTech is a leading iGaming solutions provider, delivering sportsbook, casino, lottery, and player management software as a service. We power betPawa - Africa’s largest betting brand, operating across multiple markets.'




Our platform is designed for scale, handling millions of active users across web and mobile, processing thousands of transactions every second. With nearly a decade of industry expertise, we don’t just build software - we craft tailored solutions that drive innovation. Our expertise, commitment to quality, and client-focused approach make us a trusted partner in Africa and beyond.




What you’ll be doing:

Building and maintaining scalable infrastructure across on-premises and cloud environments;
Improving Infrastructure as Code practices;
Automating infrastructure provisioning, deployments and operational workflows;
Building and supporting Kubernetes infrastructure and internal platform services;
Managing infrastructure reliability, scalability, security and performance;
Working with networking components and infrastructure services;
Collaborating with engineering teams on infrastructure architecture and operational improvements;
Participating in incident management and continuously improving operational standards and automation processes.




What makes you a great candidate:

5+ years of experience in DevOps, Infrastructure Engineering, or Platform Engineering roles;
Strong hands-on experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code practices;
Experience working with Linux systems (Ubuntu), networking, and production infrastructure;
Experience designing reliable and scalable infrastructure for production environments;
Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and containerised infrastructure;
Experience working with cloud providers such as AWS and GCP;
Strong understanding of scalability, reliability, redundancy, and operational best practices;
Experience with automation, CI/CD pipelines, and GitOps;
Ability to work independently in a goal-oriented engineering environment;
Strong ownership mindset and ability to make and explain technical decisions;
Motivation for continuous learning and professional growth;
Strong communication and collaboration skills.




Extra expertise we appreciate:

Experience with large-scale Terraform refactoring projects;
Experience building internal platform engineering capabilities;
Experience with hybrid cloud and on-premise infrastructure;
Knowledge of production-grade network architecture and network equipment configuration;
Experience working with scalable web application infrastructure hardware;
Understanding of operational processes such as incident management, change management, and vulnerability management;
Experience in introducing engineering standards and operational maturity practices.




Why choose pawaTech?

We make an impact and contribute to shaping of the iGaming culture;
We offer growth inside dedicated teams of seniors and career prospects;
We embrace a modern technological stack and an innovative approach;
We value balance - a hybrid environment, wellness-related perks and team events are waiting for you;
We play it fair with compensation that recognises and backs your achievements, team budgets and recognition milestone bonuses.
