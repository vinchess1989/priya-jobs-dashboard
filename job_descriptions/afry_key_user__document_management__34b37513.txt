Job Description

We are looking for a Key User, Document Management Systems and Tools to strengthen AFRY’s Project Delivery Tools team in Finland. The role focuses on reliable daily support and continuous improvement of the document management solutions used in engineering and project delivery. You will work closely with our existing DMS specialists, project teams, IT colleagues and system partners.

Your initial tool focus will be Alfresco based DMS and M-Files. You will support users and projects, configure and maintain DMS environments, solve incidents, develop guidance and training, and participate in testing, upgrades and development activities. Experience with other comparable project document-management and common-data-environment solutions is valuable.

This is a practical, service-oriented expert role with an opportunity to influence how document management is delivered and harmonised across projects. You will help improve service continuity, reduce dependence on individual experts and support consistent tools, data structures and ways of working. The role offers exposure to varied industrial and engineering projects and collaboration in an international environment.

Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you have:


Relevant experience in document management systems, application configuration and support, project information management or document control.
Hands-on expertise in Alfresco and/or M-Files, and readiness to develop strong capability across both platforms.
A structured, service-minded and solution-oriented way of working, with ownership from issue identification through resolution and documentation.
Clear communication and collaboration skills for working with end users, project teams, IT specialists and vendors.
Fluent professional English.



You stand out if you have:


Experience with various DMS/CDE platforms.
Experience from engineering, EPC/EPCM, construction or industrial projects.
Knowledge of document-control practices, records management, integrations, migration, testing or release management.
Experience creating user instructions and delivering training.
Finnish or Swedish language skills.
