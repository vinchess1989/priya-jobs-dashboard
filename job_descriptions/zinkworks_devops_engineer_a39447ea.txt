About Zinkworks

Zinkworks partners with leading Telecommunications and Financial Services organizations to modernize legacy systems, migrate mission-critical platforms to the cloud, and engineer AI-driven automation. From OSS transformation to rApp development and network intelligence, our teams simplify complexity and turn it into competitive advantage. Based in Ireland and operating across the EU, UK, and US, Zinkworks combines deep domain expertise with delivery excellence to help clients modernize faster and operate smarter.

Key Responsibilities


Build and maintain GitLab CI/CD pipelines and shared pipeline templates used across multiple development groups
Own the compliance layer of the delivery process — integrating and automating SonarQube and Snyk checks, and writing tooling that enforces organisational code standards
Manage application releases to OpenShift, deployed via GitOps/ArgoCD, including pre-production and production releases executed within formal change windows
Administer and troubleshoot OpenShift clusters — node issues, cluster failures, and platform-level incidents
Deliver package deployments and configuration changes to Cisco NSO and Itential IAP, including custom network orchestration workflows, user access, and administration of the underlying servers
Maintain platform infrastructure through Ansible automation and self-managed GitLab runners, alongside platform documentation and incident runbooks


Ideal Experience


5+ years in platform, DevOps, release, or infrastructure engineering roles
Strong hands-on experience with GitLab CI/CD, pipeline-as-code, and Git-based workflows
Solid Linux administration skills and scripting experience (Bash, Python) with Ansible for configuration management and automation
Experience running containerised workloads on Kubernetes/OpenShift, ideally with GitOps tooling such as ArgoCD
Familiarity with code quality and security scanning tools (SonarQube, Snyk) and with service mesh (Istio) and identity/SSO (Keycloak) configuration
Willingness to learn network orchestration platforms (Cisco NSO, Itential IAP) — used here to deliver and maintain automated network service workflows
Experience supporting production systems in a regulated or enterprise environment, working to a structured change management process
