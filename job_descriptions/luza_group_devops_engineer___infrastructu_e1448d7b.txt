What You'll do:


Work closely with internal and external stakeholders, developers, and other teams to ensure timely and high-quality deliverables;
Design, implement, and continuously evolve Infrastructure as Code solutions;
Automate infrastructure and system provisioning, configuration, and operational tasks using Terraform, Terragrunt, Ansible, and scripting;
Develop reusable modules, automation, and golden paths that enable development teams to provision and consume infrastructure through self-service mechanisms;
Continuously improve the infrastructure platform, reducing manual processes, operational toil, and technical debt;
Implement and improve CI/CD pipelines and automation to support infrastructure and application delivery;
Manage and evolve our Azure cloud infrastructure and services, ensuring scalability, reliability, security, and cost efficiency;
Contribute to the management and evolution of our Kubernetes / AKS environment as part of the wider infrastructure platform
Design, develop, and implement new platform capabilities that improve quality, reliability, scalability, and developer experience;
Promote Cloud Native, DevOps, SRE, and Infrastructure as Code best practices across teams;
Maintain cost efficiency and promote FinOps principles across infrastructure and cloud platforms;
Analyse, troubleshoot, and resolve complex infrastructure and platform incidents, ensuring root causes are addressed and recurrence is avoided;
Implement code review, testing, validation, and quality mechanisms for infrastructure and automation code;
Promote a culture where infrastructure and operational processes are managed through version-controlled code rather than manual provisioning and administration;



Who You Are:


Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code, particularly Terraform, including reusable modules, state management, and infrastructure automation;
Ability to design reusable infrastructure, automation, and platform capabilities, with a strong focus on standardisation and self-service;
Knowledge of configuration management and automation, preferably Ansible;
Strong hands-on knowledge of GitLab and GitLab CI/CD, including pipeline development and automation;
Background in public cloud platforms, preferably Azure;
Good knowledge of Kubernetes and containerisation;
Familiarity with GitOps methodologies, preferably Argo CD;
Ability to develop automation using Python, Bash, or similar scripting/programming languages;
Good understanding of DevOps, Cloud Native, and SRE principles;
Knowledge of code repositories, code reviews, and automated testing practices;
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with a proactive and autonomous mindset;
Fluency in English (mandatory);
Mostly Remote;



Additional Preferred Skills


Strong knowledge of Kubernetes / AKS and the wider CNCF ecosystem;
Familiarity with Observability, including monitoring, logging, and tracing
Knowledge of developer self-service and automation, particularly using Atlassian products (Jira, Confluence, JSM);
Good knowledge of Linux administration and troubleshooting;
Understanding of FinOps and cloud cost optimisation;
Familiarity with development platforms and internal developer tooling;
Exposure to Agile ways of working, particularly Kanban;
Curiosity and motivation to learn new technologies and processes;
Autonomy, critical thinking, and strong collaboration skills;
Relevant certifications in Terraform, Azure, Kubernetes, or similar technologies;



What you'll get:

Benefits plan;
