(Senior) Process Development Engineer
Location:  

Porvoo, FI

Country/Region:  FI
Job Area:  Technology & Product Development
Business Unit:  Technology & Innovation

Are you looking for a career in an inspiring, global environment where your expertise makes a difference? At Neste, you will be working for the world’s leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel, which enable our customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions.

We are looking for you,

(Senior) Process Development Engineer

 

to our Renewables Processes team in Porvoo.


As a (Senior) Process Development Engineer, you will be in a key role in supporting the continuous improvement of our existing renewable products refineries in Finland, the Netherlands and Singapore. As a subject matter expert, you will develop safe and feasible process concepts and processes, perform technical design or simulations/modelling or perform technoeconomical evaluations, and/or acting as the project team leader. You will be a contributor to our SAF production growth strategy and the development of Neste’s pretreatment and/or upgrading processes. In our team, you will also contribute to the production sites' operational troubleshooting, develop new process concepts in low carbon, or renewable technology through supporting technology development and investment projects. Together with your ambitious contribution, we can do meaningful work to drive Neste forward.

The job is located in Kilpilahti, Porvoo, which is about 30-45 minutes by car from the capital region. We organize free bus transportation to Kilpilahti for our employees from the capital region, Porvoo and the nearby areas. It is possible to work partly remotely through our hybrid working practice.

 

Reasons to join

We operate in an inspiring and evolving business environment as a global leader in renewable fuels. We contribute to Neste’s success by living our values every day: we care, we have courage, we cooperate.

We prioritize safety at all times and in every location. We strive to create a positive and impactful work environment.

Transformation brings new possibilities and fosters our courage and curiosity. As a global  company, we support personal development with versatile learning and career opportunities. Collaboration is key, and we believe in flexible, smart ways of working. We work at the office at least three days a week to ensure seamless teamwork and a sense of belonging.



In addition, our benefits for employees in Finland include, for example, a personnel fund as part of the overall remuneration, good occupational health care, a membership in a sickness or health insurance fund and leisure time accident insurance. We also support leisure time activities, e.g. by offering benefits for sports and culture (ePassi).



 

More about the job

 

Your role in our team

 

Providing subject matter (technical) expertise to production site development initiatives, troubleshooting, projects and/or acting as a task coordinator

Contributing to the early phase concept creation and the process design through later stages of projects

Supporting R&D in experiment and pilot testing design

Collaborate actively with a global network of process engineers, R&D specialists, modeling experts, and engineering disciplines to achieve Neste’s strategic goals

 

Keys to success in this role

 

M.Sc. degree in Chemical Engineering

Work experience in relevant industry process engineering in the field of oil refining, petrochemical or chemicals

Knowledge of thermochemical conversion, catalytic processes, or bioprocesses 

Understanding of process design activities (for example, material and heat balance calculations, equipment sizing)

Understanding of processing of renewable or circular feedstock is a plus

Interest and high motivation of digitalization and use of AI

Fluent written and spoken command of English

A hands-on, proactive attitude and a strong willingness to learn new technologies quickly

Strong interpersonal, communication and self-leadership skills

A responsible attitude with a safety-oriented mindset

Neste in brief

Neste (NESTE, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with production on three continents. The company’s renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027. Neste also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland. The company has a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offering, such as EV charging, in Finland and in the Baltics.

Neste’s strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels, which help its customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. Neste is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion. Read more: neste.com
