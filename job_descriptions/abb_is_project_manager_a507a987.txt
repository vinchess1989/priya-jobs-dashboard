IS Project Manager

In this role, you will lead strategic Information Systems (IS) projects that support digital transformation, business process improvement, and operational excellence across the Sales and Service Operations domain. You will be responsible for driving delivery from initiation through deployment, ensuring projects achieve agreed business outcomes while aligning stakeholders, managing risks, and maintaining effective governance.

Each day, you will plan, coordinate, and deliver IS initiatives across Salesforce platforms, enterprise integrations, and service operations. You will serve as the primary point of accountability for project execution, stakeholder alignment, delivery governance, and business value realization.

The work model for the role is: hybrid ().

Preferred locations are: Krakow (Poland), Helsinki (Finland), or Bangalore (India).

This role is contributing to the IS Sales and Service Operations Domain for ELDS and ELSE organization and reports to the IS Domain Lead.

You will be mainly accountable for


Leading end-to-end delivery of medium to large-scale IS projects using Agile, Waterfall, or Hybrid methodologies.
Defining project scope, objectives, timelines, governance structures, success criteria, and business outcomes together with key stakeholders.
Driving cross-functional teams consisting of Business Process Owners (BPOs) business SMEs, product owners, architects, developers, testers, integration specialists, change managers, and delivery partners.
Ensuring successful delivery against agreed scope, timelines, quality standards, adoption targets, and business outcomes, while acting as the primary point of contact for business and IT stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle and leading governance forums, Steering Committees, project reviews, and status reporting activities.
Managing stakeholder expectations, facilitating decision-making, resolving delivery roadblocks, capturing lessons learned, and driving continuous improvement of project delivery practices; communicating delivery progress, risks, decisions, and recommendations clearly to business and technology leadership.
Identifying, tracking, and mitigating project risks, issues, dependencies, assumptions, and constraints through effective RAID management.
Leading Salesforce and digital transformation initiatives while ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture and platform standards.
Managing external vendors and delivery partners to ensure successful execution of project deliverables, driving organizational change, business readiness, stakeholder adoption, and successful transition into operational support.
Leveraging Azure DevOps to manage project plans, delivery roadmaps, work items, dependencies, progress reporting, and governance tracking.


Qualifications For The Role


You are highly skilled in project management methodologies including Agile, Waterfall, and Hybrid delivery approaches, and you have a proven track record of independently driving project governance, stakeholder alignment, escalation management, and delivery execution.
You have 5+ years of experience delivering enterprise-scale Information Systems projects, and have been leading Salesforce-related initiatives involving Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Field Service, Experience Cloud, Marketing Cloud, or CRM transformation programs for 3 years or more.
You have strong experience managing global, cross-functional teams across business and IT organizations, external vendors, system integrators, and consulting partners; you possess strong stakeholder management, communication, facilitation, and conflict resolution skills.
You have strong experience using Azure DevOps for project planning, backlog management, sprint execution, release tracking, dependency management, reporting, and delivery governance; you are proficient in project collaboration and documentation tools including Azure DevOps, Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Project, and Microsoft 365.
You understand Agile delivery governance and are capable of using Azure DevOps to maintain roadmaps, manage work items, monitor delivery metrics, track execution progress, and support executive reporting; you have experience establishing and maintaining Azure DevOps structures including Epics, Features, User Stories, Boards, Backlogs, Dashboards, Queries, Delivery Plans, and release tracking processes.
You understand Salesforce delivery governance including release management, testing approaches, environment strategy, and deployment practices; you have experience leading CRM transformation, customer service, or digital business initiatives.
You demonstrate strong capabilities in influencing without authority and building alignment across multiple organizations, and you have practical experience driving organizational change, business adoption, and value realization.
You possess an understanding of enterprise application landscapes and integration dependencies involving Salesforce, SAP, MuleSoft, Azure Integration Services, Identity & Access Management, Master Data Management, and Enterprise Reporting platforms.
You hold a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, Engineering, or a related discipline.


Preferred Certifications


PMP, PRINCE2, or equivalent Project Management certification.
Scrum Master, SAFe Agilist, or Agile certification.
Salesforce certifications are highly desirable.
Change Management certification (Prosci, ADKAR) is an advantage.
