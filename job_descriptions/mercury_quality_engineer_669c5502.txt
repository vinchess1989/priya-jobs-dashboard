Key Responsibilities Of The Role


Assist the Quality Manager in achieving contract quality objectives by coordinating activities, monitoring compliance, and providing proactive feedback on quality performance.
Promote the company’s quality vision and strategic goals by encouraging best practices across project teams and fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
Conduct routine and ad hoc site inspections to verify that construction, fabrication, or installation activities are executed in accordance with approved ITPs, specifications, and quality standards
Plan, coordinate, and deliver quality-related training and induction sessions for all Mercury personnel and supply chain partners, ensuring consistent understanding of project standards, procedures, and compliance expectations across the workforce.
Support the creation and submission of contractual and internal quality deliverables, including plans, reports, and records, ensuring they are timely, complete, and aligned with project specifications.
Review and monitor the technical deliverables submitted by vendors and contractors—such as test plans, procedures, and certifications—to confirm they meet contractual and regulatory expectations.
Analyse all documentation provided by subcontractors and vendors to ensure it complies with relevant standards and project requirements, identifying issues and following up for corrections.
Examine Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs) for clarity, technical completeness, proper sequencing, and alignment with codes and project specifications.
Support the implementation of project-specific quality procedures by guiding project teams and ensuring proper adoption of standardized processes.
Oversee quality-related documentation from contractors, confirming its relevance, accuracy, and adherence to the agreed-upon quality specifications and formats.
Schedule and perform internal and supply chain audits at critical project stages to evaluate compliance, uncover risks, and provide actionable recommendations for improvement.
Identify and raise non-conformances when work or deliverables fall short of specification; lead the resolution process with clear documentation and root cause analysis.
Scrutinize project deliverables such as work packs, test packs, and documentation handovers to verify they fully reflect quality requirements prior to acceptance or release.
Compile detailed weekly and monthly reports capturing quality performance, non-conformance trends, and corrective actions; manage KPI metrics to evaluate overall effectiveness


Essential Criteria For The Role


5+ years Quality experience in construction/engineering/petrochemical and / or within a multinational manufacturing.
Minimum Diploma or Degree in Engineering, equivalent to BSc, in engineering (CSA, Electrical or Mechanical).
ISO 9001:2015 Lead or Internal Auditor.
Presenting and Training skillset.
High ability to read and interpret QMS documents such Plans, Procedures (SOPs), Inspection and Test Plans.
Effective communication and collaboration abilities across multidisciplinary teams.
Confident, creative, and energetic approach to addressing all quality-related issues
