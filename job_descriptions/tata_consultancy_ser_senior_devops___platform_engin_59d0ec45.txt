Are you a Senior DevOps & Platform Engineer in Madrid seeking a new interesting challenge? 🔍

✍️ WHAT WILL YOU DO?

We are looking for a dynamic, proactive and talented person to join our team and perform the following tasks:

Build and maintain scalable cloud and on-premises infrastructure.
Develop and optimize CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
Manage Kubernetes platforms and containerized applications.
Implement infrastructure automation and platform monitoring solutions.
Support RF test environments and integration of additional RF instruments.
Improve platform reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency.
Troubleshoot infrastructure, deployment, and test environment issues.
Collaborate with Software, RF, FPGA, Hardware, DSP, and QA teams.




💻 WHAT TECHNOLOGIES, METHODOLOGIES OR TOOLS WILL YOU USE?

Kubernetes
Docker
Jenkins
GitLab CI/CD
AWS
Terraform / CloudFormation
Python / Bash
Splunk, Prometheus and Grafana




🧐 WHAT ARE WE LOOKING FOR?

Years' experience: 6-10 Years

English: C1

Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, or related Technical Discipline

Experience with Kubernetes Administration, Docker Containerization, CI/CD pipeline development, AWS Cloud, Infrastructure as Code, Linux/Unix Administration, monitoring platforms, and engineering/test environments.




📅 WHERE AND WHEN?

Workplace: On-site — Madrid

Work Schedule: 09:00-18:00 with one hour lunch break

💡 WHO ARE WE?

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is an Information Technology (IT) company founded in 1968 as part of the Indian Tata Group and is one of the top 3 technology companies globally 🚀

With a presence in 55 countries and more than 600,000 employees, TCS is considered one of the 10 best companies to work for worldwide in 2025 according to the Top Employers Institute 🥇

TCS Spain started operations in 2001 and currently has a diverse workforce that collaborates with the main Spanish and multinational companies 🌏

TCS Spain has been certified as a Top Employer 2025 and has also been chosen as one of the 100 Best Companies to Work for in Spain in 2025 according to Forbes 🏆

Among the portfolio of services, TCS has information technology services, asset-based solutions, global consulting, engineering and industrial services, digital solutions and services, application maintenance and development, quality assurance and testing services, IT infrastructure and BPS 🎯

Responsible for development, TCS Spain is committed to inclusion, diversity and sustainability, and promotes flexibility policies that support wellbeing and work-life balance 💯
