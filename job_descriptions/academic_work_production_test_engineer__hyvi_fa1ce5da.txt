Production Test Engineer, Hyvinkää

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

This onsite Production Test Engineer role in Hyvinkää focuses on providing technical support, troubleshooting, updates, and development of production test systems.
Candidates should hold a B.Sc. or M.Sc. in electrical, electronics or automation engineering or equivalent and be able to read wiring diagrams and use oscilloscopes and multimeters.
Basic LabVIEW skills, some production testing experience, and understanding of I2C, SPI and UART are required, while TestStand and electrical design experience are advantageous.
The company offers a collaborative international environment, lunch benefit, yearly academic development support and opportunities for professional growth and networking.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



Join our client’s production testing team in a global market-leading company and play a key role in supporting and developing test systems. This position offers a chance to grow your skills through diverse tasks such as troubleshooting, technical support, modifications, updates, and test system development. Whether you are a recent graduate, have a few years of work experience, or are nearing the completion of your studies, this is an excellent opportunity to build your career as a Production Test Engineer in a supportive and international environment. The work is primarily onsite at Hyvinkää.

About the role

As a Production test Engineer, you will work primarily onsite in Hyvinkää, focusing on production testing and test system support. You will provide technical support for test stations, perform troubleshooting, and participate in modifications, updates, and development activities. You will collaborate closely with a team of engineers and gain valuable experience in a global environment.

You are offered

Be part of a collaborative team of designers and testers, contributing to the development of current product versions. Benefit from a supportive environment where your initiative and self-direction are valued.

A role where you will directly contribute to the quality and reliability of products
An opportunity to grow professionally and develop your expertise in testing and test systems
A collaborative and encouraging work environment
Lunch benefit
Yearly financial support for academic development
As a consultant at Academic Work, you are offered the chance to extend your network and establish valuable contacts for the future

Work tasks

Provide technical support for test stations
Troubleshoot and perform updates or modifications to test systems
Support test system development activities
Collaborate with the production testing team and other engineers
Read and work with wiring diagrams and electrical schematics
Use measurement instruments (oscilloscopes, multimeters, etc.)

We are looking for

Ms.c or Bs.c in electrical, electronics or automation engineer or equivalent fundamental knowledge in electrical engineering
Ability to read wiring diagrams and electrical schematics
Some experience of production testing
Basic knowledge of LabVIEW
Basic knowledge of measurement instruments (oscilloscopes, multimeters, etc.)
Some understanding of communication protocols (I2C, SPI, UART)
Strong communication skills in English (Finnish is an advantage)
Good teamwork skills and motivation to learn and develop
Desire to develop personal skills and motivation for on-the-job learning
Aspiration to work and succeed as a Test Equipment Engineer

Additional advantages:

Experience with TestStand
Experience in electrical design or electrical installations
Experience with PCBAs and related components
Interest in test methods and test development
