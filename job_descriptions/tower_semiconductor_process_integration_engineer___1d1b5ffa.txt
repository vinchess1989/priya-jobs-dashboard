About Tower Semiconductor







Looking for a career path in the high-tech industry? Become part of a team focused on delivering the most exciting semiconductor technology in the world! If you enjoy working with others in a dynamic, fast-paced environment and are looking for an opportunity to grow your career in this exciting industry, then Tower Semiconductor is the place to be!







Tower Semiconductor, the leading foundry of high-value analog semiconductor solutions, provides technology, development, and process platforms for analog integrated circuits to more than 300 customers worldwide across growing markets such as communications, mobile, infrastructure, consumer, industrial, automotive, medical, and aerospace and defense, among others.







Join Tower and become part of a dynamic organization with exceptionally talented and dedicated team members who work collaboratively to provide best-in-class technological solutions to our clients.







Job Description







Responsible for improvement and sustaining of all manufacturing processes; development of new process modules; leading or coordinating large projects. Interact and collaborate with Process Engineering, Equipment Engineering, Operations and R&D groups on inter-departmental and cross-functional projects. Perform circuit level failure analysis, analyze E-test and product yield data, and provide feedback to engineers and managers to continuously improve the manufacturing process.







This Job Is For You If







You excel at improving and sustaining manufacturing processes
You have the ability to develop new process modules
You thrive in cross-functional collaboration with Process Engineering, Equipment Engineering, Operations, and R&D teams
You can analyze E-test and product yield data to drive continuous manufacturing improvements
You possess strong circuit-level failure analysis skills
You are naturally curious and eager to learn new things
You are a self-starter who takes initiative
You enjoy managing multiple priorities simultaneously
You make decisions based on data and analytics
You are diligent and detail-oriented







Job Requirements







MS or PhD in Electrical or Chemical Engineering, Physics or Materials Science
Strong ability to effectively communicate and present complex technical concepts and analytical findings to diverse audiences
Demonstrated knowledge and hands-on experience in at least one of the following domains: Process Integration, BiCMOS and/or Photonics Device Physics, Thin Films, Etch, CMP, Photolithography, or Diffusion
Proven experience with Statistical Process Control (SPC), Design of Experiments (DOE), and yield analysis tools
Minimum of 7 years of professional experience in semiconductor process integration or related field







Compensation: $112,000 - $170,000
