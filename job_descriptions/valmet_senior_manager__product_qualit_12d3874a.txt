Shape the Future of Product Quality as a Senior Manager, Product Quality Assurance

Join a global quality transformation journey. In this role, you will work closely with Product Management, Engineering, Technology Transfer teams, and other key stakeholders to develop and harmonize product quality requirements across the product lifecycle, strengthen engineering quality practices and standardization, and further develop Technology Transfer quality governance. You will help ensure that quality is built in from the beginning, while turning experience and lessons learned into preventive improvements. You will also help build digital quality frameworks by leveraging AI and data-driven insights to improve decision-making, organizational learning, and continuous improvement.

Your responsibilities

Lead the Product Quality Assurance function and develop product quality requirements across business areas and the product lifecycle.
Partner with Product Management to identify and harmonize critical-to-quality (CTQ) and critical-to-safety (CTS) characteristics across product families and technologies.
Use quality cost, nonconformance, failure, and feedback data to strengthen product, engineering, and technology transfer requirements.
Drive standardization of engineering processes and embed them into a unified Engineering Quality Framework across global networks.
Lead the development of digital quality data models and technology transfer QA governance, including Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) development and product-specific applications.

Expectations for you

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Quality, or a related technical field.
Strong experience in quality management or related roles, including line manager responsibilities.
Solid understanding of product quality management principles, quality assurance frameworks, lifecycle-based quality management, and engineering development processes.
Experience using quality and failure data to identify trends, risks, and improvement opportunities.
Understanding of digital tools and AI applications in quality assurance, data analysis, and requirement traceability.

To succeed in this role

You combine strategic thinking with practical execution and can translate a broad quality vision into clear priorities and frameworks. You build strong relationships across functions, influence stakeholders through data and business impact, and stay focused on the initiatives that create the greatest value. You are comfortable working in an evolving environment and driving continuous improvement through structured quality practices and digital innovation.

We also expect you to align with Valmet’s Leadership Principles—practical behaviours that guide how we lead, collaborate, and succeed together:
• Take accountability – Act with ownership, set clear expectations, and follow through
• Lead with courage – Champion change, try bold ideas, and learn quickly
• Build on trust – Listen to understand, be open, and create psychological safety
• Collaborate for growth – Work across boundaries, value diverse perspectives, and focus on customer value
