Job Description

This position is part of the FN BBN TOP DevOps team, which defines and implements the digital architecture for key R&D areas in support of a business transformation to achieve product development acceleration/continuous delivery on the longer term.

The DevOps Continuous Integration Engineer contributes to the FN BBN DevOps enablement roadmap implementation following the priorities set by the DevOps transformation lead.

He or she will be responsible for optimizing the sw integration & test flows within FN BBN. Cooperation with product delivery units and service teams is key part of the job.

As an expert, he or she will leverage on and re-use DevOps practices and tools introduced by other BUs and explore new possibilities provided by Open Source SW.

#NIFN2026

How You Will Contribute And What You Will Learn


Own the CI/CD Pipeline: Design, implement, and manage robust and scalable CI/CD pipelines for our software development lifecycle, ensuring smooth and efficient integration and delivery.
Champion Containerization and Automation: Leverage your expertise in Docker and Kubernetes to build and manage containerized applications and automate deployment processes.
Master Build Systems and Scripting: Utilize your strong scripting skills (Python, Bash) and knowledge of build systems (Make, Bazel) to optimize build processes and implement advanced caching techniques.
Thrive in a Linux Environment: Demonstrate your deep understanding of Linux systems, including system administration, networking, and security, to ensure the stability and performance of our CI/CD infrastructure.
Collaborate and Problem-Solve: Work closely with development teams to troubleshoot issues, implement best practices, and continuously improve our CI/CD processes and tools.


Key Skills And Experience


Champion CI/CD Automation: Design, develop, and implement automation scripts and solutions to streamline and optimize the entire CI/CD pipeline for FN BBN products.
Manage & Harden Environments: Take ownership of build and test environments, ensuring their stability, security, and scalability across physical infrastructure, OpenStack, and Kubernetes.
Accelerate Software Delivery: Continuously improve CI availability and cycle times to accelerate the software validation and delivery process for increased efficiency and faster time-to-market.
Elevate Code Quality & Testing: Strengthen static code analysis practices and enhance test automation frameworks to ensure high-quality code and robust software releases.
Collaborate & Drive Improvements: Partner with product development, cloud infrastructure, and operations teams to identify and implement CI/CD pipeline optimizations and process improvements.
Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written (EN).
Cross-cultural knowledge and global mindset.
