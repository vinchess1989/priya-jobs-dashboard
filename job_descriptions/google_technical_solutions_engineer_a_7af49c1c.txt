About The Job

The Google Cloud Platform team helps customers transform and build what's next for their business — all with technology built in the cloud. Our products are developed for security, reliability and scalability, running the full stack from infrastructure to applications to devices and hardware. Our teams are dedicated to helping our customers — developers, small and large businesses, educational institutions and government agencies — see the benefits of our technology come to life. As part of an entrepreneurial team in this rapidly growing business, you will play a key role in understanding the needs of our customers and help shape the future of businesses of all sizes use technology to connect with customers, employees and partners.

As a part of the Scaled Solutions team, you will work with Google Server Operations and the Data Center Software teams, delivering technical and process solutions that drive operational excellence.

In this role, you will help problem-solve at scale, troubleshooting issues globally by creating innovations in technology, operational processes, and policies. You will focus on resolving workflow execution blockers due to systems and software issues that arise across the supply chain software stack in the course of capacity fulfillment and enablement operations in Google data centers around the world by delivering innovative solutions and drive operational efficiency and cost-savings for Google Cloud.

You will have a mature set of performance metrics and invest in a culture of continuous improvement and operational excellence, having a strong technical background is an important component of the role as we support multiple backend systems with interdependencies.
