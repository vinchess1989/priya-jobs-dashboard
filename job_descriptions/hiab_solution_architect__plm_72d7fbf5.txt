Your mission

As the PLM Solution Architect, you will bridge engineering processes with our broader enterprise data ecosystem, connecting production, service, and sales. You will translate business requirements into robust technical blueprints for Windchill, drive enterprise standards, manage technical project streams, and ensure high-quality master and transactional data delivery. In this position, you will provide technical support and functional knowledge required to configure, troubleshoot, and optimize specific solutions and own the technical development backlog within the project.

The primary location is Helsinki, Finland, but we are also open to applications from Tampere and Raisio, Finland.

Key Responsibilities


Lead technical streams for PLM (Windchill) implementations, upgrades, and data migrations from scoping to deployment
Partner with business and IM stakeholders to capture complex functional requirements and translate them into scalable, robust technical blueprints
Design robust data models, workflows, and lifecycle states while prioritizing out-of-the-box functionality
Partner with cross-functional stakeholders, software vendors, and external partners to evaluate new features and optimize solutions
Manage solution costs, operational/development budgets, and release management to minimize business interruptions.


What We Are Looking For


Bachelor's or Master's degree in IT, Business Administration, or a related field
5+ years of experience in Windchill PLM platform administration, configuration, and development. Knowledge about the customizations is seen as a benefit
Strong knowledge of CAD technologies, manufacturing business processes, and IT/IM enterprise architecture
Proven track record in project management, change management, and third-party vendor management
Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and analytical skills in an international environment
Occasional travelling, a few times per year depending on the projects
Full professional proficiency in written and spoken English.
