Product Lead, Measurement Infrastructure

Do you enjoy working with complex technical systems and ensuring they continuously perform at their best throughout their lifecycle? Are you motivated by improving product quality, driving changes, and keeping offerings competitive over time? Join us in shaping the long-term success of our products within the Measurement Infrastructure team at Bluefors. Interested? Keep reading!

We are now looking for a RF Product Lead to join our Measurement Infrastructure team in Helsinki. In this role, you will take ownership of the maintenance and continuous development of products and options within the Measurement Infrastructure domain.

In this role, you will lead and coordinate product lifecycle activities across systems and configurable options, including product changes, upgrades, and end-of-life transitions. You will ensure that product structures, configurations, and data remain accurate and aligned across systems. Working closely with Product Managers, engineering, sourcing, and production, you will drive consistency and improvements across the product offering and ensure that changes are implemented effectively across system-level solutions.

We are looking for a structured and proactive professional with a strong technical mindset and the ability to work across complex system environments. You are comfortable managing dependencies, coordinating across interfaces, and ensuring that both details and the overall system perspective are under control. Your ability to communicate and collaborate across functions helps you move topics forward smoothly. You stand out if you bring experience working with system-level products or configurable solutions and have a strong understanding of product maintenance, data quality, and lifecycle management in a technical environment.

We offer you an interesting, challenging, and responsible position, supported by a highly skilled and growing organization. This is an excellent opportunity for your own professional growth in a dynamic, enthusiastic, and professional team where we work together and support each other. We offer a multicultural working environment with room to grow, where you can fully use your potential and continuously develop your skills.

Main responsibilities:

Lead and coordinate product maintenance activities within Measurement Infrastructure

Maintain product technical requirements

Drive product changes, upgrades, and engineering change processes across system-level offerings

Maintain and develop product structures, configurations, and data quality

Ensure alignment between product requirements and technical specifications across system interfaces

Drive quality and performance improvements for systems and configurable options

Maintain RF performance requirements and coordinate verification and validation of RF-related product changes with engineering and quality teams

Support product transitions and end-of-life management

Contribute to development of product lifecycle management (PLM) processes

Act as a technical expert for Measurement Infrastructure products

What we expect from you

Relevant technical education (e.g. Electrical Engineering, RF Engineering, Physics or similar)

Experience in product lifecycle management, product maintenance, or engineering roles

Experience working with complex systems or configurable product structures

Solid understanding of RF measurement technology and the ability to translate product requirements into measurable RF performance specifications

Understanding of verification, validation, and test planning for RF products and complex technical systems

Understanding of product changes, configurations, and data management

Structured and proactive way of working with strong attention to detail

Ability to collaborate across functions and manage dependencies

Good communication skills in English

We also appreciate

Experience with PDM, ERP or similar systems

Project management experience

Experience in system-level product environments

Strong quality and customer-focused mindset

Previous experience in a line management role with direct responsibility for team members

Understanding of cryogenic systems and experience with structured root cause analysis and quality improvement in hardware products

Please apply as soon as possible, on November 30, 2026, at the latest through our recruitment system at BLUEFORS.COM. We will review applications on an ongoing basis, and the position will be filled as soon as a suitable candidate is found.

For more information, please contact Jukka Korhonen, Director, Measurement Infrastructure at jukka.korhonen@bluefors.com.

About Bluefors

The world's leading manufacturer of ultra-low temperature cryogen-free dilution refrigerators.

We design and manufacture measurement systems, that can provide clean and quiet environment for scientific research, even at ultra-low temperatures. Our systems have options which include wiring and superconducting magnets and can be customized to meet the requirements of each customer. We have a strong focus on the quantum computing and information community and are here to support scientists and industries go further in their endeavors. For this we have brought together the best minds from a variety of fields. If you are a skilled physicist, technician, engineer or business professional and looking for an interesting job in multicultural environment, join our growth story at Bluefors.

Here we are curious to the core, have wide-range expertise, and have one mission: progress. Continuous development, learning, and collaboration are fundamental to our way of working. In our agile company structure customers are in direct contact with scientists and engineers who design their system. We deliver systems all around the world and the markets for our products are constantly growing. Currently we employ more than 600 professionals from over 50 different nationalities. We offer competitive benefits package, employee share program, flexible working environment and a non-smoking workplace.
