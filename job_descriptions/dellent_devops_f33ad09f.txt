Descrição da Função

Dellent is a consulting company focused in System Information and Telecommunications. Our goal is to help our candidates and consultants to take a step forward in their careers through projects that meet their needs and expectations. In this project you'll contact with the European stock market area. Despite its development center being located in Porto and all administrative support in Lisbon, you will be part of a multicultural team that is spread across several European cities. We are seeking a DevOps to join our team in Porto (hybrid). Requirements: - Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, with 5+ years of experience as a Cloud & DevOps Engineer, preferably working with AWS and GitLab. - Strong experience with CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and automation tools such as Git, GitLab, GitHub, Jenkins, and Terraform. - Solid knowledge of cloud infrastructure, microservices architectures, containerization technologies, Windows and Linux environments, and security best practices. - Experience managing databases (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server, MongoDB) and designing, maintaining, and supporting SaaS platforms and cloud-based applications. - Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with development teams and work in dynamic, fast-paced environments. - Fluent English is required. Responsibilities - Design, implement, and maintain cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code solutions to support secure, scalable, and efficient software delivery. - Develop automation solutions for development, testing, and production environments, improving deployment processes, operational efficiency, and time-to-market. - Monitor infrastructure performance, capacity, and availability, troubleshoot complex issues, optimize systems, and ensure platform reliability across cloud environments. - Collaborate with development, operations, and architecture teams to design scalable application architectures, improve engineering processes, and implement DevOps best practices. - Produce and maintain technical documentation, support software and infrastructure projects, perform root cause analysis, and drive continuous improvement through automation and process optimization. We offer: - Health insurance; - A personalized training plan, with a budget to spend on the training you find necessary; - Constant feedback so you can grow professionally; - Remote onboarding process; - Team events every semester so you can live new adventures; - Culture of proximity and transparency. Your ideas and needs are heard and valued by us!
