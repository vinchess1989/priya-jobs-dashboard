Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy. We are looking for a Senior Engineer full-time on permanent basis.

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We're not just a bank, we're a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

Meet the Black Ops team. Our role is to help and support all different stakeholders who are working on the Internal Developer Platform of Nordea. In short we help our (internal) customers, we help the developers of the platform and we assist in complex situations where expectations fail reality.

Main responsibilities in this role:


Assist on the Agentic AI roll out towards the different development teams to refactor legacy code into code able to run on current strategy supported platforms
Build Continuous Integration and Deployment systems to fully support an end to end pipeline for developers and teams
Collaborate in a highly technical environment always trying to find the best solution for the customer while delivering on our promise to reduce legacy
Develop DevOps culture within Service Domains and prepare them to be independent
Collaborate with developers on how to get the most out of our Agentic AI solution, but also provide feedback so we can improve the current offering


We follow hybrid model of working (up to 40 % remote). This role is open in Finland (Helsinki). We offer employment with various benefits .

Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you:


Are self-confident but humble
Have excellent understanding what other than just code quality drives quality when it comes to developing and delivering software
Are passionate about DevOps, Automation & AI solutions
Are solution driven and you can share lots of examples how this is visible in your daily work
Possess initiative and are innovative
Have outstanding communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence and build relationships at all levels of the organization
Are able to develop yourself and build up knowledge by seeking for answers


Your background and skills include:


Experience with Spring (boot), NodeJS / Java Development
Strong Linux and Windows based system administration skills, with practical experience operating them in a virtual, cloud or containerized environment
Continuous Integration and Deployment experience, utilising tools like Tekton, ArgoCD, Terraform, Jenkins, Ansible, in co-operation with GIT/Bitbucket
Familiarity with container based architecture and infrastructure solutions, using Docker and Kubernetes


It would be ideal if you also:


AWS or Google Cloud Experience
Groovy
Vibe coding experience
