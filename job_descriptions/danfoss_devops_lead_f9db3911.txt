Job Description
The Impact You'll Make

You will shape the future of software development at Danfoss Drives by defining the technical direction for a platform used by more than 500 developers. Your work will directly improve how embedded software is built, tested, and released—at scale.

In this role, you will connect DevOps, data, and AI to create an intelligent development ecosystem. You will enable faster delivery, better quality, and smarter automation through a modern data platform and AI-ready semantic layer.

This is a hands-on senior technical role, where you influence through expertise, mentorship, and strong engineering standards—playing a key role in transforming a large-scale development platform for the future.

Based in a hybrid setup in Graasten, Vaasa, Kolding, or Tampere, you will collaborate closely with distributed engineering teams.

What You’ll Be Doing
Set technical direction and standards for DevOps services to improve engineering quality and platform consistency
Build and evolve a data platform and semantic layer to make engineering data accessible and usable for AI-driven insights and automation
Optimize cloud-based development infrastructure to ensure scalability, reliability, security, and cost efficiency
Design and improve CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure as Code to increase speed, stability, and developer productivity
Mentor engineers and collaborate across teams to strengthen technical capabilities and drive platform adoption
What We're Looking For
Educational background in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, combined with proven experience leading DevOps or platform engineering initiatives in large-scale, complex environments
Strong coding and scripting skills (e.g., Python, Bash, PowerShell, plus one general-purpose language such as Go, Rust, or C#)
Hands-on experience with private and public and cloud platforms (HPE-GLPC, Azure, GCP), CI/CD platforms (ADO,Gitlab, Github) and containerized workloads, Agentic AI harnesses (Github Copilot SDK et. al.) and Lakehouse platforms (Databricks, Spark,,)
Ability to design and evolve scalable architectures and work with distributed systems and multiple integrated platforms
Strong communication and technical guidance skills, with the ability to mentor and influence without formal people management responsibilities
What You'll Get from Us

 

We promote from within and support your learning with mentoring, training, and access to global opportunities.
You’ll have flexibility, autonomy, and support to do your best work while maintaining a healthy work-life balance. Your well-being matters to us.
We strive to create an inclusive work environment where people of all backgrounds are respected, and valued for who they are.
You’ll receive benefits like annual bonus, paid vacation, pension plans, personal insurance, and more. These vary by country and contract, but they’re worth asking about—we think they’re pretty great.

Danfoss engineers solutions that increase machine productivity, reduce emissions, lower energy consumption, and enable electrification.

Our solutions are used in areas such as refrigeration, air conditioning, heating, power conversion, motor control, industrial machinery, marine, and on- and off-highway equipment. We also provide solutions for data centers, renewable energy, energy storage, heat recovery, as well as contribute to district energy solutions for cities.

Our innovative engineering dates back to 1933. Danfoss is family-owned and employs over 39,000 people. We create long-term value for our customers in more than 100 countries with a global footprint of around 100 factories.
