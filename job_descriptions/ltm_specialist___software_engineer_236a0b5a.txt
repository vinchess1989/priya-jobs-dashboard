Overview




LTM — a Larsen & Toubro company — is an AI-centric global technology services company and the Business Creativity partner to the world’s largest and most disruptive enterprises. We bring human insights and intelligent systems together to help clients create greater value at the intersection of technology and domain expertise. Our capabilities span integrated operations, transformation, and business AI — enabling new ways of working, new productivity paradigms, and new roads to value. Together with over 87,000 employees across 40 countries and our global network of partners, LTM owns business outcomes for our clients, helping them not just outperform the market, but to Outcreate it.




Senior DevOps Engineer – High Risk CI




We are looking for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join our ongoing transformation in the standardization and maturity of our DevOps practices across a portfolio of applications including datadriven codeonly and thirdparty configurable solutions. You will play a key role in designing implementing and maintaining robust CICD pipelines embedding SDLC best practices and collaborating closely with product teams to ensure scalability reliability and efficiency.




If you are passionate about DevOps excellence and ready to make a measurable impact we would like to hear from you.




Responsibilities




·       Design develop and maintain standardized CICD solutions to streamline development and operations




·       Anchor SDLC disciplines within teams ensuring consistent adoption of best practices




·       Collaborate with relevant stakeholders to optimize delivery process and deployment frequency




·       Improve and maintain existing DevOps tools and infrastructure




    Required Skills




·       Strong programming skills in Java Python or C




·       Expertise in CICD tools Jenkins Bitbucket Bamboo Git




·       Experience with agile methodologies Scrum XP




·       Proficiency in containerization ie Kubernetes Docker ArgoCD




·       Infrastructure as Code ie Ansible Terraform




·       Data solution management including ETL and orchestration tools




·       Experience leveraging AI in DevOps processes
