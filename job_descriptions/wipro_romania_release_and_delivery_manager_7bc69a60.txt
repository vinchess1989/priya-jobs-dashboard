As the Release and Delivery Manager for the PB TOM project, you will play a pivotal role in driving transparency, alignment, and execution across all workstreams and application stakeholders. You will be responsible for end-to-end delivery monitoring, release scheduling, and risk mitigation to ensure on-time PB TOM delivery and safeguard the production environment. By establishing robust processes and maintaining strong stakeholder relationships, you will enable senior leadership to make informed, data-driven decisions.

This role requires a proactive, highly organized individual capable of managing high-pressure environments, including coordinated weekend deployments and complex technical cutovers.




Key Responsibilities




Process Establishment & Governance




Centralized Tracking: Establish and maintain a comprehensive delivery repository (primarily utilizing Jira) to track the status, milestones, target dates, and ownership of all delivery items.
Framework Design: Define, implement, and continuously improve Release and Delivery Management processes, ensuring seamless collaboration across workstreams, application teams, test management, and environment management.
Release Calendaring: Create, maintain, and publish the Release Overview (ROV), detailing dependencies, scope, key milestones, and planned downtimes for all PB TOM initiatives. (also within JIRA)




Release Execution & Environment Safeguarding




Change Governance: Maintain a holistic view of all planned production changes (utilizing ServiceNow, etc.) to optimize release windows, resolve scheduling conflicts, and minimize operational risk.
Cutover Planning: Develop detailed, step-by-step cutover plans for major release weekends, mapping out technical tasks, validation steps, and rollback procedures.
Control Room Leadership: Drive weekend deployment activities by leading the "Control Room," tracking execution progress in real-time, coordinating technical teams, and managing live escalations.
Incident & Change Alignment: Align release activities with corporate ITIL frameworks, managing emergency changes and coordinate post-implementation reviews (PIRs) to capture lessons learned.




Stakeholder Management & Communication




Cross-Functional Orchestration: Serve as the central liaison between diverse stakeholder groups, including Production Management, Change Management, Test Management, IT Application Owners (IT-AOs), and Application Release Managers.
Meeting Facilitation: Prepare for, host, and document critical alignment forums, including routine Delivery and Release Management updates with workstreams and high-profile status calls with Senior Management.
Executive Reporting: Translate complex technical release risks and delivery statuses into clear, actionable executive summaries for senior leadership.




Required Skills & Qualifications




Technical & Tooling Expertise




Project Tools: Advanced proficiency in Jira (including workflow setup, customization, dashboard creation, and reporting) and MS Project.
ITSM Platforms: Strong practical knowledge of change, incident, and release management workflows within ServiceNow (or similar ITIL-aligned platforms).
Productivity & Collaboration: Expert level in Microsoft 365 suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word) and experience leveraging AI productivity tools (e.g., MS Copilot) to streamline administrative and reporting workflows.




Methodologies & Frameworks




Release & Project Management: Proven experience in project-, release management and modern software delivery lifecycles (SDLC), utilizing both Agile (Scrum/Kanban) and Waterfall methodologies.
ITIL Framework: Solid understanding of ITIL best practices, particularly Service Transition (Change, Release, and Deployment Management).




Soft Skills & Leadership




High-Stake Communication: Outstanding verbal and written communication skills in English, with a proven ability to present to, negotiate with, and influence Senior Management.
Proactive Problem Solving: A self-driven attitude with the capability to identify delivery bottlenecks or deployment risks early and implement mitigation strategies independently.
Resilience & Flexibility: Comfort operating in a fast-paced environment with a high workload. Ability to support night and weekend shifts during critical production deployment windows.




Preferred / Desirable Skills (Optional Assets)




Professional certifications in Project/Release Management (e.g., PMP, Prince2, ITIL v4, or SAFe/Scrum Master certifications).
Basic understanding of CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, and automated deployment tooling.
Experience working in highly regulated industries (e.g., Financial Services or Banking).
