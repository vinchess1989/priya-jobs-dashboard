We are looking for an Integration Lead to join Solita’s Group IT organization and to take ownership of Solita’s internal integrations and help shape how our Business Tech landscape works as we continue to grow.




In this role, you will ensure our internal systems connect reliably, support business needs, and evolve in a scalable way. You will combine hands-on integration expertise with management, prioritization, and stakeholder collaboration across domains such as HR, Finance, and Operations.

This is a role for someone who enjoys bringing clarity to complexity, driving collaboration across teams, and improving how integrations are built, maintained, and governed. You are most comfortable in providing excellent Integrations Services but are not afraid to roll-up your sleeves too.




What you’ll do

As Integration Lead, you will manage Solita’s internal integrations and help shape how integration development and operations are run going forward.




Your responsibilities include:

Owning and managing internal integrations across the Business Tech landscape, including systems such as ERP and CRM.
Ensuring existing integrations are maintained and operate reliably and meet SLAs
Driving the development of new integrations together with internal stakeholders and technical experts
Contributing hands-on when needed, especially with Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions, JavaScript, and selected custom integrations
Defining ways of working for how integrations are designed, built, maintained, and supported
Coordinating internal development capacity and services by leveraging Solita’s wider integration expertise when needed. We have ~140 customer-facing Integration Architects and specialists to back you up and help you evolve. What are the latest best practices on building integrations or leveraging AI? – we've got you!
Working with other service managers, application owners, and business stakeholders to maintain and prioritize the integrations backlog
Helping service domains enable small-scale maintenance and continuous improvement in their existing integrations. E.g. HR & Finance could maintain and develop Payroll & Banking integrations independently.




You will report to the Director, Business Tech & Development and work closely with stakeholders across our Business Platform (EPR, CRM, Analytics tools, AI tools, Collaboration tools like Slack, Teams, M365 + various in-house built apps). There are no managerial nor economic responsibilities in this role other than tracking the cost of integrations and related tools themselves.




What we’re looking for

We expect you to have:

At least 5 years of relevant experience in integration development and/or integration-related coordination or service management roles
Strong hands-on experience with Azure Logic Apps, Azure Functions, and JavaScript
Experience working with integrations in complex business system environments
The ability to operate both strategically and hands-on

To succeed in this role, you are:

A proactive communicator who keeps stakeholders informed and moves topics forward
Structured and detail-oriented, with the ability to turn complexity into clear next steps
Business-minded and pragmatic, able to focus on what creates value within available resources
Strong at building relationships and trust across teams and organizational boundaries
Experience with Workday or integrations involving ERP systems is considered an advantage.




Why join Solita in this role?

This is an opportunity to have a real impact on how Solita’s internal business platform develops and supports our operations. You will work in an international role that sits at the intersection of technology, business needs, and cross-functional collaboration. You will have the support of an experienced and collaborative internal IT organization, functional leads across key domains and Solita’s wider community of integration professionals.




At Solita, freedom, responsibility, and trust go hand in hand. We support continuous learning, flexible ways of working, and a healthy work-life balance.




Location and salary

This position is primarily based in the Helsinki metropolitan area, with flexibility for hybrid work. Strong candidates from elsewhere in Finland will also be considered. Please note that relocation support is not available for this position. Applicants must have a valid residency and right to work in Finland at the time of application.




The salary range for this role is €5,000–6,000/month, depending on experience and capabilities. This position does not include bonus components. A standard benefits package is included.




How to apply

Please submit your application through our recruitment system by 24.9.2026. We review applications on a rolling basis and may move forward with suitable candidates before the deadline. Our recruitment process consists of short phone interview and 2 interview rounds.

For more information about the role, please contact hiring manager Jouko Kiuru via jouko.kiuru@solita.fi or LinkedIn to arrange a call.







Please note that, for GDPR reasons, we cannot process applications sent by email. Also, as a key technology, data, and design partner, Solita’s business is built on trust and responsibility. For this reason, the selected candidate’s identity, work history, technical capabilities and credit record will be verified. These checks are carried out in accordance with and within the limits of the Act on the Protection of Privacy in Working Life.
