Cognizant Romania is home to 2400+ creative technologists and is one of Eastern Europe's largest Software Product Engineering delivery networks. We serve global clients in several industries, including Banking & Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare & Life Sciences, Communication Media & Technology, and Retail & MLEU (manufacturing, logistics, energy & utilities).

Cognizant Romania was established in 2018 when Cognizant acquired Softvision, a company founded in the late 1990s in Cluj-Napoca, Romania. We continue to build on our history as the preferred engineering partner for thriving Silicon Valley tech companies, now as a prominent Cognizant next generation studio.




We are seeking an experienced Release Manager to join our team.




Key Responsibilities:




Release Planning & Scheduling: Develop and manage release plan across PODs including Release deliverables/Features, Release timelines
Support on processes for data availability, Architecture approvals, risk and compliance reviews etc.
Coordination across PODs for dependency planning and scoping
Support dependency and conflicts across teams/PODs
Support prioritisation and approvals for environment set up, data availability etc
Risk Management & Mitigation
Participation in Go/No Go Decisions and communication




Must-Have Qualifications:




Strong understanding of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
Knowledge of Agile, Scrum, and ITIL frameworks
Experience with release orchestration and automation tools
Experience with MLOps/DataOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Airflow)
Proficiency in version control and configuration management
Ability to manage cross-functional teams and complex dependencies
Risk assessment and problem-solving capabilities
Communication and stakeholder coordination
Knowledge of Data and AI projects and concepts
