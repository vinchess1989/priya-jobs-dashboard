Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

We are looking for a Senior Operation Quality Specialist to join R&M Data Product Enablement unit (full-time employment agreement).

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We’re not just a bank; we’re a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

About Reference and Master Data Unit 

Reference & Master Data is responsible for enabling trusted, governed and high-quality reference and master data across Nordea. Our unit ensures that critical data is designed, managed and continuously improved to support business processes, regulatory requirements and strategic initiatives across the Group.

Our teams work across the full lifecycle - from defining strategy and designing new capabilities, through delivery enablement and lifecycle management, to stable operations and continuous improvement. We embrace modern technologies, including AI-embedded ways of working.

Working closely with business areas, technology teams and data specialists, we help create consistent, reliable and scalable data capabilities that support Nordea’s Tech and Data 2030 strategy incl. AI enablement across the bank´s Business Areas and Support Functions.

We aim to drive change and become the go-to unit for critical data challenges across the bank.

About Enablement Team

Enablement is a Team responsible for contributing to the development and establishment of the new RMD platform, Reference Master Data. RMD will become the enterprise-wide solution for master and reference data and will replace the legacy MDN system.

The team brings together expertise in software development, functional and automated testing, technical analysis and solution design. Working closely with stakeholders, it delivers stable, scalable and sustainable solutions, with a strong focus on automation, AI adoption, quality and continuous improvement. Team members are self-motivated, collaborative and solution-oriented engineers who combine technical expertise with a solid understanding of business needs.

Enablement Team offers an opportunity to work with a modern master data platform, contribute to an important enterprise-wide transformation and be part of a skilled, high-performing team.

What You’ll Be Doing

As Senior Operation Quality Specialist you will be responsible for:


Driving daily operational maintenance and interact with other IT teams to maintain stable operation
Analyzing incidents, finding root causes and proposing solutions
Working with problem tickets
Developing and improving monitoring and tooling
Identifying opportunities for automation and developing new solutions, including AI agents


Key Responsibilities


Oversee the stability, resilience, compliance, and operational performance of the application portfolio, including KPI monitoring.
Collaborate with cross-functional and international teams across multiple locations.
Promote modern engineering practices, automation, AI-driven solutions and continuous improvement.
Support regulatory, risk and data governance requirements within the master data domain.
Take technical ownership of reliable, secure and scalable services, including carrying on deployments to preproduction and production environments.
Define and improve observability and operational practices across logging, monitoring, alerting, SLOs, incident response, resilience, cost control and production readiness.
Translate business and operational needs into sustainable technical solutions, identifying dependencies, risks and opportunities for improvement.
Provide expert guidance to users and engineering teams, resolve complex platform issues and support effective adoption of environments and tools.
Own the implementation and lifecycle of preproduction and production platforms, ensuring stable, high-performing and maintainable solutions.
Drive technical decisions based on evidence, platform knowledge and long-term value, and align choices with architecture, security and governance requirements.
Act as a senior technical partner who shares knowledge, reviews solutions and helps raise engineering quality across the team.


Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you:


Are familiar with DevOps, ITIL, Agile ways of working, preferably Scrum and SAFe, and you have the ability to work effectively in cross-functional delivery teams.
Are proactive, structured, detail-oriented and quality driven.
Have the ability to work independently and as part of a team, cooperating effectively with developers, testers, architects and other stakeholders.
Have strong stakeholder management and communication skills and ability to balance business priorities, operational stability and technology modernization.
Are solution-oriented with a can-do approach when dealing with challenges.
Have excellent troubleshooting skills and can work effectively under pressure.
Are fluent in English (written and spoken)


Your background and skills include:


Strong experience as a Senior Operation Quality Specialist or similar role within complex enterprise environments. Senior-level (6+ years).
Understanding of technologies such as TIBCO EBX, Kafka, Oracle, SQL Server, Linux, APIs and microservices.
Programming skills: Bash, Java
Experience with monitoring tools such as Splunk, AppDynamics, Graphana
Knowledge of ITIL and CI/CD. Strong DevOps and operations expertise across support, maintenance, deployment, automation and platform administration.
Experience with Power BI reporting and data quality monitoring.
Practical knowledge of AI-assisted ways of working.
Advanced troubleshooting skills, including the ability to interpret logs, metrics and traces and resolve complex application and infrastructure issue.
