ChillBase studio is an independent and innovative player in the gaming industry. We’re fueled by our passion for crafting outstanding products.




We are working on OneState — a unique Social RolePlay game — and are currently looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer.




What we want to see:

4+ years of experience as a DevOps Engineer
Strong hands on experience with Kubernetes and deep understanding of its architecture
Experience working with GitOps approaches
Experience administering relational and column oriented databases
Linux administration experience
Experience building and maintaining monitoring systems
Advanced networking knowledge
Experience working with Ansible and Terraform
Willingness to work from one of our offices in Almaty or Limassol




Additionally, it would be great to have:

Knowledge of programming languages for task automation (Bash, Python)




What you will do:

Developing and scaling infrastructure
Improving system reliability and stability
Implementing and evolving automation and delivery tools
Proposing and implementing infrastructure improvements
