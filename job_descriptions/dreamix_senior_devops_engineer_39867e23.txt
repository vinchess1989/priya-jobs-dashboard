Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Dreаmix was founded 20 years ago by passionate IT students, who wanted to create the dreamiest workplace where everyone is heard, works under transparent management, and lives up to their full potential. Now, many years later, we deliver software solutions for renowned companies from Germany, the UK, Switzerland, and Silicon Valley. Dreamix provides quality software services and products for top enterprises around the world through Java and Web technologies.

We believe the employer-employee relationship must be in the form of a partnership, not a transaction. You can be sure that we'll invest as much as we can in your development, but we expect the same commitment to Dreamix. Our culture is defined by our actions not by what we say.

Currently, we are seeking a passionate and experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join our team to support the further development and maintenance of our development environments and development-related IT infrastructure. In this role, you will establish the foundation for efficient software development and seamless collaboration across team boundaries.

Responsibilities:


Administer and maintain Linux-based systems and environments
Operate and support cloud infrastructure in Microsoft Azure, from containerized workloads to Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
Deploy, configure, and maintain web servers and related services within cloud environments
Apply a strong understanding of networking fundamentals, particularly application-layer protocols such as HTTP, SSH, SSL/TLS, TFTP, and related technologies
Design, implement, and maintain automated CI/CD workflows using Jenkins and GitLab
Build and optimize Jenkins and GitLab pipelines to support reliable, efficient software delivery
Provision and manage infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) with Terraform
Automate configuration and system management using Ansible
Develop scripts and automation solutions using Python and Bash
Support the end-to-end software development lifecycle by providing and maintaining effective DevOps processes, tools, and practices
Manage source code and version control using Git, including GitLab and GitHub
Implement and maintain monitoring and observability solutions using Grafana and Prometheus
Collaborate closely with development and other technical teams to improve system reliability, automation, deployment processes, and overall engineering efficiency


Requirements:


Minimum 5+ years of experience as a DevOps Engineer
Strong hands-on experience with Linux administration and troubleshooting
Solid experience with Microsoft Azure, including containerized environments and Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
Good understanding of networking concepts and application-layer protocols, including HTTP, SSH, SSL/TLS, TFTP, and related technologies
Strong experience designing, implementing, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitLab
Practical experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC), particularly Terraform
Hands-on experience with Ansible for configuration management and automation
Proficiency in Python and Bash scripting for automation and operational tasks
Strong knowledge of Git and experience working with GitLab and/or GitHub
Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Grafana and Prometheus
Independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working
Strong communication skills and experience working in agile teams
Proficiency in written and spoken English, German is an advantage.


What you'll get:


A warm and supportive work environment where you can reach your full potential
Flexible working hours that allow you to balance your work and personal life
Unlimited home office to help you stay productive and focused
Opportunities for professional development, including certifications and training
Additional benefits for academic teaching and speaking engagements
Knowledge-sharing sessions where you can learn from our Dreamix team
Team and company-wide events that bring us together
Amazing week long summer office and winter office initiatives
Additional health insurance and dental allowance to ensure your well-being
Multisport card to encourage a healthy and active lifestyle
Office massages to help you relax and unwind
