Who We Are

Applied Materials is the global leader in materials science and engineering solutions that are at the foundation of virtually every new semiconductor chip and advanced display in the world. The equipment that we create and service is essential to advancing AI and accelerating the commercialization of next-generation semiconductor chips. Join us and push the boundaries of materials science and engineering in a company at the foundation of the electronics industry. The work we do together advances the world's technology.

Job Description

We are looking for a Technical Writer in Finland for our Technical Publication team, who will develop clear technical procedures / manuals with concise information/content for our customer and our service teams.

What's in it for you?

You will become part of an international team of Technical Writers, collaborating with colleagues and subject matter experts across different regions to develop high-quality technical documentation. You will work closely with Technical Writers, internal business partners, and tool experts to create clear and effective technical content. In this role, you will contribute to content management initiatives, process improvements, and quality governance activities, while independently managing multiple projects and expanding your expertise in technical communication within a global environment.

Key Responsibilities
Under general supervision, may develop, write, and edit material for system manuals, operation instructions, etc.
Under general supervision, may observe production, developmental and experimental activities to determine operating procedure and detail.
Under general supervision, may interview production and engineering personnel to become familiar with product technologies and production methods - including interacting with engineering, Global Product Support (GPS) and Applied Global Services (AGS) to develop and update documentation.
Under general supervision, may study blueprints, sketches, drawings, part lists, specifications, mock ups and product samples to integrate a delineate technology, operating procedure, production sequence and detail. This also includes control tasks to ensure that technical documentation is reviewed on time.
Under general supervision, may review published materials and recommends revisions or changes in scope, format, content and methods or reproduction and binding.
Drive suppliers to deliver documentation on time.
Requirements
Proficient in technical publication software:
DITA XML: Oxygen Editor OR Similar applications
Content Management System (CMS): SDL Tridion Docs
Graphical: Illustrator, Photoshop, Inkscape, Notepad++
Adobe Suite: Acrobat Pro DC.
MS Office Suite: Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and Outlook
Ability to read electrical schematics and mechanical drawings.
Ability to work in a cross-functional organization and multitask on multiple projects.
Must be a quick learner, self-starter, with strong attention to details and can work with minimal supervision in a fast-paced, high-tech environment.
Requires working both with European, USA and Asia based Teams.
May require 20% international travel.
Demonstrates expanded conceptual knowledge in own discipline and broadens capabilities
Problem Solving
Solves problems in straightforward situations; analyzes possible solutions using technical experience and judgment and precedents
Impact
Impacts quality of own work and the work of others on the team; works within guidelines and policies
Interpersonal Skills
Explains complex information to others in straightforward situations
Excellent interpersonal communication skills
