Job description

Job ID: 6484

 

Would you like to challenge yourself in an inspiring and sometime quite demanding work environment with excellent colleagues? We are now looking for a DevOps Engineer to our Application Maintenance  team to solve incidents, assist in problem management and work with other application management related tasks. 

 

You will work as a part of the Nordea Finance Service Maintenance unit where we support close to 200 applications in 5 legal entities. You will work together with colleagues from other Nordic countries and our extended delivery center in India.  

 

In Nordea Finance, we know our customers’ and partners’ needs are demanding – and we’re deeply committed to meeting them with the financial solutions of tomorrow. We’re creating an agile environment where we’re harnessing the power of technology – one where you can make an impact. So bring your skills, ideas and unique background. With us, you’ll find plenty of opportunities to grow and make your mark on something bigger. 

 

About our team

 

Welcome to the Application Maintenance team within Nordea Finance IT. You’ll join a highly skilled and dedicated team. We add value by securing that our systems are available for our internal users in addition to our external customers and partners. As a IT operations specialist, you’ll play a crucial role in solving incidents and problems within service level agreements, collaborate with other IT & Business subject matter experts across the company.  

Position is located in Helsinki, Finland. 

 

What you’ll be doing:

Handling and coordinating productions issues – solving incidents and problems tickets 

Handling and coordinate production deployments – executing changes tickets 

Handling and coordination ad hoc request from various stakeholders - service requests 

Collaborating with developers and hardware/infrastructure engineers 

Communicating with a variety of other internal service providers 

 

Who you are

 

To succeed in this role, we believe that you:  

Are a true team player with excellent communication skills 

Take ownership and follow-through your tasks 

Enjoy working in a multi-cultural, diverse and dynamic organization 

Eager to learn new things and have an open mind for changes 

Flexible working in different shifts/weekends whenever required 

 

Your background and skills include:

Good knowledge of:

Kubernetes workloads and resource usage / optimization 

CI/CD with Jenkins 

Basic understanding of rest API  and databases 

Monitoring and usage of sysdig 

Pod / Node management and rightsizing 

ITIL Framework hands on experience on Incidents , Change and Problem tickets. 

Nice to have:

Java error log 

Cloud – ESP 

FTP/SFTP Protocols 

Ninjago deployments 

Weblogic server  

Windows and linux OS 

Batch schedular - Windows/UC4  

Tools: Splunk, MQ, App-dynamics, OEM

 

What we offer

 

Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us in how we work and how we make decisions – and that we imagine you share with us.  

 

People come here when they want to get somewhere. For some, it’s to take their career to the next level. For others, it’s to break new ground within their area of expertise – in other words, with us, you will always move forward.

 

A culture that fosters performance and growth in one of the largest Nordic banks, offering various opportunities to evolve, develop and learn from brilliant colleagues with diverse backgrounds in a vibrant working environment.

 

Hybrid working model – we believe in the value of bringing people together and at the same time we embrace the freedom of flexibility.

 

Diversity and inclusion are a natural part of our daily work. We know that an inclusive workplace is a sustainable one. We genuinely believe that our diverse backgrounds, experiences, characteristics and traits make us stronger together. Every day we strive to find new ways to improve diversity and inclusion within our community e.g. we have signed the European Diversity Charters in the countries where we operate to show our commitment and engage with others to continue learning and improving.

 

If this sounds like you, get in touch!

 

Next steps

 

Submit your application no later than 02/12/2026. For more information, you’re welcome to contact Jan-Anders Lindblad at anders.lindblad@nordea.com. Please note that we will proceed with potential candidates already during the application period.

 

If you have any questions about recruitment process, please contact our recruiter Annina Perttu at annina.perttu@consult.nordea.com.
