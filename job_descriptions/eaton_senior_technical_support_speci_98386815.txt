Eaton's new campus for its critical power systems in Vantaa, Finland, was completed in November 2023. The 16,500 m² site houses research and development, manufacturing, warehousing, sales and service under one roof, which will create plenty of additional jobs.
 
As one of the world’s largest manufacturers of three-phase uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), Eaton’s expansion in this area is driven by strong business growth and the demand for systems that ensure business continuity. Situated in a prime location next to the Helsinki airport, the Vantaa facility serves as Eaton’s Critical Power Solutions headquarters and a center of excellence for data centers.
 
Join Eaton and help us provide energy-efficient solutions that have a real impact on the environment. We make what matters work. Find out more on https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=baa_aiJ4L7E  
What you’ll do:

In this role, you will act as a technical expert on Eaton EMEA 3ph UPS products to enable and support smooth operations of Eaton Field Service Engineers (FSEs) and partners (ASPs) across EMEA region. You will also provide service trainings for Field Service Engineers while creating and maintaining service documentation.

Diagnose and resolve technical issues with 3-phase UPS systems.
Provide first class technical support to local and regional field service engineers in their daily activities
Manage and lead troubleshooting and take corrective actions in challenging field incidents
Report to Quality organization of field findings
Run service trainings for FSEs and ASPs, including onsite, remote and E Learning trainings
Assist FSEs and customers with the installation, configuration and start-up of UPS systems
Improve the content and quality of service trainings
Create and maintain a comprehensive service knowledge base, technical documentation, field service bulletins and service guides
Participate new product development projects as a voice of customer and provide field service perspective
Give service related input to other stakeholders, for instance Engineering and Sales Support
Contribute to the zero-incident culture supporting safety of field service engineers through instructions and training
Coordinate with the factory teams located in other countries for technical and product support
Qualifications:
University degree
Solid experience in Electrical and/or Electronic industry
Skills:
Technical knowledge on power electronics and/or three phase electrical systems and solutions. Experience on UPS technologies. Basic computer and IT skills. 
Strong work ethic, Communication and interpersonal skills, Problem-Solving Skills, Teamwork, Adaptability
Positive attitude in customer service

Excellent working environment - safety and ethic are really important for us
