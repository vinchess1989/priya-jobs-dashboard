Job Description

The Developer Experience tribe offers an exciting opportunity to improve the productivity and satisfaction of developers here at Danske Bank.

As s Senior IT Platform Engineer, you will ensure the availability, stability, security, and integrity of the company’s Platform. Furthermore, you will monitor, maintain and evolve the platform and troubleshoot problems that may arise.

Build & Release Engineering provides Standard, Compliant, centrally managed and Supported CI/CD pipelines for Cloud and Mobile Development. We manage and support standard CI/CD and Repository tool chain.

Joining us means being a part of the biggest cloud migration journey in the bank, taking ownership of engineering tools and providing users a toolset to improve their development productivity.

You will:


Design reusable GitHub workflows and standardised, centrally managed CI/CD pipelines, including deployment of software products to AWS public cloud
Develop GitHub Actions using JavaScript and composite actions
Migrate Azure DevOps standard templates to reusable GitHub workflows
Build automation and self-service capabilities for our solutions
Manage and support EKS namespaces and clusters
Contribute to documentation and user guides for CI/CD solutions
Manage and support GitHub, Azure DevOps, Artifactory


About you:


Hands-on experience with GitHub Actions with GitHub Enterprise Cloud
Creating and managing custom actions (composite, JavaScript)
Proven experience working with AWS public cloud resources and services
Able to work independently, take ownership, and collaborate across development and security teams
Proficiency with observability platforms such as Grafana and Elastic
Proficiency with containerization and orchestration (OpenShift, K8s)
Proficient in at least one programming language (C#, Python, etc.)


We offer:

Monthly salary range from 4960 EUR to 7440 EUR gross (based on your competencies relevant for the job).

Additionally, each Danske Bank employee receives employee benefits package which includes:


Growth opportunities: professional & supportive team, e-learnings, numerous development programs; (incl. professional certificates); 100+ professions for internal mobility opportunities.
Health & Well-being: a diverse, inclusive, work & life balance work environment; additional health insurance; mental well-being practices; partial psychologist counselling compensation; silence and sleep zones at the office; game rooms.
Hybrid working conditions: Work from home up to two days a week; home office budget (after the probation period); modern Danske Campus workplace developed with anthropologist for the best employee experience.
Additional days of leave: for rest, health, volunteering, exams in higher education institutions, and other important activities. Moreover, for seniority with Danske Bank.
Monetary compensation package: accidents & critical diseases insurance; financial support in case of unfortunate events, travel insurance; IIIrd Pillar Pension Fund contribution.
