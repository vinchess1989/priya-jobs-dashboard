Fintech Infrastructure




Trust in SODA are partnered with a rapidly growing European fintech redefining how investment products are launched across the continent.




The business has built an API-first investment infrastructure platform that enables banks, fintechs and wealth managers to offer trading and investing products far faster and more efficiently than traditional brokerage providers.




Their technology simplifies the complexity of capital markets and regulatory infrastructure, helping partners launch modern investment experiences in weeks instead of months.




Now operating with the backing of a major financial institution while maintaining a strong engineering-led startup culture, they are continuing to scale their cloud and platform engineering capabilities across Germany.




This is an opportunity to join a highly collaborative DevOps / Platform Engineering team where you will have genuine ownership over cloud infrastructure, platform reliability, security and developer enablement in a modern AWS-native environment.




Why this opportunity stands out

Modern engineering environment with strong technical leadership
API-first fintech platform used by leading financial organisations across Europe
Strong product and engineering culture with real autonomy and ownership
Hybrid working model with flexibility across Germany
Backed by significant financial stability while still operating with startup agility
Excellent progression opportunities as the engineering organisation scales further
Collaborative and international team with English as the working language
Meaningful engineering challenges across scalability, compliance, cloud security and platform reliability




What you’ll be working on

Building and evolving secure cloud infrastructure on AWS
Managing and improving Kubernetes/EKS environments
Developing and maintaining Infrastructure-as-Code using Terraform/OpenTofu
Enhancing CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
Improving platform observability, monitoring and reliability
Supporting engineering teams with scalable internal tooling and developer platforms
Driving cloud security, IAM best practices and compliance standards within a regulated fintech environment
Contributing to architectural decisions and long-term platform strategy

Experience within fintech, SaaS, regulated environments or fast-growth startups is highly beneficial
Comfortable working cross-functionally within lean engineering teams
Engineers who enjoy ownership, autonomy and solving complex infrastructure challenges
