Job posting end date: 12/03/2026

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to join our Digital Infrastructure and Cybersecurity team in the Minerals business area. In this role, you will help design, implement, and maintain our cloud and edge infrastructure, supporting scalable, reliable, secure, and cost-efficient solutions and successful cloud adoption and optimization.

You will report to the Director, Digital Infrastructure, and the role location is Espoo or Tampere, Finland, with a hybrid working model available.

Team you will belong to

You will join the Digital Infrastructure and Cybersecurity team, which is based in Espoo and focuses on cloud and edge infrastructure.

You will collaborate with stakeholders across development, security, operations, and finance to support cloud adoption and optimization.

What You'll Do



Develop cloud architectures with a focus on scalability, reliability, security, and cost efficiency.
Build and configure cloud resources using Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and automate builds, testing, deployments, and workflows through continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.
Monitor performance, uptime, and resource utilization, troubleshoot issues, and optimize production cloud workloads.
Implement identity and access management (IAM) controls and support audits, compliance, and vulnerability management.
Track cloud spending, right-size resources, and recommend architectures that reduce costs while meeting solution needs.
Build and operate containerized solutions using Docker and Kubernetes.



What You'll Bring



A degree in a field such as Information Technology.
Experience in cloud computing or related fields, including hands-on experience with at least one major cloud provider such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and experience supporting production cloud environments.
Experience administering Linux and Windows systems, with an understanding of operating systems and databases.
Experience with IaC technologies such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Azure Resource Manager (ARM), along with exposure to CI/CD pipelines and DevOps tools.
Understanding of cloud infrastructure, automation, deployment, containers, orchestration, and the cost impact of cloud design decisions.
Ability to collaborate across development, security, operations, and finance, propose cloud-based solutions to business challenges, and align those solutions with business goals.
Interest in AI-assisted development and operations, together with strong self-leadership skills.
