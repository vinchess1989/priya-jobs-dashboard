Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

SOGECLAIR Engineering GmbH in Germany is successfully active in the aerospace sector as a competent engineering service provider of the international SOGECLAIR Group.


We accompany well-known companies in the development, design and implementation of future-oriented projects at a high technical level. A special focus is on aerospace technology with close cooperation to various large companies in the industry. As part of an established network within the SOGECLAIR Group, we and our partners offer the best possible service in all aspects of product development, from concept to series production. Within this framework, we work on projects on a national and international level with our dedicated employees.


Our expertise: aerostructure and systems installation, aircraft interiors, industrialization, configuration management, management and operations consulting, product and process performance, digital transformation, operational excellence, environmental transition.


Our values:

We're expanding and currently looking for a Confirmed Configuration Manager (m/f/d) to join and strengthen our team in Hamburg, Germany

In this role, you will manage and coordinate configuration and engineering change activities within a highly technical and complex aerospace environment.

As a key point of contact for configuration management, you will work closely with engineering, manufacturing, quality, program management, suppliers, and customers to ensure configuration consistency, traceability, and the efficient implementation of technical changes throughout the product lifecycle.

Your main tasks


Manage configuration management activities throughout the product lifecycle
Coordinate engineering changes from initiation through assessment, approval, implementation, and closure
Ensure the completeness, quality, consistency, and traceability of configuration and change documentation
Prepare and coordinate changes for Change Control Boards (CCB) and other decision-making forums
Consolidate technical, industrial, cost, planning, and customer inputs related to engineering changes
Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders including engineering, manufacturing, quality, program management, suppliers, and customers
Monitor modification status, configuration baselines, applicability, and associated technical documentation
Support aircraft modification, customization, production, and delivery configuration activities
Identify configuration risks, inconsistencies, and dependencies and drive corrective actions
Monitor performance through relevant KPIs such as On-Time Delivery, Right First Time, quality, backlog, and efficiency
Contribute to continuous improvement of configuration and change-management processes




Requirements


5 or more years of relevant experience in configuration management, change management, engineering, or a comparable aerospace environment
Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Industrial Engineering or similar
Proficient with ICCACP and Google Workspace
Knowledge of the Airbus Single Aisle (SA) / A320 Family is required, as the role supports projects directly related to these aircraft programs
Strong knowledge of Configuration Management and Engineering Change Management processes
Experience working with complex technical products and multidisciplinary engineering environments
Strong understanding of product lifecycle, technical documentation, configuration baselines, and modification management
High level of autonomy and ability to independently steer complex configuration topics
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities
Process-oriented mindset with the ability to follow established processes while proactively identifying opportunities for improvement
Strong stakeholder-management and coordination skills
Experience in airframe configuration management
Experience with aircraft modifications and customization
Experience with Change Control Boards
Experience with modification implementation and closure processes
Experience with PLM, PDM, configuration-management tools, or configuration databases
Experience within Airbus or comparable commercial aircraft programs
Experience coordinating engineering, manufacturing, quality, suppliers, and customer stakeholders
Experience with KPI monitoring, risk management, workload planning, and performance reporting
English required, German a plus
