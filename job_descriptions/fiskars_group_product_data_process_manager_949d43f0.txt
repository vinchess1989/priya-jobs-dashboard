Product Data Process Manager

OUR ROLE

We are looking for a Product Data Process Manager to join our BA Fiskars Product organization. In this role, you will shape how product information is created, managed and shared throughout the product lifecycle. By establishing clear processes, responsibilities and data standards, you will help ensure high-quality product data, more efficient ways of working and successful product launches across our global organization.

You will work closely with functions such as Product, IT, Master Data Management, Sales and R&D to build clarity around product-data processes, responsibilities and system interactions. Your mission is to help the organization understand what information is needed, when it is needed, who is responsible for it, and how it moves across PLM, SAP and other business systems.

This position is based in Espoo, Finland, reports to the Strategic Value Creation & Delivery Lead, and is offered on a full-time hybrid basis.

What You Will Do At Fiskars Group

As Product Data Process Manager, you will play a key role in ensuring that product data is created, managed and utilized effectively throughout the new product development and launch process.


Lead the implementation and adoption of OnePLM across the organization, ensuring agreed milestones are delivered on time and within scope.
Drive the development and adoption of a harmonized end-to-end product data process covering data creation, enrichment, approval, transfer, publication and consumption throughout the product lifecycle.
Coordinate and drive product-data readiness across system configuration, integrations, testing, deployment activities and launch preparations, working closely with business stakeholders and IT.
Lead role-based training, communication and adoption activities to ensure successful implementation of agreed processes and systems across functions and locations.
Monitor and improve product-data quality, process compliance and user adoption, ensuring product information is complete, accurate, timely and fit for downstream users and channels.
Drive cross-functional governance and continuous improvement of product-data related processes across OnePLM, SAP and related systems, maintaining a prioritized roadmap and tracking business benefits.



Who You Are

We're looking for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complexity and connecting people, processes and technology.


Relevant university degree in business, engineering, technology, supply chain, product development or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
Demonstrated experience working with product data, master data or product lifecycle processes in a complex, cross-functional and international environment. Experience leading process improvement, change adoption and PLM/ERP-related initiatives is highly valued.
Working knowledge of product lifecycle and product-data flows across PLM, ERP and related enterprise systems. Experience with PLM implementation, adoption and process development is strongly preferred.
Experience with Windchill, FlexPLM and SAP is considered an advantage.
Strong process-development capability, including process mapping, harmonisation, requirements definition and continuous improvement.
Experience translating business needs into functional requirements and working effectively with IT and technical specialists
Proven change-management and adoption skills, including stakeholder engagement, training design, communications and user support
Ability to lead through influence, facilitate decisions and align stakeholders with different functional, geographical and operational perspectives
Proven ability to design and execute role-based training, adoption, communications and hypercare for diverse global user groups.
Structured, hands-on and analytical working style, with strong follow-through and the ability to manage detail without losing sight of the overall objective
Strong written and verbal communication, facilitation and presentation skills
Experience from consumer durables or adjacent industries is highly qualifying
Ability to distinguish business-process, data-governance, system-configuration and integration issues and mobilise the right owners to resolve them.
Fluency in written and spoken English.
Ability to facilitate global workshops, training and decision forums in English across business and technical audiences.

Fiskars Group (FSKRS, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the global home of design-driven brands for indoor and outdoor living.
