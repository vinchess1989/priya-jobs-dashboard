Job description

atNorth is a leading Nordic data center company providing sustainable, high-performance computing and colocation solutions across Iceland, Sweden, Finland and Denmark.

We design mission-critical ecosystems powered by 100% renewable energy and circular economy principles, staying ahead through a relentless focus on reliability, security, and performance.

We believe world-class technology requires a world-class culture. Our people-first mentality ensures that technical excellence and human wellbeing go hand in hand. Built on collaboration, ownership, and trust, our environment empowers individuals to take responsibility, grow their expertise, and make a real impact.

By joining our international team, you play a direct role in shaping the future of sustainable digital infrastructure while supporting the communities and industries we serve.

Project Engineer (Commercial Manager) at atNorth

Are you a commercially driven professional with expertise in financial management, procurement, and contract negotiation?

atNorth is looking for a Project Engineer/Commercial Manager to oversee and optimize the financial and contractual aspects of our development projects.

You will ensure financial efficiency, cost control, and contractual compliance across our rapidly expanding infrastructure.

This role offers a unique opportunity to shape commercial processes in a high-growth, international environment while working on innovative and sustainable development projects.

Key responsibilities:

Oversee budget preparation and financial oversight for data center infrastructure projects.
Support with contract negotiations and ensure compliance across all development phases.
Lead claims management, variations, and the processing of payment applications.
Collaborate closely with procurement, project management, and construction teams.
Proactively identify financial risks and implement robust mitigation strategies.
Monitor project costs and supplier agreements to drive cost optimization.
Provide commercial leadership and financial discipline to maximize project profitability.
Drive commercial discussions and ensure all agreements align with atNorth's strategic goals.

Your profile:

Master's or Bachelor's degree in Finance, Engineering, Business, or a related field.
5+ years of experience in commercial management, preferably in construction or infrastructure; large scale projects.
Strong expertise in budget preparation, cost control, and procurement processes with projects.
Proven track record in managing complex contracts, claims, and variations.
Analytical and meticulous mindset with an independent approach to problem-solving.
Excellent communication and negotiation skills with a proactive, diplomatic style.
Resilience under pressure and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced scale-up environment.
Passion for both high-level financial strategy and hands-on project execution.
Fluent written and spoken English, Finnish skills being very much appreciated.
Background check is conducted to all our employees.

What we offer:

Weekly office breakfast on Wednesdays and daily snacks at the office
Regular team events to stay connected and have fun
Lunch and recreational benefits
Extensive occupational health care
Accident insurance covering your free time
Holiday accrual of 2,5 days from the beginning of tenure
Newest model of Iphone or Samsung for work phone
Supportive and friendly colleagues across international teams
A chance to contribute to something meaningful
Opportunities for personal and professional growth within the organisation

We believe that sustainable success starts with the wellbeing of our people. For us, an open, transparent, and inclusive culture is more than a priority, it is the core of how we lead and innovate.

We don't just talk about culture; we nurture it. Through regular one-to-one engagements and monthly wellbeing surveys, we listen carefully to our teams, ensuring that every voice is heard and every opportunity for improvement is embraced.

We recognize that innovation and collaboration thrive where people feel truly connected, which is why we come together each week to share ideas, strengthen relationships, and nurture a sense of belonging, sometimes over a simple breakfast and good conversation.

At atNorth, we are building a community where you can do your best work and shape a sustainable future together.
