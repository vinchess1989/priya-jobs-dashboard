About u-blox
u-blox is a global leader in automotive, industrial, and consumer markets, driving innovation through our cutting-edge positioning and short-range communication technologies. We are the pioneers behind high-precision technologies, providing smart and reliable solutions that enable people, vehicles, and machines to determine their precise position and communicate wirelessly. With headquarters in Thalwil, Switzerland, and offices across Europe, Asia, and the USA, we are making a global impact.

Job Description

We are looking for a Test Engineer for product testing. Test engineer is responsible of developing, maintaining, and executing the tests for the new HW and FW releases, managing and controlling test activities, as well as making sure that necessary equipment and processes are in order. Test Engineer will also take care of required test configurations, setups etc. As part of a cross-functional Scrum team, the Test Engineer will be closely collaborating with other product team members as well as with the Verification and Validation teams.
The role focuses on solutions related to Positioning products, ensuring reliable and resilient position and timing performance in challenging environments.
The role is available in Tampere.

Your Responsibilities
Test support tasks for product development in agile teams
Contribute to the creation of custom use cases together with the product development team
Support possible needs in-field testing
Test GNSS receivers
Pre-analysis of test results and creation of product verification and performance overview
Test automation script development and update based on product requirements
Product team specific test environment update and maintenance
Active participation in test process improvements

Your Accountabilities
Create test plans according to the product development phase
Create full-automated test asset for positioning and timing products
Perform planned test activities for HW and FW releases and provide results at the right time
Communication towards the test manager and the product development team
Test setup related problem solving
Owner and coordinator of test review and issue topics

Your Competencies
Behavioral/Soft skills
Ability to work and collaborate as a member of bigger projects and distributed teams.
Good communication skills
A proactive way of working
Continuous learning mindset
Tester mindset (problem-oriented, questioning, curiosity)
Required Technical/Hard Skills
BSc or higher in SW engineering, electrical engineering, computer science or equivalent.
Embedded system architecture
Scripting skills (Python/Robot Framework)
Experience of using AI tools
Bonus points: Technical/Hard skills
Software, Hardware, and/or Embedded System testing experience in agile teams
Good background and in-depth understanding of GNSS
Requirement based testing
SW development skills
Experience with the Atlassian Toolstack is a benefit
Experience with Github, Linux and test/measurement environments is a plus
Experience in embedded systems in e.g. industrial, positioning or automotive products

A multicultural and international company with over 60 different nationalities
Project-based activities working with colleagues distributed across the globe
A start-up and innovation mindset while in the process of scaling-up processes and efficiencies
Hybrid working model & flexible working hours
A strong learning environment
Modern office in Hervanta, Tampere
Sport activities and Team events
