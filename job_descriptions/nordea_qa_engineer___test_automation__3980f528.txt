Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

We are looking for a test automation engineers to join our Martech & Sales Technology Department in Helsinki. If you have several years of experience in test automation frameworks and want to ensure correctness, compliance, and stability of NBA (next best action, an AI-driven decision-making strategy to identify the single most relevant interaction to take with a customer real-time) outcomes across releases, including data flows across Hadoop/Cloudera, Google Cloud Platform, and Pega CDH / Infinity, then read on!

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We’re not just a bank; we’re a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

Meet the core Next Best Action (NBA) team. Our mission is to build, govern and scale Nordea’s NBA capability as a Nordic-wide decisioning foundation, enabling relevant, data-driven and personalized customer interactions across markets, business areas and channels. We own the common decisioning frameworks, data and intelligence foundations, governance and platform enablement.

What You’ll Be Doing

In this role, your main responsibilities will be:


Validate decision logic outcomes and eligibility behaviour
Test data integration and model usage across on-prem (Hadoop/Cloudera), GCP/Snowflake and Pega CDH / Infinity
Support system testing and SIT across distributed data pipelines and decisioning platforms
Validate explainability, auditability, and regulatory requirements
Ensure correctness and quality of data flows from Hadoop/Cloudera to GCP/Snowflake and into Pega decisioning platform
Support MVP go-live and further releases
Ensure compliance with Nordea Quality Standards


Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you have:


Engineering Mindset
Attention to Detail
Curiosity and Continuous Learning
Pragmatism and Problem-Solving
Experience in Big Data Technologies: strong knowledge of Hadoop ecosystem (Cloudera/MapR) including streaming technologies


Your background and skills include:


Experience with leading quality assurance and test automation
Hands-on experience with Robot Framework, Pytest, or similar
Hands-on experience in Python, Bash, SQL, Java
Practical experience with databases such as Hadoop HDFS, Apache Kafka, Apache Hive
Experience with GCP CI/CD, Jenkins, JIRA, Bitbucket, Git, Docker
Proven ability to analyze complex systems and identify quality risks
Hands-on experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) or/and Snowflake, including data processing and storage services
Experience validating data pipelines and large-scale data processing systems across on-prem and cloud environments
Experience in testing data integration or decisioning outputs in Pega (CDH / Infinity) or similar systems is an advantage
Proficiency in managing automated functional and non-functional testing on large-scale data infrastructure


It would be ideal if you also have knowledge with:


Experience with Atlassian Suite (Confluence, JIRA)
Solid understanding of financial service environments
ISTQB Foundation Certificate
Any Pega Certifications
