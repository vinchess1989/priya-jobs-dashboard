Hae paikkaa

I’m Robert Lemming, Head of ACE Devops and Infra team, and I’m searching for a DevOps Engineer  ready to grow with us. Telia ACE is a trusted, market-leading Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) platform that enables Telia’s B2B customers to deliver outstanding customer service across channels and devices. With over 25 years of experience, Telia ACE supports organizations ranging from small businesses to multinational corporations, municipalities, and government agencies. Our platform helps customers succeed in their communication efforts - whether in finance, insurance, healthcare, or other vital sectors - by offering smart solutions for online self-service, omnichannel support, and secure, scalable infrastructure. At Telia ACE, we build systems that are not only technically robust and secure but also serve important societal functions.
 

Are you a DevOps Engineer passionate about building, operating, and developing a robust cloud environments for critical B2B IT- Services? Do you thrive in an agile setup, driving innovation and reliability in a high-performing SRE and Devops team. If so, this could be the perfect opportunity for you to make a lasting impact within a collaborative environment of colleagues.

 What you'll do

You'll help build, operate, and continuously improve the cloud platforms and delivery pipelines behind Telia ACE's critical communication services.

• Develop and operate secure, scalable cloud infrastructure across multiple platforms and vendors
• Create and maintain CI/CD pipelines using modern automation tools such as GitHub Actions
• Implement Infrastructure as Code solutions and drive automation across platform operations
• Develop solutions that improve reliability, efficiency, and operational excellence
• Enable observability through monitoring, alerting, logging, tracing, and dashboarding capabilities
• Collaborate closely with engineers, architects, and product owners across geographically distributed teams

 

What will help you thrive in this role

You are a collaborative problem-solver who enjoys automating, improving reliability, and working with modern cloud technologies.

• Proven experience with Linux, networking, and cloud infrastructure
• Strong knowledge of Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, Ansible, or Pulumi
• Experience with automation and scripting using Python, Go, or Bash
• Hands-on experience with containers and orchestration platforms, including Kubernetes and Podman
• Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and Git-based workflows
• Knowledge of observability, monitoring, logging, tracing, and incident management practices
• Familiarity with GitHub, GitOps, ArgoCD, cloud-native architectures, and platform engineering approaches is a plus
• Ability to document technical solutions clearly and communicate fluently in English
