Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About The Role

Sulzer is transforming its global product configuration system landscape and is looking for Variant Configuration Transformation Lead to drive this change to global Variant Configuration models.

In this senior expert role, you will lead the SAP CPQ implementation related changes to models, own the transition from classic SAP Variant Configuration (LO VC) to S/4HANA Advanced Variant Configuration (AVC), and define the next generation of configuration models, standards, and best practices. The position can be based at any of Sulzer's locations in Finland.

This is a hands on transformation and architecture role with global impact across sales, engineering, manufacturing, and aftermarkets.

Your Responsibilities


Lead the SAP CPQ implementation related changes to Variant Configuration models
Lead the migration and redesign of SAP LO‑VC models to S/4HANA AVC, including migration methodology, scoping, validation, and end‑to‑end testing
Design and implement next‑generation configuration models leveraging advanced AVC capabilities
Ensure seamless integration of configuration models with: SAP S/4HANA, SAP Commerce, Teamcenter PLM
Establish global configuration modeling standards, governance, and best practices
Train, coach, and mentor configuration modelers in best‑practice modeling approaches


What You Bring


Deep, hands‑on experience with SAP Variant Configuration (LO‑VC and AVC) and SAP CPQ
Experience migrating and redesigning configuration models from SAP ECC LO-VC to S/4HANA AVC
Strong understanding of CTO and ETO product configuration scenarios
Experience designing global configuration models in global, cross‑functional environments
Experience with Teamcenter Product Configurator, modular product structures, and multi‑BOM scenarios is a strong advantage
Ability to work at architecture and transformation level, while remaining hands‑on
Fluent English communication skills


Important Note

This role is intended for senior SAP configuration experts and architects.

It is not a general SAP consultant or junior configuration modeling position.

The salary for this position ranges from 6.200 EUR to 7.000 EUR per month. The final salary will be determined based on the selected candidate’s experience and qualifications. The salary range reflects the planned level of responsibility associated with the role.
