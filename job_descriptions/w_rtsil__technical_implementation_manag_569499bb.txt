Wärtsilä is an innovative technology company known for being a forerunner in the Marine and Energy markets. Every day, we – Wärtsilians - put our hearts and minds into turning Wärtsilä's purpose of enabling sustainable societies through innovation in technology and services into reality. Wärtsilä Information Management (IM) is a global organization of more than 350 IT professionals who work together to deliver best-in-class IT services in Wärtsilä. IM is responsible for operating and developing the ICT landscape, and in addition is a strategic partner to our businesses, driving innovation to accelerate growth of digital solutions.

We want to transition the world towards a decarbonised and sustainable future. By pushing the boundaries of engineering and technology, we can make it work. Together, we can create new business opportunities and more sustainable future that we can all be proud of. Want to join the ride?

We are looking for a Technical Implementation Manager with a main responsibility for planning and delivering strategic finance technology projects across Wärtsilä’s global finance landscape. The preferred location for this position is Helsinki, Finland, but also Vaasa or Turku in Finland may also be negotiable. We offer the possibility of hybrid work.

Your Role And Responsibilities

Your journey at Wärtsilä will begin with strategic finance projects, where you will take a key role as the Technical Implementation Manager. The role focuses on technical delivery leadership, deployment readiness, testing governance, cutover management and transition to operations, rather than hands-on solution architecture or people management. In this position, you will ensure the technical project streams are delivered on time, within budget and according to agreed business outcomes. You will also support change management, user adoption and the successful transition of new solutions into business operations.

You will coordinate architects, developers, testing teams, service teams and vendors to ensure successful implementation, testing, deployment and transition to operations, driving complex initiatives from solution design through go-live and operational handover.

Your work will focus on these responsibilities:


Lead finance projects’ technical workstreams in collaboration with service managers, technical experts and global program management
Manage project IT scope, timelines, budgets, resources, risks, issues and dependencies
Translate approved business requirements and solution designs into executable implementation roadmaps
Coordinate solution design, testing, deployment and transition into operations
Drive technical solution realization, including build, configuration, development, integration and deployment activities
Coordinate and oversee system, integration, regression and user acceptance testing together with development teams and business stakeholders
Coordinate deployment planning, cutover preparation and go-live readiness assessments
Coordinate operational readiness together with Service Managers, ensuring support teams, documentation, monitoring and service management processes are in place before go-live
Support the transition from project delivery into operations, ensuring knowledge transfer and sustainable support models
Collaborate with Finance stakeholders, Solution Managers, Solution Architects, Service Owners, Wärtsilä IT teams, business units and external vendors
Lead the IT delivery in the projects, prepare status reports, steering committee materials, business cases and project gate reviews
Ensure compliance with project governance, internal controls, cybersecurity, data privacy and enterprise architecture requirements
Ensure technical quality and solution integrity in line with architecture, security and operational standards
Drive continuous improvement of implementation methodologies and delivery practices
Support change management, communication, training and user adoption activities


To be able to succeed in the position, we expect you to have:


Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Information Technology, Business, Finance, Project Management or an equivalent field
At least 8 years of experience leading IT projects, preferably in the finance area
Proven experience in managing complex, cross-functional projects across multiple countries and stakeholder groups
Experience in project management related to finance processes, enterprise applications and integrations
Extensive experience leading SAP, ERP or enterprise application implementations
Strong understanding of system integration, software development lifecycle (SDLC), testing, release management and deployment processes
Experience working with finance systems such as SAP S/4HANA, SAP Finance, treasury, tax, invoicing, procurement or similar solutions
Strong project governance, planning, financial management and reporting skills
Familiarity with Agile, Waterfall or hybrid delivery methodologies
Experience in handling compliance, internal controls, data privacy and cybersecurity topics
Experience in managing external vendors and delivery partners
Ability to balance business requirements, technical constraints, timelines and operational readiness
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with fluent written and spoken English
