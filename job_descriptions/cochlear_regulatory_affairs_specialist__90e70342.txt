Help Bring Life-Changing Medical Devices to Patients

At Cochlear, our mission is to help people hear and be heard. Regulatory Affairs plays an essential role in ensuring our innovative medical device solutions meet regulatory requirements and can reach the people who need them.

We're looking for a Regulatory Affairs Specialist EMEA to join our Regulatory Affairs Shared Services team and support regulatory activities across the EMEA region. This is a fully remote role, open to candidates based anywhere in the European Union (EU).

About the Role

Working closely with EMEA Regulatory Affairs Managers, Regulatory Operations, regional teams, distributors, and other internal and external stakeholders, you'll help ensure regulatory registrations, assessments, intelligence, data, and reporting activities are delivered consistently, on time, and in compliance with applicable requirements.

You'll support product registrations and launches, assess regulatory requirements and product changes, maintain regulatory databases and reporting, and act as a regulatory point of contact for an assigned region or focus area.

What You'll Do

Prepare regulatory registration packages for EMEA markets, coordinating dossier readiness in line with local requirements and product launch timelines
Monitor, assess, and communicate regulatory intelligence and contribute to regulatory strategies for new and changed products
Coordinate and perform regulatory assessments of product, labelling, manufacturing, and other changes, communicating required actions to stakeholders
Maintain regulatory databases, trackers, EUDAMED records, and reports to ensure accurate and timely regulatory information
Support product launches and provide regulatory guidance to internal and external stakeholders on vigilance, tenders, reimbursement, marketing materials, and economic operator compliance
Drive continuous improvement of Regulatory Affairs processes, procedures, templates, systems, and ways of working

What You'll Bring

Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant field such as science, biomedical sciences, engineering, or a related discipline
2-3+ years of professional experience in Regulatory Affairs within medical devices or pharmaceuticals
Good working knowledge of medical device regulations, particularly the EU MDR, and regulatory requirements across EMEA markets
Experience preparing regulatory submissions or registrations and/or conducting regulatory assessments
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and work independently
Excellent communication, stakeholder management, analytical, and problem-solving skills
Excellent English skills, both written and spoken

Preferred

Experience with EUDAMED, regulatory databases, data quality management, and product registrations across EMEA markets
Experience in regulatory intelligence, product change assessments, vigilance/reportability, or regulatory strategy
Experience coordinating multiple projects, with an interest in project, portfolio, or process management
Experience with reporting and dashboarding tools such as Excel or Power BI, and systems such as Confluence, Jira, Windchill, or SAP
Knowledge of ISO 13485, quality management systems, and other regulated areas such as REACH, RoHS, WEEE, RED, batteries, or toys
An additional language spoken within the EMEA region

Why Cochlear?

Make a real difference in the lives of people with hearing loss

Competitive salary, bonus scheme, and annual salary reviews
30 days' annual leave plus additional days off on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve
Flexible working hours and fully remote working
Employee share plan, Corporate Benefits, childcare support, and sabbatical options
Ongoing learning and development opportunities
Modern office environment, free parking, and excellent transport links
