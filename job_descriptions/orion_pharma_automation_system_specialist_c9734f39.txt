Job Description

We are looking for a proactive and solution-oriented Automation Systems Specialist to strengthen Fermion’s automation and digitalisation capabilities. This is a permanent position, and the preferred location can be Oulu, Hanko or a hybrid remote working arrangement, depending on the successful candidate’s location.

Your role

Fermion’s manufacturing sites rely extensively on automation, recipe management and reporting systems that play a critical role in pharmaceutical API manufacturing. As digitalisation continues and investment activity grows, we are looking for a specialist to develop and maintain production-critical software, automation and OT systems across Fermion.

In this role, you will work closely with Production, Automation, IT and external partners. You will have the opportunity to contribute to both the enhancement of existing systems and the design of future solutions. The position offers a unique opportunity to make a significant impact on Fermion’s production systems, digitalisation initiatives and the harmonisation of manufacturing operations across the company.

Responsibilities


Following an onboarding period, act as the key user for the recipe and batch record systems (MES) across Fermion.
Be responsible for system functionality, maintenance, development and compliance.
Develop and harmonise OT-related systems across Fermion sites in Oulu, Hanko and Espoo.
Participate in the development of cybersecurity, virtualisation and maintenance practices within IT/OT environments.
Develop recipe management and reporting systems in collaboration with Production and Automation teams.
Contribute to digitalisation projects and technology renewal initiatives within manufacturing operations.
Ensure long-term system performance, availability and reliability.
Define and coordinate system changes together with suppliers, partners and internal stakeholders.
Participate in computerised system validation activities and the continuous development of a GMP-compliant operating environment.


What You Can Expect From Us

We offer you the opportunity to work in an expert role with a significant impact on the future of Fermion’s production systems and digitalisation. You will take on a responsible and versatile position in a growing environment where investment and development projects continue at a strong pace. Our working culture is open, supportive and collaborative. You will work with a broad network of experts and partners while continuously developing your own expertise in modern manufacturing and automation systems.

At Orion, your work creates true impact and well-being for our customers, patients and society at large. Our culture of friendliness, respect, mutual appreciation and diversity creates a safe working environment where you can strive for excellence. We offer a wealth of career paths and development opportunities that support the development of innovative solutions and improving the quality of life.

Please visit our website to find further information about our values and Orion as an employer https://www.orion.fi/en/careers/orion-as-an-employer/ .

What are we looking for?

To Succeed In This Position, You Have


A relevant technical degree (BSc, MSc or equivalent in Engineering or a related field)
Experience working with automation, software or production systems in an industrial environment
Strong project management and communication skills
The ability to develop and maintain system documentation in a structured and high-quality manner
Fluent written and spoken Finnish and English


Considered An Advantage


Experience with Honeywell systems, particularly Experion PKS
Pascal programming skills
Experience with IT/OT networks
Understanding of Computerised System Validation
Database and software development skills
Experience in the pharmaceutical, chemical or food industries

Your new team

Fermion Oy is a subsidiary of Orion and develops and produces all active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for Orion's in-house developed innovative medicines and for some of Orion's branded, generic and animal health products. Fermion also develops and produces a series of generic APIs for various indications and is active as CDMO, contract development and manufacturing organization, to serve other pharmaceutical companies. The company has produced APIs in Hanko, Finland since 1973, and the plant currently has personnel of 180. The company's Oulu plant commenced operations back in 1964 and currently employs more than 100 professionals. Fermion’s head office is in Espoo where R&D, S&M and part of QM personnel are located. Altogether approximately 400 professionals work for Fermion.

Orion Pharma is a globally operating Nordic pharmaceutical company – a builder of well-being for over a hundred years.

Together we develop, manufacture, and market human and veterinary pharmaceuticals and active pharmaceutical ingredients. Proprietary products developed by Orion Pharma are used to treat cancer, neurological diseases, respiratory diseases, and more.
