Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

The Data & Platform Specialist will build and operationalize the target-state data platform, run hands-on data governance, produce Power BI reports, and lead cross-functional data deliveries.
The role requires strong data and platform engineering skills including data modelling and Power BI, experience with modern platforms (Snowflake, DBT, Microsoft Fabric) and familiarity with governance tools (Collibra, Ellie).
The position is based in Helsinki or Tampere, Finland, reports to the Director, Digital Customer Experience, and applications must be submitted via UPM’s online system by September 17, 2026.
UPM offers a purpose-driven, sustainability-focused workplace with a caring community, strong development opportunities, and international career growth.

We are now looking for Data & Platform Specialist to join UPM Adhesive Materials. This is a fixed-term position with development track located in Helsinki or Tampere, Finland.

Greetings from your future manager

" Our team drives digital transformation at Adhesive Materials. We reinvent and improve the commercial engine with data, AI, and automation. Data is the foundation that makes all of it possible, and this hands-on role is where that foundation gets built.  If you like working where data meets the business, learn fast, and want to grow the role you start in, I'd like to hear from you.”

– Sascha Butter, Director, Digital Customer Experience

What you will do

Contribute to data architecture work. Support the organization in developing the target-state data platform and the processes around it
Run hands-on data governance execution, and set up and embed governance principles and processes across domains (ideally in Collibra)
Lead data teams toward defined objectives and support data initiatives across the organization. Support the preparation and running of our cross-functional Data Community meetings
Collaborate closely with commercial stakeholders and progressively take on project leadership responsibilities
Audit our MyAccess and Power BI workspaces and reports, and produce Power BI reporting hands-on
Own Information Modelling tasks to support how our business entities and master data relate across systems (ideally with Ellie)
Run delivery sprints with the team in DevOps (Scrum, delivery cadence)

Who you are

You have strong data and platform engineering background
You have a strong technical foundation: data modeling, analysis, and Power BI
You have gained working knowledge of modern data platforms: Snowflake, DBT, Microsoft Fabric
You are familiar with documentation and governance tooling: Collibra, Ellie
You are interested in AI-driven process reinvention: Foundry, Copilot Studio
You have good commercial sense and the appetite to work beyond technical teams
You are a fast learner, open to acquiring new skills quickly
Experience working across functions and geographies is seen as an asset
Project leadership and stakeholder engagement experience are considered an advantage
