About Us




Outokumpu is accelerating the green transition as the global leader in sustainable stainless steel.




Our business is rooted in the circular economy: our products are made from 95% recycled materials and are fully recyclable. Our stainless steel is used across society—from infrastructure and mobility to household appliances.




We are committed to the 1.5°C climate target and, with up to 75% lower carbon footprint than the industry average, we help our customers reduce their emissions.




Together, we are working towards a world that lasts forever. Outokumpu employs approximately 8,200 professionals in close to 30 countries, with headquarters in Helsinki, Finland, and shares listed on Nasdaq Helsinki. Learn more at www.outokumpu.com.




What you'll do




Design, develop and manage APIs using Azure API Management (APIM).
Build integration solutions with Azure Integration Services (Logic Apps, Azure Functions, Event Grid, Service Bus).
Develop secure, scalable data ingestion and event-driven integration patterns.
Implement API security, authentication, monitoring and lifecycle management.
Automate infrastructure and deployments using Terraform, Bicep and Azure DevOps CI/CD.
Develop integration components in Python, C#, .NET, PowerShell or Node.js.
Collaborate with Data Engineering and AI teams to support data, analytics and AI initiatives.
Contribute to integration standards, reusable frameworks and platform evolution.




What we're looking for




Several years of experience in Azure integration, API engineering or cloud data ingestion.
Strong hands-on experience with Azure API Management and Azure Integration Services.
Knowledge of REST APIs, event-driven architecture and API security (OAuth2, Entra ID, Key Vault).
Experience with Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD.
Familiarity with Azure data platforms such as ADLS, Databricks, Synapse or Azure SQL.
Strong scripting or development skills in Python, C#, .NET, PowerShell or Node.js.
Excellent problem-solving and collaboration skills.




Nice to have




Experience with AI/LLM integrations, RAG or intelligent automation.
Databricks administration and Unity Catalog knowledge.
Experience in manufacturing or other complex enterprise environments.




What We Offer

An opportunity to grow your career in a global company driving sustainable innovation.
A collaborative and inclusive work environment where your ideas are valued.
Meaningful work that contributes to a more sustainable future.
Learning and development opportunities to support your professional growth.
Flexible working arrangements, depending on the role and location.
Competitive compensation and a locally tailored benefits package.
The chance to work with international teams and experts across the organization.




Salary range in Finland: 4,260 to 5,330 gross. Final offer depends on candidate experience and competence.
