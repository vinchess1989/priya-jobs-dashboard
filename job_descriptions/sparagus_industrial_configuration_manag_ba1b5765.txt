As Configuration Manager, you will work on the Herstal site within a small and dynamic team of 2 to 3 Configuration Managers, within the larger team of Design & Configuration (~12 people) which is part of the Engineering Department. You will report directly to the Design&Configuration team leader. You will interact on regular basis with the Designers and Draftmen, the Product Design Authorities, the Quality, Purchasing, Logistics and Production departments.

Responsibilities:

- Implementation of the Configuration Management system.

- Consistency of information in the configuration management system.

- Responsible for the establishment of reference baselines.

Main tasks:

- Tailors the Configuration Management system, process and tools to ensure traceability, quality and productivity from design to production.

- With subcontractors and suppliers, ensures the application of Configuration Management requirements (SOW, etc) and ensures the follow-up of configuration management requirements.

- Participates, with respect to the criteria, in the selection of Configuration Items selected for configuration monitoring

- Animates, organises and manages the Configuration Control Board (CCB) as well as pre and post CCB activities (including administrative tasks).

- Participates in the phase reviews (PDR, CDR, etc) and in configuration audits (FCA and PCA)

Profile:

· Minimum 5 years of experience in a configuration management role

· Experience in the Defence, Aerospace or Automotive industry is a plus

· Experience with Windchill PLM is a plus

· Self-motivated, taking ownership and driving topics to fulfill company mission

· Rigourous, well-organized and finisher mind-set

· Good interpersonal and good communication skills towards stakeholders

· Fluent in French and proficient in English
