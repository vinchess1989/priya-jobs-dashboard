Do you enjoy automating tests and continuously improving software quality?

Are you skilled in Python and experienced in enhancing the quality of complex embedded and electromechanical systems?




We are looking for a Senior Test Automation Engineer

with strong experience in the design and implementation of test automation involving hardware. As a consultant, you'll work at the intersection of hardware and software, developing automated integration and system-level tests for our clients' embedded and industrial products - one project at a time.

Our clients support hybrid work, so presence at their office around Tampere Metropolitan area is required (1-3 days per week).




We expect you to have

several years of experience working with python & Robot Framework,
experience with automated hardware testing, measurement equipment, and industrial interfaces,
familiarity with HIL platforms, simulators, or communication protocols,
mentoring and consultative approach, and
great verbal and written communication skills in English.




It’s a great plus if you also have experience with:




Background and experience from electricity related products
Experience with C#/C/C++ or firmware debugging.
Experience with CI/CD tools like Docker, Kubernetes and Azure DevOps.
Knowledge of industrial standards and safety requirements.




What do you get?

You will be part of a relaxed workplace community free from bureaucracy where the CEO and Back Office team always have your back. You’ll get:




to work with meaningful, long-lasting projects,
fair salary that correlates with your expertise and skills, ranging between 5000 - 5500 €/month.
friendly, highly skilled colleagues
comprehensive benefits package and
leadership that truly cares about your wellbeing and work-life balance.




All in all, an environment where you can focus on interesting and genuinely value-adding aspects of your work.
