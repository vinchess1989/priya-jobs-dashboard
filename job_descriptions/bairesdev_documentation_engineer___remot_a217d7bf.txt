Documentation Engineer at BairesDev

As a Documentation Engineer, you will specialize in technical writing, focusing on API documentation, user guides, and internal knowledge management. You will ensure that complex technical features are communicated clearly, making software easier to understand for both developers and end-users.

What You'll Do


Create and maintain comprehensive, accurate documentation for software products and APIs.
Streamline documentation workflows by integrating them directly into the software development lifecycle.
Manage internal knowledge bases and technical resources to facilitate effective team communication.
Collaborate with engineering teams to produce clear instruction manuals and how-to guides.
Optimize content delivery processes to ensure all documentation is centralized, reliable, and up-to-date.


What We Are Looking For


5+ years of experience in technical writing or documentation engineering.
Proven expertise in creating API documentation, user guides, and internal knowledge bases.
Proficiency with documentation tools such as Confluence, GitBook, and MkDocs.
Hands-on experience with Markdown and Git-based documentation workflows.
Advanced proficiency in English.
