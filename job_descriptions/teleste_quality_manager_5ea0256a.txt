Hae 
Tallenna
Quality is more than processes and audits. It's about helping projects succeed, solving problems, and building customer confidence. As a proactive business partner, Teleste's Quality organization drives continuous improvement and quality excellence across the business. Together with our R&D, Projects, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, and Service teams, we ensure customer satisfaction and deliver reliable, high-quality solutions throughout the product and project lifecycle.

We're looking for a Quality Manager (Tampere or Forssa, Finland) who enjoys working at the intersection of projects, customers, engineering, and operations. In this role, you'll drive project quality, support continuous improvement, and play a key role in customer-facing quality discussions.

You'll work closely with project teams and customers, helping navigate quality requirements, resolve issues, and negotiate practical solutions when challenges arise. Success in this role requires both technical understanding and the ability to communicate clearly, influence decisions, and build trust across different stakeholders.

What you'll do


Drive project quality activities, including quality plans and FAI/iFAI
Support customer discussions, quality reviews, and quality-related negotiations
Lead non-conformance management and corrective actions
Facilitate root cause analysis and continuous improvement initiatives
Monitor quality performance, risks, and improvement actions
Collaborate closely with project management, R&D, manufacturing, supply chain, and service teams


What helps you succeed


Experience in quality engineering, project quality, or industrial operations
Understanding of FAI/iFAI, NCR processes, and systematic problem-solving methods
Strong communication and negotiation skills
Confidence in customer-facing situations and the ability to handle challenging discussions constructively
Ability to build credibility and trust with both customers and internal stakeholders
A practical, structured, and solution-oriented approach
Fluent English communication skills


Why Teleste?

Your work will have a visible impact. Teleste's solutions are used every day in trains, trams, and public transportation systems around the world.

You'll join a team where quality is not only about compliance, but also about enabling project success, maintaining customer confidence, and continuously improving the way we work.

If you enjoy solving complex challenges, working with customers, and influencing outcomes through collaboration, communication, and sound judgment, we want to hear from you.
