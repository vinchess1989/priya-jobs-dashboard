VR-Yhtymä Oyj is hiring a Senior Developer for the Software Development Platform team in Helsinki to build and evolve the company’s internal developer platform.

Description

Build the platform that empowers modern software development 

At VR, we are building the next generation of sustainable transportation and green logistics in Finland and Sweden, making public transport and logistics more attractive, efficient and responsible every day. 

We are now looking for a Senior Developer to join our Software Development Platform team in Helsinki and help shape the future of our internal developer platform. 

In this role, you will build and evolve the shared capabilities, tooling, and automation that enable development teams to deliver software reliably, securely, and efficiently. Your work will have a broad impact across the organization by improving developer experience, increasing platform consistency and accelerating software delivery.  

As a senior member of the team, you'll have a voice in how our platform evolves. You'll contribute to technical decision-making, evaluate new technologies and engineering practices, and help define the future toolset that supports our developers.  

You will collaborate closely with developers, architects, product teams and other engineering stakeholders to create scalable platform services and engineering practices that make everyday development easier and more effective.  

Our values, We Care, We Work Together, We Strive for Better, guide how we design solutions, collaborate across teams and continuously improve. 

 

Your Role 

You won’t be building each team’s infrastructure yourself. Instead, you will create and maintain the building blocks that teams use to build their own, so that a single improvement benefits dozens of developers at once. You will: 

Develop and govern Terraform templates and reusable modules that teams can use to provision their AWS infrastructure with confidence 
Create and evolve GitHub repository and workflow templates, so that starting a new service the right way, with CI/CD included, takes minutes instead of days 
Maintain our golden images, so that every team starts from a secure, up‑to‑date and well‑tested base 
Look after our source code management and artifact practices, keeping repositories, packages and releases tidy, secure and traceable 
Keep the platform healthy and observable, and use reliability metrics to decide where to invest next 
Try out promising new tools, including AI‑assisted development, and bring in the ones that make a real difference 

 

Being a trusted partner for development teams 

You won’t be building in isolation. Development teams are your customers. You will act as their technical sparring partner: helping them adopt new capabilities, untangling tricky delivery problems and collecting feedback that shapes what the platform team builds next. Along the way, you will share what you know and help grow platform expertise across the organisation. 

 

Setting the direction and seeing it through 

Alongside daily delivery, you will help steer the platform’s long‑term course. In practice, this means: 

Assessing the current tech stack, including its strengths, technical debt and risks 
Shaping the platform roadmap together with architects, lead developers and fellow platform experts 
Making well‑reasoned technical decisions, documenting the trade‑offs and contributing to our engineering standards and technology radar 
Planning and driving larger changes across multiple teams, while keeping stakeholders and developers informed and on board from start to finish 

Experience & Skills 

We are looking for an experienced engineer who combines strong cloud and automation expertise with a passion for enabling other development teams. 

Must-have Skills 

Proficiency in English, both written and spoken.  
At least 3 years of hands-on experience developing and operating AWS infrastructure using Terraform.  
Experience developing, maintaining, and governing reusable Infrastructure-as-Code modules, templates, or internal registries.  
Experience designing, implementing, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, workflows, and automation used by software development teams.  
Experience creating reusable development templates, golden paths, reference implementations, or engineering standards adopted by multiple teams.  
Experience working with enterprise Source Code Management (SCM) platforms such as GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket.  
Experience supporting software artifact lifecycle management, including package creation, versioning, storage, promotion, and distribution.  
Strong understanding of modern software development practices, software delivery lifecycles, DevOps principles, and developer tooling ecosystems.  
Demonstrated ability to collaborate across multiple development teams and act as a technical advisor, key user, or champion for engineering tooling and practices. 

Nice-to-have skills:  

Proficiency in Finnish, both written and spoken.  
Experience administering and governing GitHub Enterprise environments, GitHub Actions, repository standards, or CI/CD platform controls.  
Experience implementing observability, monitoring, logging, alerting, and reliability practices using tools such as Sumo Logic, Sentry, Grafana, Datadog, or equivalent platforms.  
Experience applying SLI/SLO-based operational models and reliability metrics to improve platform quality and developer experience.  
Experience managing container-based development and deployment platforms, including Docker and container registries.  
Experience supporting development teams working with modern application ecosystems such as Java, Node.js, .NET, Python, or similar technologies.  
Experience contributing to platform architecture, engineering governance, technical standards, technology roadmaps, or technology radar activities.  
Experience improving Developer Experience (DevEx) through automation, self-service capabilities, Internal Developer Platforms (IDP), standardisation, and engineering productivity initiatives.  
Experience contributing to the development, enhancement, or adoption of software development platforms and developer enablement capabilities.  
Experience evaluating, selecting, or implementing software development tools and platform technologies in an enterprise environment.  
Experience integrating AI-assisted capabilities into software development workflows, developer tooling, or engineering processes.  

You Have 

A strong hands-on approach to solving technical challenges. 
The ability to simplify complexity through automation and standardization. 
Excellent collaboration and communication skills. 
The confidence to work independently while supporting multiple stakeholders. 
A continuous learning mindset and curiosity about new technologies. 
A passion for helping other developers succeed. 
A quality-focused approach to platform reliability, security, and operational excellence.
