Kranthi Kumar
Lead Principal Consultant - GCS Ireland (I'm hiring!)

We're partnering with an innovative technology company seeking a Technical Engineering/Delivery Manager to lead the delivery of a complex enterprise SaaS platform.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys coordinating cross-functional engineering teams, driving release management, resolving delivery challenges, and ensuring high-quality software reaches customers reliably.




Role: Technical Engineering/Delivery Manager

Location: Dublin, Ireland (Hybrid, 3 days onsite)




Key Responsibilities

Coordinate releases across Development, QA, Staging, and Production environments.
Drive delivery plans, manage dependencies, and remove blockers.
Lead release readiness, bug triage, and stakeholder communication.
Participate in major incident management and post-incident reviews.
Collaborate with Product, Engineering, DevOps, QA, and customer-facing teams.
Support successful customer deployments and operational excellence initiatives.

Requirements

7+ years' experience delivering enterprise software products.
Strong understanding of SaaS platforms and software delivery lifecycle.
Experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD practices.
Familiarity with Java/Spring-based environments and REST APIs.
Experience with Agile delivery methodologies.
Excellent stakeholder management and problem-solving skills.

What's on Offer?

Opportunity to work on a market-leading SaaS platform.
Exposure to modern cloud-native technologies.
Highly collaborative and fast-paced environment.
Hybrid working model based in Dublin.
