Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all.

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Roles & responsibilities can include but are not limited to:


Define the global DL4 matrices (Turnaround time, overdue matrices, detection compliance, Statistical Bin Limits (SBL) reactions etc)
Ensure all sites adhere to globally aligned DL4 workflows
Drive global automation and AI enhancements (YIT, Auto-contain, auto-disposition, Smart Manufacturing & Artificial Intelligence (SMAI) assisted detection)
Oversee universal alignment so DL4 signal signals behave the same across sites
Monitor the Assembly and Test specifications
Improvements and optimization in existing DL4 systems via automation systems
Spec limits (Analog values and limits, SBL Bins, Product level ESD)
Test coverage/DL4 improvements (based on lessons learnt, Material review boards and Fail Mode Registers(FMR))
Network standardization (Dispositions/problems)
FMR, Failure analysis, Failure analysis modelling and Defense Line Correlations
Test program review and release
Collaborate with cross-functional teams from Product Engineering, Media Health, Global Quality, Advanced Packaging Technology Development and Test Systems Engineering and drive projects to further enhance defense line 4 capabilities


Mandatory Requirements


Strong understanding of semiconductor device physics
Experience or know-how in device modelling and simulation using TCAD (Silvaco, Sentaurus, etc), as well as compact modelling.
Proficiency in circuit-level analog analysis, and validation using industry-standard tools such as HSPICE
Demonstrated ability to undertake rigorous analysis of electrical test data
Performing root cause analysis for various package and device failures by leveraging various data sources
Hand-on experience in data analytics, scripting and statistical modeling skills (e.g., Python, MATLAB, JMP, SQL).
Ability to communicate and collaborate effectively in cross functional, multidisciplinary team environments.


Education Qualifications


M.Tech in Electrical/Electronics Engineering/ Physics /Materials or related field project experience in semiconductors industry, preferably in packaging, test and reliability.
