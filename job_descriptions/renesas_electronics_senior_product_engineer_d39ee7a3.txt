Job Description



Position Summary:



Connectivity organization is seeking a Senior Product Engineer to provide end-to-end ownership from engineering material planning through product release and volume-production support. It serves as the central coordination point for engineering supply while also driving product readiness, quality, yield and cost performance.



Key Responsibilities:



Initiate and track prototype assembly builds, including assembly priorities, material availability and schedule alignment.
Create and maintain product BOMs and product lifecycle data in PLM systems such as Agile.
Publish timely NPI schedules, risks, constraints and status updates to product teams through daily, weekly and milestone-based communication.
Work with planning, manufacturing, subcontractors and operations teams to remove material-flow obstacles and recover schedule gaps.
Ensure a controlled hand-off from NPI to mass production, including engineering-lot conversion, material readiness and production-planning alignment.
Own the assigned product lifecycle from concept and first silicon through qualification, production release, sustaining and end of life.
Define ATE test, qualification and manufacturing plans to demonstrate datasheet compliance and production readiness.
Drive first-silicon bring-up and debug with Design, Test, Applications and Failure Analysis teams.
Plan and coordinate voltage, temperature and process characterization, including production limit setting and guard-banding.
Own qualification planning and execution in accordance with applicable JEDEC and AEC requirements.
Prepare product-release documentation, datasheet compliance evidence and automotive compliance reports as applicable.
Monitor production data and use statistical analysis to identify and correct yield, quality and test-time deficiencies.
Optimise PAT, SYL, SBL, SPC and disposition limits to protect outgoing quality without creating excessive manufacturing waste.
Drive product cost and gross-margin improvement through yield enhancement, test optimization, BOM control and manufacturing efficiency.


Qualifications



Required Qualifications and Experience:



Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering or Industrial Engineering
At least 5 years of relevant semiconductor experience spanning product engineering, NPI, manufacturing, test, planning.
Practical understanding of semiconductor wafer fabrication, assembly, test, qualification and production-release flows.
Experience managing engineering wafer starts, prototype builds, customer samples, inventory and product BOMs.
Working knowledge of RF and mixed-signal devices, common circuit building blocks, device behaviour and semiconductor test methodology.
Familiarity with ATE platforms such as Teradyne J750 or Advantest V93000, and with laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies and digital multimeters.
Familiarity with JEDEC/AEC qualification standards, qualification hardware/software and semiconductor failure analysis.
Experience with PLM and yield-management tools such as Agile, PDF Exensio or JMP.
Experience working with external foundries, OSATs, subcontractors and globally distributed product teams.
Experience using data and statistical methods to manage yield, quality, cycle time and operational priorities.
Strong analytical, planning, coordination and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and the ability to work under schedule pressure.
Clear written and verbal communication skills and the ability to influence cross-functional teams without direct authority.
