The Wow

We are looking for a skilled DevOps Engineer to help build, maintain, and optimize the infrastructure powering our world-class gaming products. In this role, you will design and implement scalable, secure, and reliable infrastructure solutions while driving DevOps best practices across the organization. You will play a key role in automating deployments, improving system reliability, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless delivery of our products.

Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and maintain scalable infrastructure and technological solutions for production and development environments.
Build, manage, and optimize CI/CD pipelines to enable efficient, reliable, and automated software deployments.
Develop, enhance, and maintain monitoring, logging, and alerting systems to ensure high availability and rapid incident response.
Collaborate with Engineering, Product, QA, and other stakeholders to support infrastructure needs and improve operational efficiency.
Automate repetitive processes to improve deployment speed, consistency, and reliability.
Troubleshoot infrastructure, deployment, and application issues while driving continuous improvements.
Implement and promote DevOps best practices, including infrastructure as code, automation, and security.
Support production environments, ensuring system stability, performance, and scalability.


Requirements


Proven experience as a DevOps Engineer or in a similar infrastructure-focused role.
Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools and deployment automation.
Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform).
Strong knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
Experience with infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible.
Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Datadog, or similar).
Good understanding of Linux systems, networking, and scripting (Bash, Python, or similar).
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to work effectively across multiple teams.


Nice to Have


Experience within the online gaming or iGaming industry.
Knowledge of security best practices and DevSecOps principles.
Experience managing high-availability, distributed systems.
Familiarity with GitOps workflows and modern cloud-native architectures.
