Company Description

As a Product Specialist (f/m/x) for Particle Characterization you will act as the interface between R&D, Product Management, Sales, and our customers. You will support our product portfolio throughout its entire lifecycle—from product development to successful customer implementation. Your responsibilities include contributing to technical specifications and documentation, as well as driving innovative applications in the fields of particle characterization and measurement technology.

Job Description


Provide application-related support to our international subsidiaries as well as existing and prospective customers
Actively contribute to innovation in product and application development
Develop and optimize customer applications and new analytical methods, and share this expertise with our partners
Prepare sales support materials and deliver technical training sessions
Support key projects as a technical and application expert
Contribute to technical and application-related verification testing prior to product release, including field test support
Participate in scientific conferences and industry events and present our solutions


Qualifications


Degree in a natural science discipline (Chemistry, Materials Science, Physics, or a comparable qualification)
Ideally, experience in developing measurement methods or analytical techniques
Analytical, structured, and solution-oriented approach to work
Passion for measurement technology and hands-on work with laboratory and analytical instruments
Strong communication skills and enjoyment of working with customers and cross-functional teams
Team player with a high level of initiative, motivation, and resilience
Excellent written and spoken German and English skills
Willingness to travel occasionally


Additional Information


A reliable and long-term workplace in our expanding and international high-tech company
An innovative company culture that promotes technical excellence guaranteeing us the position of a technological leader in the global market
A value-oriented working environment that fosters friendly, supportive cooperation between colleagues and, at the same time, provides room for involvement, dedication and individual responsibility
Individual training and onboarding
Work Outside Office (occasion-related possibility to work outside the office)
Professional and personal development


We offer a fair and market-aligned salary based on the collective agreement for employees in the metal trade – adjusted to the regional conditions of the Styrian labor market. The minimum gross monthly salary for this position is € 3.300 (based on full-time employment). What truly counts for us are your personality, your skills, and the experience you bring. That’s why we take the time to determine your actual gross salary individually and are happy to offer a higher pay in recognition of your qualifications.
