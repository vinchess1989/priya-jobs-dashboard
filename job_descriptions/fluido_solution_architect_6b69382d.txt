What You Will Get To Do

Solution Architects advise customers about declarative functions in Salesforce.

You will work with challenging and rewarding projects sharing your knowledge with our multinational client companies, and coach a group of talented professionals within Fluido.

What You Will Be Responsible For


Actively influencing customers’ salesforce practices and decisions
Challenging the customers’ thinking and status quo when pertaining to their salesforce solution
Understanding the purpose of the solution from the business point of view
Presenting and communicating technical topics to both business and technical stakeholders, acting as a bridge between the business and development team(s)
Translating business requirements into software design
Helping customers turn their sales, service, or marketing processes into viable solutions using Salesforce best practices
Providing thought leadership towards the client to ensure defined success criteria are met/suggest improvement roadmaps on the business capability level that can be translated into additional project modules and Salesforce improvement roadmap
Driving estimations for new projects/work as add-ons to already agreed-on client deliverables
Making sure of the quality of the project, roll-out effectiveness, and deliverables – suggests corrective measures


The Skills We Are Looking For


Minimum of 5 years of Salesforce experience
Deep Salesforce administration and declarative skills
Deep understanding and experience with different Salesforce products and the best practices
Experience in consulting
Understanding at least one business practice skill

Local language professional proficiency


At Fluido, you will get


Environment for sharing and growing your technical and consulting skillset. Fluido is the best place for Salesforce professionals in Europe
Coaching and support of your Team Lead to bring you to the best projects for you to grow and excel
A diverse team (origins, backgrounds, and field of expertise), supportive and an appropriate environment conducive to performing your work
The possibility to become a superhero in a rapidly growing company: make a difference to global companies as a partner
