About The Job Configuration Manager (m/f/d).

NAXCON GmbH, headquartered in Weil am Rhein with additional offices, including Stuttgart, is one of Germany's leading engineering and IT service providers.

Our experts possess extensive expertise in software and hardware development, modern electronics, and cutting-edge technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI).

In addition to delivering high-quality engineering solutions for our customers, we are passionate about driving our own innovation projects as well as research and development activities. Renowned companies from a wide range of industries place their trust in our expertise, reflecting the outstanding knowledge, professionalism, and commitment of our engineers.

Position: Configuration Manager

Location: Bremen, Germany

Field: Defence

Type: Full-time

Tasks


Configuration management in accordance with DIN ISO 10007 (planning, identification, record-keeping, and change control) in projects and product teams
Document control, comprehensive maintenance of product and project documents with a focus on audit compliance.
Coordination and tracking in change management, as well as participation in the change control board
Support in defining the project-specific methodology for the structure, numbering, and labeling of the product structure (hardware, software, including material number assignment)
Conducting internal and external configuration audits, as well as preparing analyses and change overviews
Further development and maintenance of the configuration management system in SAP, as well as cross-departmental coordination to develop consistent and standards-compliant configuration solutions.


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in a technical field (e.g., mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, industrial engineering, or comparable) or certification as a state-certified technician (m/f/d)
Professional experience in configuration management, ideally in a technical environment; knowledge of configuration management standards is a plus
Fluency in German and English
Experience in the areas of product structure, technical drawing sets, and documentation
Very good knowledge of MS Office and SAP (Material Management, Configuration and Change Management)
Good knowledge of Jira, Confluence, and SharePoint is desirable
Current CM2 / CMPIC certification (Levels 1–4, 10) is desirable Willingness to travel worldwide
