About Seidor

SEIDOR helps organisations transform and remain competitive through technology and innovation, always putting people first. With more than 8,000 professionals across 45 countries, we are a diverse and inclusive global team committed to talent, equal opportunities, and sustainable growth.

Will you join us in humanizing technology?

We are looking for a Release manager to join our team in Brussels, working Fully remote within Europe.

WHAT WILL YOU DO IN YOUR DAY-TO-DAY?


Plan and coordinate release schedules and activities, maintain the enterprise release calendar, and manage dependencies to prevent change collisions.
Manage and oversee the end-to-end release process, ensuring alignment with Change and Deployment Management processes.
Collaborate with development, testing, operations, and business teams to ensure readiness and smooth coordination across the release pipeline.
Ensure compliance with defined quality standards, release criteria, and regulatory and audit requirements for traceability.
Identify, mitigate, and document potential release risks, including defining rollback and recovery procedures.
Document release plans, processes, contingency strategies, and post-release reports to provide transparency and audit readiness.
Conduct structured release readiness reviews and go/no-go meetings, validating test results, risk assessments, and rollback criteria.
Monitor release progress and track metrics (deployment success rate, lead time, defect leakage), integrate with CI/CD pipelines and automation dashboards.
Communicate release status, risks, and post-release outcomes to stakeholders using standardized reports and dashboards.
Continuously improve release management processes by incorporating lessons learned, automation, and industry best practices.
Oversee post-release hypercare to ensure stability, defect triage, and handover to operations.


Requirements

WHAT DO WE EXPECT FROM YOU?


If holding a Master’s degree (EQF Level 7): min 10 years of experience.
If holding Bachelor’s degree: min 12 years of experience
working with software development lifecycles (such as Agile, Waterfall, DevSecOps or equivalent) and their impact on release planning and delivery.
working with relational databases such as Oracle or equivalent.
10 years of experience using CI/CD tools (such as Jenkins, Bamboo, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps or equivalent) to enable automated builds and deployments.
10 years of experience using version control systems (such as Git, SVN or equivalent) and continuous integration toolchains (such Bamboo, Jenkins or equivalent).
7 years of experience using monitoring and logging tools (such as Splunk, ELK Stack, Prometheus, Grafana or equivalent) to validate release stability post-deployment.
3 years of experience in Oracle WebLogic (up to at least version 14 or more recent versions).
English: level B2


Certificates And/or Standards

One of the following or an equivalent certification:


ITIL 4 or 5 Foundation
an Agile delivery methodology certification (such as SAFe or equivalent)


Nice To Have


3 years of experience in containerization and orchestration technologies like Docker and Kubernetes.
3 years of experience in Java (version JDK 11 or more recent versions) and JEE (version 7 or more recent versions).
3 years of experience in deployment automation and release orchestration tools (such as XL Deploy, Harness, Spinnaker, or equivalent) to coordinate enterprise releases.
3 years of experience in release governance practices (such as change advisory boards, go/no-go criteria, rollback strategies or equivalent) for controlled deployments.
3 years of experience in scripting languages such as Python, Bash, or PowerShell or equivalent.
3 years of experience using issue tracking tools (such as JIRA or equivalent) and collaboration tools (such as Confluence, SharePoint or equivalent) to support planning and delivery.
