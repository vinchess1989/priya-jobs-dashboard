Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

We are hiring a Functional Safety Specialist to join the Automation team in Kilpilahti, Porvoo, working on automation design from initial studies to commissioning. 
Key responsibilities include risk analysis, SRS and SIL determination, SIL verification, SIF and functional safety design, functional safety assessments, procedure development, and OT cybersecurity validation and testing. 
Required qualifications are 10+ years of experience in process automation, a B.Sc. or M.Sc. in automation or a related field, functional safety certification, and knowledge of SIS systems and functional safety lifecycle standards. 
The role offers hybrid work with a commuter bus from Helsinki, Finnish employee benefits and training, and applications are due by September 27, 2026.

Are you looking for a career in an inspiring, global environment where your expertise makes a difference? At Neste, you will be working for the world’s leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel, which enable our customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. 

We are looking for you,

Functional Safety Specialist 

to join our Automation team.

Do you have a strong background in engineering and the drive to lead process and energy industry projects? Or are you a driven engineer ready to elevate your career and step into a leadership role? If so, an exciting opportunity awaits you!

As our Functional Safety Specialist, you will have a key role in automation engineering. You'll leverage your expertise to guide and mentor the automation design team, collaborating closely with internal stakeholders and across various engineering disciplines, from initial studies and basic engineering to detailed design, implementation, and commissioning. We provide versatile training and development opportunities with the latest technology in the industry, as well as comprehensive employee benefits.

The position will be located in Kilpilahti, Porvoo. We provide a free commuter bus service from Helsinki and nearby regions to Kilpilahti. Partial remote work or working at our remote working office in Espoo are also possible.

Neste in brief

Neste (NESTE, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with production on three continents. The company’s renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027. Neste also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland. The company has a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offering, such as EV charging, in Finland and in the Baltics.

Neste’s strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels, which help its customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. Neste is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion. Read more: neste.com
