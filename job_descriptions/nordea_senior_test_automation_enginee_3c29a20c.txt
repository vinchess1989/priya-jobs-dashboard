Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

Would you like to join a team delivering competitive, streamlined, and compliant card products for everyday spending to our customers?

At Nordea, we are at the forefront of a technology-driven revolution in banking, committed to evolving alongside our customers' changing needs. By fostering an agile and inspiring environment, we empower you to bring your unique skills, ideas, and perspectives. As one of the largest IT employers in the Nordic region, we offer a wealth of opportunities to collaborate with international teams and drive impactful change. Join us in shaping the future of Cards business, where your contributions can truly make a difference and help you grow within something bigger.

About Our Team

We are looking for a Test Automation Engineer with Java experience to increase quality of solutions focusing on automation in Cards Product teams. With us, you’ll be in good company, with a chance to make your mark on something bigger.

Main responsibilities in this role:


Implementing automated test cases together with framework improvements
Eliminating manual testing and trivial activities
Gathering and managing test data needed for testing
Monitoring, debugging and documenting test results
Proactively increasing test coverage


Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you:


Have ability to drive and support QA activities across the delivery team
Take an independent approach to ongoing project tasks
You will take the ownership of the Business deliverable of the features
Are willing to constantly improve your competences in test automation
Knowledge of and willingness to learn and adopt AI tools in day-to-day work is considered an advantage
Have good communication and presentation skills


Your background and skills include:


5+ years of experience with developing and maintaining test automation solutions
Java coding skills (senior-level programming experience)
Backend testing experience like Rest API
Solid technical knowledge for effective collaboration with developers
Experience in working with source code control tools (GIT), as well as experience in working with CI/CD tools (Jenkins)
Experience in Unix/Linux would be additional asset


It would be ideal if you also have:


Python experience
GUI\API\DB\queues testing experience
Robot framework experience
The Cards domain knowledge
