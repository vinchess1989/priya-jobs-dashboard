Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

As part of Nordea’s 2030 strategic direction, we are modernizing our technology and data estate while accelerating the adoption of AI-enabled capabilities across the bank.

We are looking for experienced AI & Cloud Engineers to join our Integration SRE team. You will help deliver secure, automated and reusable cloud infrastructure and platform capabilities with the focus on AI products in close collaboration with engineers, architects, product owners, and governance stakeholders.

You will design, build, test and improve cloud platform patterns, automation and operational controls that enable internal teams to provision and operate modern data and AI solutions safely in a regulated banking environment.

This is a full-time permanent position that may require participation in the on-call rotation in the future.

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We're not just a bank, we're a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

Meet the Integration SRE team. Our role is to provide, automate, and support IT infrastructure platforms based on a modern IT toolset to drive highly available and cost-efficient applications.

What You'll Be Doing

Main responsibilities in this role:


Build, configure and improve infrastructure components, based on cloud platforms and tools.
Implement reusable infrastructure-as-code modules for projects, networking, IAM, service accounts, secrets, logging, monitoring and policy guardrails.
Support secure cloud landing-zone patterns, including organization and project structure, shared VPC, network segmentation, private connectivity, firewall policies and environment isolation.
Create and maintain CI/CD pipelines for provisioning, validation, testing, deployment and lifecycle management of platform components.
Support AI platform enablement, including platform patterns such as AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex, Azure OpenAI.
Build observability and operational controls using logging, monitoring, alerting, dashboards, runbooks and production-readiness checks.
Collaborate with cybersecurity, governance and architecture teams to ensure platform capabilities meet security, compliance and operational expectation


Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you have a good mix of deep technical and soft skills and strive to continuously improve our services.

Your background and skills include:


Extensive experience with at least one of the cloud platforms: AWS, GCP, Azure.
Container orchestration: Advanced usage of Kubernetes.
Terraform and IaC: Strong Terraform skills, including module usage, reusable patterns, state handling, variables, outputs, validation and promotion across environments.
IAM and security: Practical knowledge of IAM, least privilege, service accounts, workload identity, federation, secrets management, key management and secure access patterns.
CI/CD and automation: Experience building and operating pipelines for infrastructure provisioning, validation, deployment, policy checks and release management.
AI technologies: Experience with Large Language Models, Model Context Protocol, AI-powered automation.
Observability: Experience with cloud logging, monitoring, alerting
Ability to work in a regulated enterprise environment where compliance, resilience, and auditability matter.
Good English skills.
