Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

At Skyfora, we transform existing GNSS infrastructure into the world's most advanced, densest atmospheric observation network — powering sharper, faster forecasts for industries, governments, and communities worldwide. We believe the best scientific breakthroughs happen when diverse perspectives, deep curiosity, and cross-disciplinary expertise come together.




We are looking for a Senior Operations Engineer, a professional with the drive and capability to design and run modern cloud infrastructure for next-generation weather intelligence. If you are motivated by having a major role in building reliable operations for systems that power high-impact, data-intensive weather and AI services, we would love to see you bring your expertise to Skyfora.




How will you make an impact

Infrastructure Design & Scalability: Design, build, and operate our cloud infrastructure and services with a strong focus on reliability, simplicity, and scalability.
Operational Ownership: Take ownership of operational quality—including availability, performance, and incident response—for our platforms and applications.
Observability & Visibility: Establish and evolve observability (metrics, logs, alerts) to provide clear visibility into overall system health.
IaC & Automation: Implement infrastructure-as-code and automation (modules, scripts, pipelines) to eliminate manual toil and ensure reproducible environments.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with software engineers and data teams to design deployment workflows, CI/CD pipelines, and operational practices for microservices.
Production Resilience: Diagnose and resolve production issues, lead root-cause analysis, and implement long-term fixes that elevate system resilience.




What You bring to the team

Cloud & Operations Experience: Solid experience operating cloud-based services and infrastructure in production (operations, SRE, or DevOps roles).
Distributed Systems: Proven ability to run distributed systems or microservices reliably in a cloud environment.
Efficiency Track Record: A demonstrated track record of improving system reliability, deployment processes, or overall operational efficiency.
Modern CI/CD Expertise: Strong understanding of CI/CD and modern software delivery practices.
Problem-Solving Mindset: A self-driven approach, customer-service-oriented attitude, and strong problem-solving skills for complex operational issues.




Bonus points for

Experience with HashiCorp tools such as Nomad and Consul.
Prior work in data-intensive environments.
Interest in weather, climate, or other mission-critical domains where reliability is essential.




Why Join Skyfora?

Build Something Truly New: Take pride in pioneering technology that solves real-world climate and weather challenges from the ground up.
Real Ownership & Autonomy: You will have the space to take full ownership of your work, drive technical direction, and see your ideas directly impact customers.
Collaborative Culture: We operate with a high-trust, communication-first mindset. We take pride in an environment built on mutual respect, where you are fully supported to communicate openly and show up as your genuine, true self.
