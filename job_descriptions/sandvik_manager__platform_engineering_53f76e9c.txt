Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Business Unit Automation is the home of AutoMine® - the world-leading solution for autonomous mining - and Newtrax – the world leader in collision avoidance and operations management solutions for underground mining – and Universal Field Robots UFR, a fast-growing Australia-based provider of autonomous interoperable solutions for the surface mining and underground mining markets.

Our hubs are located in Tampere (Finland), Montreal (Canada) and Brisbane (Australia) where we focus on developing fully automated and robotic mining technologies for mobile machinery, and safety solutions including collision avoidance system. Our overriding aim is to make mining safer and more sustainable for the future. We work closely together with our Sandvik Mining sales areas to serve our customers across the globe, striving for the highest level of service and support.

We are now looking for a self-driven and collaborative Platform Engineering Manager to join Business Unit Automation and help shape the shared technology foundations of our AutoMine® product family. You will build and lead the platform engineering team, develop a strong Finnish supplier network and bring internal teams and external partners together around a common roadmap. You will report directly to Olli Snellman, Head of Product Development, AutoMine®. The position is based in Tampere, Finland at our home office in Tulli Business Park located in the city center.

Your role is to turn platform ambitions into practical engineering execution. You will drive shared capabilities, reusable technology foundations and common development and verification practices that reduce duplication across products. Working closely with R&D teams, technical product owners, architects and other stakeholders, you will align priorities, dependencies and delivery commitments. You will build an effective partner ecosystem in Finland and collaborate with other external parties to complement internal capabilities. Your roadmap will connect technology choices and architecture with automated testing and a path to continuous delivery. A central expectation is to embed AI into every process step—from requirements and planning to design, implementation, testing, documentation, release and maintenance—with appropriate human oversight and attention to quality, safety and cybersecurity.

Your Key Responsibilities


Build and lead the platform engineering team, defining roles, recruiting and developing talent.
Build supplier network and set clear collaboration practices with partners.
Align internal stakeholders across R&D, product management and technical teams on priorities and dependencies.
Own the platform engineering roadmap, translating product needs into prioritized work and technology decisions.
Unite development and verification through test automation, simulation and virtualization.
Drive AI adoption at every process step, coaching teams and partners while retaining engineering accountability.


Your Profile

You have a relevant technical education, for example a bachelor’s or master’s degree in engineering, and experience leading software or platform engineering work. You can connect technical direction with practical execution and understand architecture, reusable software capabilities, modern development practices and continuous delivery. You understand interfaces between software and automation systems, including their technical constraints, limitations and integration dependencies, and can translate these into realistic platform decisions. You have the ability to build and develop an engineering team, bringing together the skills needed to deliver the platform roadmap. You bring experience in supplier or partner collaboration and can build an effective Finnish engineering network. You are comfortable creating roadmaps, managing dependencies and bringing stakeholders together around clear priorities. Experience with automation, autonomous industrial equipment or software-intensive products is an advantage.

You have a strong practical interest in AI-assisted engineering and can turn that interest into everyday ways of working across the full lifecycle. You combine a systematic approach with curiosity, sound judgment and a willingness to challenge established practices. You build trusted relationships across functions and organizations, communicate clearly in English, both verbally and in writing, and work proactively and independently in a changing environment. Finnish language is considered an advantage. You enjoy helping teams and partners learn, take ownership and deliver together.

What is in it for you?

We offer you an engaging role in an international business environment, extraordinary products, great colleagues, and opportunities for professional development.

At Sandvik, we believe in finding the right balance between work, family life, and personal interests, and we actively support our employees in achieving this. We offer a hybrid office/home working environment and an open-minded culture where diversity and inclusion are part of how we work.
