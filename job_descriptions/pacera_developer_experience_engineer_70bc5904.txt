Developer Experience Engineer

Team: Engineering/Development - Location: Stockholm OR Espoo OR Riga


Pacera unifies financial close, consolidation and FP&A into one intelligent platform. With AARO, Aico and Mercur, finance teams turn trusted data into AI-driven insight — powering robust, scalable, and smarter decision-making for the Office of the CFO.


About the role

We're looking for a Developer Experience Engineer to our new DevEx Team who is passionate about engineering effectiveness, developer experience, developer joy, and software delivery capabilities.

Your mission is to make engineers more productive, effective, and successful by reducing friction across the software development lifecycle. You'll work with developer tooling, automation, CI/CD, AI capabilities, standards, and self-service platforms to create a world-class developer experience.

As AI transforms software engineering, you'll help shape how our organization adopts AI-assisted and agentic development, turning emerging technologies into practical capabilities that teams can use every day.

This role offers a unique opportunity to influence how software engineering is practiced across the organization. You'll help define the future of developer experience and AI-enabled engineering, while creating the tools, platforms, and ways of working that allow teams to deliver their best work every day.


What you will do

Drive adoption of AI-assisted and agentic software development practices.

Build and evolve shared AI capabilities, engineering tools, and developer platforms.

Improve developer workflows, onboarding, documentation, and self-service experiences.

Create reusable templates, golden paths, and standards that enable teams to move faster.

Enhance CI/CD capabilities, quality gates, and software delivery processes.

Measure and improve engineering effectiveness using data and developer feedback.

Partner with development teams, Cloud, Security, Compliance, and Architecture to remove friction and enable secure, scalable delivery.


What we’re looking for

You likely have experience in software engineering, platform engineering, DevOps, developer tooling, or automation and enjoy creating solutions that help other engineers succeed.

We believe you'll bring:

Strong technical skills and the ability to automate and build tooling using languages such as Powershell, Python, Bash, or similar.

Experience with GitHub, CI/CD, and modern software delivery practices.

Experience with both Windows and Linux environment

Interest in and experience with AI-assisted software development.

A collaborative mindset and the ability to influence through expertise rather than authority.

A passion for simplifying, automating, and continuously improving the developer experience.

Experience with cloud platforms, Infrastructure as Code, internal developer platforms, security tooling, compliance frameworks, or DORA metrics is a plus.


What we offer

The opportunity to be part of our next phase of growth and expansion, backed by strategic private equity investment

Competitive compensation and benefits package including pension, flex hours, wellness allowances and more

Hybrid working options

About Pacera

Pacera is redefining financial operations for the Office of the CFO — powering planning, financial close, consolidation, and reporting through one unified platform, trusted by 700+ organisations and 70,000+ users across more than 140 countries.


Our platform brings together three specialised products — Aico by Pacera, AARO by Pacera, and Mercur by Pacera — giving finance teams the accuracy, control, and confidence they need to run mission-critical operations, without compromise.


Aico automates and streamlines financial close processes

Mercur powers FP&A, budgeting, forecasting, business intelligence, and financial analytics

AARO delivers consolidation and group reporting solutions


Each product has a loyal customer base and a strong track record of growth. Built on decades of proven expertise, our platform helps finance and executive leaders automate, govern, and make faster, smarter decisions.


We're headquartered in Stockholm, with offices across Sweden, Finland, Latvia, and the UK. And we're driven by the same principles wherever we are: collaboration, continuous learning, flexibility, curiosity, and the drive to win together.


Backed by Accel-KKR, a global technology-focused investment firm, the group has ambitious growth plans driven by both organic expansion and acquisitions. We are now strengthening our operations by investing in key roles that support our platform and future scalability.
