Hae 
Tallenna

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer — someone who has proven they can build and operate production infrastructure and is ready to bring that expertise to projects that power business-critical systems across industries. At Forge, your work enables real business impact for organizations that keep society moving. If that sounds like your kind of work – let's talk.




First, let us tell you more about Forge Digital

We are Forge Digital — a technology agency with cutting-edge solutions and strong domain knowledge in digitalization across manufacturing, retail & logistics, and energy. Our mission is to improve our clients' performance. We guide companies in assessing their current digital maturity, identifying digital challenges and opportunities, and designing a customized digitalization roadmap. We leverage cutting-edge technologies to enhance operational efficiency. Our expertise in analytics, machine learning, edge computing, and sensor-machine integrations enables us to create a sustainable competitive edge.




What we look for

As a (Senior) DevOps Engineer at Forge, you will design, build, and operate infrastructure and CI/CD platforms for our customer projects — taking ownership of production environments across hybrid cloud environments. Your day-to-day involves Infrastructure as Code, container orchestration, CI/CD pipelines, and continuously improving system reliability and security. Our projects serve sectors where system uptime and reliability are not optional. As a senior member of the team, we also expect you to guide technical decisions, mentor colleagues, and help improve our DevOps practices.




How to forge success in this role

To succeed in this role, you bring solid, hands-on experience — we expect at least five years of professional work in DevOps, platform engineering, or infrastructure roles. The following capabilities are what we consider essential:

Linux System Administration: Solid hands-on experience maintaining and troubleshooting Linux environments, especially in a cloud context
AWS: Understanding of core AWS services, infrastructure deployment, cloud networking, security and best-practice architectures
Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Proficiency with Terraform for provisioning cloud resources and Ansible for configuration management and automation workflows. We also use Python and Bash for task automation and scripting
CI/CD Tooling: Experience designing, maintaining, and troubleshooting CI/CD pipelines, including work with runners, containerized build environments, and pipeline configurations
Communication & Customer Interaction: As a senior engineer in a consultancy setting, you are comfortable communicating with clients and collaborating across teams.




The following experience is considered an advantage:

Strong Git skills and prior experience with GitLab, including GitLab CI/CD
Experience running and maintaining a self-managed GitLab environment
Monitoring and observability tools such as Splunk, Grafana, or Prometheus
Willingness to run maintenance procedures outside of normal working hours when needed




What we offer

At Forge, our DevOps engineers have real influence over how infrastructure is designed and delivered — you will shape tooling, define standards, and solve problems that directly affect project outcomes. Our clients operate in sectors where the systems you build genuinely matter. You'll get technical variety and depth, combined with the autonomy to make decisions and own the results.




With us you get to specialize in making the digitalization in the industrial sector happen. You will join a highly motivated and experienced team, with whom to build the story of Forge Digital. As a newly established company, we provide a real opportunity to put your mark on how we work, operate and innovate with clients. We have ambition and goals placed, and you can read more about us in the latest press release.




This position is full-time, and the start date is agreed with the chosen candidate. We offer you a good salary based on your experience level. Our comfy offices in GO21-building in Tampere and Helsinki city center offer a nice atmosphere to work in. We work in a flexible hybrid model, thus we put a lot of focus on building a strong team culture and cohesion. We offer great employment benefits, which at the moment include lunch benefit, Epassi Flex budget of 1000€/year, mobile phone benefit, home internet, health and accident insurances and daily refreshments at the office. Your supervisor will be there to sparr with your personal learning, career development and possible training needs.




Our recruitment process

If Forge's approach resonates with you, send us your application, and join our skilled team! Our application form is easy and quick: simply add your contact information, CV, and cover letter to the form below. You can also share your LinkedIn profile or attach any other relevant documents you wish. Apply soon, as we will process applications during the application period. In our recruitment process, you can expect to have a couple of interviews, including technical interview.




For more information about the position and our company, you can contact Principal Technology Consultant Mikko Viskari (mikko.viskari@forgedigital.fi / 050 465 1322).
