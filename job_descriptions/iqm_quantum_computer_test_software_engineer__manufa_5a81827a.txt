Test Software Engineer /Manufacturing Engineering

Job description
Would you like to help build the testing solutions behind the world's leading quantum computing technology?

Our client, IQM Quantum Computers, is looking for a Test Software Engineer to join its Manufacturing Engineering team in Espoo.

In this role, you will develop software for automated test stations used in the testing, quality control, and calibration of advanced electronic products in both R&D and production environments. You will play a key role in ensuring that products perform reliably and meet demanding quality requirements before reaching production.

This position combines software development, electronics, test automation and practical engineering. We are looking for someone who enjoys solving technical challenges, understands how software and hardware interact, and is motivated to develop robust testing solutions in a fast-paced technology environment.

Your role

As a Test Software Engineer, you will be responsible for developing and maintaining software used in automated production and laboratory test systems.

Your responsibilities include:


Developing software for automated test stations and manufacturing test environments

Designing and implementing new test automation solutions

Maintaining and improving existing test systems

Integrating test equipment and measurement devices into automated test environments

Troubleshooting issues related to software, hardware and measurement systems

Supporting production and R&D teams in testing-related topics

Creating technical documentation and maintaining software quality standards

Training and supporting users of the test systems

The role requires close collaboration with manufacturing, R&D and other engineering teams. You will work with both software and hardware, helping to develop reliable test solutions for complex electronic products.

What we are looking for

We are looking for an engineer with a strong software development background and an interest in electronics testing and automation.

To succeed in this role, you have:


A B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Electronics Engineering, Telecommunications Engineering or a related field

A few years of relevant industry experience in software development, testing or automation

Strong Python programming skills

Experience with software development practices, documentation and version control (Git)

Understanding of software-hardware interaction and electronics testing environments

Ability to work independently and take ownership of technical solutions

Good written and spoken English skills

The products being tested include advanced digital and RF electronics. Therefore, an interest in electronics, measurements and test systems is important for success in this role.

In addition, experience in one or more of the following areas is considered highly valuable:


Test automation development

Manufacturing or R&D test environments

RF and/or digital electronics

Linux environments

Electronic test and measurement equipment

SCPI, PyVISA, SQL or CI/CD tools

We are particularly interested in candidates who have worked with electronics products, test systems or manufacturing technologies and enjoy solving challenges that span software, hardware and measurement environments.

Why join IQM?

This is an opportunity to work at the forefront of quantum technology in an international and highly collaborative environment. You will join a team developing critical testing solutions that directly support the manufacturing and scalability of quantum computing hardware.

IQM offers:


The opportunity to work with cutting-edge technology and advanced electronics

Meaningful technical challenges and real ownership of your work

An international team of highly skilled engineers and experts

A culture that values innovation, collaboration and continuous learning

Modern facilities and flexible ways of working

The position will initially be filled through Barona Engineering, with an estimated assignment duration of at least six months. Depending on business needs and performance, there may be opportunities for a longer-term role within IQM.

Interested? Submit your application and CV as soon as possible, as we will review applications throughout the recruitment process.
