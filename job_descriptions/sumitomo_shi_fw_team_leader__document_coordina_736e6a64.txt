Job Description

About the Role

Sumitomo SHI FW (SFW) is looking for an enthusiastic and dedicated Team Leader, Document Coordinators to join the Engineering Management team.

We are seeking a professional who is passionate about documentation, information management, and continuous improvement, and who thrives in a dynamic working environment. In this role, you will lead the Global Project Documentation team and ensure that Project Document Management practices, procedures, tools, and archiving processes support both proposal and project execution activities.

You will manage and develop a team of Project Documentation Coordinators, balance team capacity, workloads, and provide practical support to proposal and project teams in complex documentation matters. The team consists of members from Europe and Asia. The position reports to the Chief Engineer of Engineering Management.

Key Responsibilities


Lead and develop the Global Document Coordinators team.
Manage team capacity, workload allocation, performance, and competence development.
Ensure effective, consistent, and compliant Project Document Management and archiving practices across proposals and projects.
Develop, maintain, and improve documentation procedures, tools, standards, and work instructions.
Provide documentation support to proposal and project teams, including complex issue resolution and cost/resource planning.
Drive continuous improvement by implementing lessons learned and resolving process bottlenecks.
Ensure documentation and archiving practices comply with applicable laws, regulations, corporate policies, and external standards.
Support customer negotiations within documentation-related matters when needed.
Be ready to step into Project Document Coordinator role when situation requires


What We Are Looking For

The ideal candidate is:


Proactive, innovative, and solution-oriented, with the confidence to take initiative.
Flexible and able to work effectively in changing environment and under the pressure
Skilled at collaborating with people from diverse backgrounds.
Eager to continuously develop both personal expertise and the skills of team members.
Open-minded, committed to work and passionate about delivering high-quality results.
Up to date with industry trends and developments in documentation and information management.
Capable of leading, supporting, and developing a team in a changing project environment.


Required Qualifications


Previous experience working with document management systems – knowledge of M-Files is an advantage.
Knowledge of archiving principles and practices.
Excellent oral and written communication skills in English.
Strong organizational and problem-solving abilities.
Experience in leading international team is seen as an advantage
Understanding of documentation standards, procedures, and compliance requirements


What We Offer


An opportunity to work in a collaborative and supportive environment.
Professional development and learning opportunities.
Challenging and meaningful work that makes an impact.
A leadership role with the opportunity to improve and develop document-management practices.
A team culture built on openness, innovation, and commitment.

About Us

Powering a decarbonized world for everyone

Sumitomo SHI FW (SFW) has been providing innovative and environmentally sound energy solutions to our customers for over 100 years. Our mission is to enable the energy transition to a carbon-free future through decarbonization, decentralization and digitalization.

Our solutions expand from the world-leading circulating fluidized bed (CFB) technology to long-duration energy storage, waste-to-energy, waste-to-value, carbon capture and services for the global power and industrial markets. Through co-creation, partnerships, and ecosystem collaboration SFW aim to accelerate the speed of decarbonization and create more value to our customers.

We are global energy experts with more than 1800 talented staff, across multiple locations, covering the globe from North America through Europe to Asia.
