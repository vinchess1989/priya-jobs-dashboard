Your Role And Responsibilities


Be responsible for driving new product development from designs stage to high volume production
Be responsible for managing timelines, product performance analysis, data sheet compliance matrix through all rounds of design optimization, customer sampling and production release.
Use data processing tools like JMP,Exensio,O+,Data Power, Excel used to calculate CPK, identify outliers, set limits and guard bands based on statistics
Participate in 1st Si debug, Yield and Test time improvement, work very closely with test and design engineers to release product to full production
Run cross functional meeting with project team members and across time zones and sites
Manage customer returns and RMA process and drive timely closure
Define product validation requirements by working with cross functional teams, including design team, DFT team and test engineers to translate concepts into tests.
Collaborate with test engineers to develop an effective and efficient manufacturing test on ATE for mass production
Drive MP readiness with multiple engineering and operation teams
Participate in yield analysis, drive necessary improvement actions on silicon process, setting optimization, and testing robustness to improve yield to target
Work with reliability engineers to achieve successful product qualifications based on JEDEC and AECQ100 Standards
Failure analysis support for customer returned issue


Preferred Qualification


Requires BE/B.Tech degree in Electrical / Electronic with 1 to 8 years of Semiconductor Product engineering experience.
Experience working with CMOS ASIC Design, Test, Foundry & Production teams.
Strong track record of ASIC design from concept to mass production all the way to EOL.
Deep understanding of statistical data analysis along with data analysis tools such as Excel VBA / Spotfire / O+ / JMP / Galaxy.
Should have basic to advance Knowledge of the ATE Tester, V93K is preferred.
Strong analytical skills, ability to problem solve and debug systems, circuits, and devices.
Experienced in product coordination, yield improvement & test time reduction.
Working knowledge of semiconductor quality and automotive standard process control
Hands-on experience with production & Characterization data collection at Wafer as well as Package level
Experience in working in production test floors, bench instruments & ATE is a plus.
Experience with Probers and Handlers is a plus
