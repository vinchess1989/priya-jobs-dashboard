Are you passionate about cutting-edge technology and eager to make a meaningful impact on the digitalization of society? Do you want to work on something with an impact and be at the forefront of Pan-European technology enabling HPC, AI and quantum computing?




At CSC, we offer unparalleled growth opportunities, a supportive and inclusive environment, and the chance to work on the future of research and learning. Join us today!




We have launched the EuroHPC Federation Platform to federate European HPC systems and quantum computers. The platform currently integrates 10 supercomputers (with more coming) across as many countries in Europe, with already thousands of users.




The main features are:

Providing a single federated identity across all supercomputers for each user
Providing uniform access to all federated supercomputers
Providing advanced features like workflows and interactive usage across all federated supercomputers
Providing resource and allocations management across the federation targets




CSC is seeking a Service Manager for the platform to join the Federated Platform Services group at CSC to manage and oversee the federation platform while in operation.




Your main responsibilities include:

Lead the day-to-day operations of the European Federation Platform (EFP).
Act as Service Manager and drive the implementation and continuous improvement of service management practices.
Monitor service performance, quality, and compliance with agreed operational targets.
Coordinate incident, problem, change, and release management processes.
Drive operational risk, security, compliance, business continuity, and disaster recovery activities.
Prepare operational reporting, KPIs, and status updates for management and governance bodies.
Bridge platform development and operations, ensuring new services are production-ready and sustainably operated.




What we expect:

Fluent written and oral communication skills in English.
Ability to work independently and in a senior role.
A proactive, structured, and solution-oriented approach with strong communication and organizational skills.
Ability to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders across multiple organizations.
Proven experience with IT service management (FitSM/ITIL) and service delivery in complex operational environments.
Strong understanding of service performance management, operational governance, risk management, and business continuity.
Hands-on experience coordinating incidents, releases, escalations, and cross-functional operational activities.
Broad technical understanding of modern infrastructure technologies, including cloud, containers, networking, storage, and identity management.
We value strong administration skills with both bare metal Linux servers and kubernetes (or equivalent)
Familiarity with monitoring, automation, CI/CD, and software delivery processes.
Knowledge of security, compliance, and audit practices, preferably including ISO 27001.

Who are we?

We work together for the future of research and learning through advanced technology. Our competence is the platform on which our society researches, learns and remembers. With us, you get to do meaningful work for the digitalisation of Finnish society, not forgetting significant international cooperation projects.




CSC is a non-profit special assignment company owned by the Finnish government and Finnish higher education institutions. We build digital solutions for data management, scientific computing, and education that enable researchers, learners, and companies to understand the world.




Our company’s key resource is our curious personnel with a willingness to learn, and their well-being at work is very important to us. As a community, we work to ensure that well-being, diversity and inclusiveness are embedded in our culture and practices.
