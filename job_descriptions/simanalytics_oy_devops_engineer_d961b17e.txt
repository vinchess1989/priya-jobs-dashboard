DevOps Engineer
Apply for this job

At SimAnalytics, we're building software that helps leading process industry companies make better operational decisions through data, machine learning and process expertise.

We're looking for a DevOps Engineer who enjoys solving complex technical challenges and wants to build reliable, scalable cloud platforms that enable both our developers and our customers to succeed.

You'll join a small, highly skilled engineering team where your work has a direct impact on our product, our customers, and the way we build software.

As part of Kemira Oyj, we combine the agility of a product startup with the stability and long-term vision of an international industrial company.


Why join SimAnalytics?

In this role you'll help build the platform powering our Industrial AI products and have a genuine opportunity to influence how we operate as we continue to grow.

What makes this role unique?

Build cloud-native infrastructure supporting real Industrial AI applications

Work with modern technologies including Kubernetes, Terraform and Azure

Shape our DevOps practices, tooling and platform architecture

Collaborate directly with software developers, data scientists and customer-facing experts

Solve technically challenging problems where software meets industrial production

Have real ownership instead of working in a narrowly defined support role

If you're someone who likes making systems simpler, more reliable and more automated, you'll probably enjoy working with us.



What you'll be doing

As our DevOps Engineer, you'll be responsible for developing and operating the infrastructure behind Factory Harmonizer and our cloud services.

Your work includes:

Building, maintaining and improving our cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes-based platforms

Developing CI/CD pipelines and improving software delivery

Automating infrastructure using Terraform, Helm and related technologies

Improving monitoring, observability and operational reliability

Troubleshooting issues across applications, infrastructure, networking, databases and cloud services

Working closely with developers to improve developer experience and deployment processes

Balancing security, scalability and reliability while keeping delivery practical and agile

Gradually taking ownership of platforms and technical responsibility areas based on your interests and expertise

As our company grows, you'll also have the opportunity to shape how DevOps is done at SimAnalytics.


What we're looking for

We're looking for someone who enjoys taking ownership, learning continuously and solving problems collaboratively.

Ideally you have:

At least three-five years of experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure or System Administration

Experience working with Kubernetes in production environments

Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or similar)

Experience building CI/CD pipelines

Good Linux administration skills

Scripting experience (Bash, Python or similar)

A structured troubleshooting mindset and curiosity to investigate unfamiliar problems

Experience with Git and modern software development workflows

Good communication skills in English (Finnish is a plus)

Most importantly, you're someone who enjoys improving systems and taking responsibility. We don't expect you to know every technology in our stack—we value curiosity and learning just as much as experience.


Our technology stack

You'll work with technologies such as

Microsoft Azure & AWS

Kubernetes & AKS

Terraform

Helm

Azure DevOps Pipelines

Linux & Bash

Python

What you'll get

Besides interesting technical challenges, you'll become part of a team that genuinely enjoys working together.

At SimAnalytics you'll find:

A low-hierarchy culture where good ideas matter more than job titles

A team of experienced engineers who enjoy sharing knowledge

Real ownership and responsibility from day one

Modern technologies and opportunities to keep learning

Flexible remote work within Finland

Salary based on your experience. For this role, the salary range is between 4500€-6000€

The opportunity to influence both technology choices and ways of working

We're looking for people with curiosity and a willingness to roll up their sleeves when needed. We believe the best solutions are built together.


About SimAnalytics

SimAnalytics develops Factory Harmonizer, an advanced industrial analytics platform that combines process expertise, systems thinking and machine learning to improve production performance.

Our customers are global process industry companies that rely on our software to optimize complex production processes.

As part of Kemira Oyj, we combine startup agility with the backing of a traditional industrial company. We're on an ambitious growth journey—and we're looking for people who want to grow with us.


Interested?

Send us your CV or LinkedIn profile and tell us why you'd like to join SimAnalytics.

We review applications continuously and will fill the position as soon as we find the right person.
