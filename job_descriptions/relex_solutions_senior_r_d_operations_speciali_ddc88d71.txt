Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
We're bold thinkers and kind teammates, growing fast but staying grounded. Our Nordic roots and global outlook shape a culture of friendliness, creativity, and collaboration, all powered by openness and shared wins. We care deeply about doing the right thing - and doing it together. We aim to improve the flow of goods, time, and energy - and we know we can't shape the future by clinging to the past. So, we keep evolving, together.

RELEX Solutions delivers a unified supply chain planning platform for retailers and manufacturers, enabled by proven AI technology. We help companies optimize demand forecasting, replenishment, merchandising, pricing and promotions, supply chain operations, and production planning across the end-to-end value chain.

With a global team of over 2,000 professionals, we work side-by-side with our customers to solve real problems with lasting impact. Companies trust RELEX to increase product availability, boost sales, deliver actionable insights, improve sustainability, and drive profitable growth. Join us and make an impact on the world and your career!

In response to our ongoing AI transformation, we are looking for a Senior R&D Operations Specialist to be the person who owns, administers, and evolves the AI developer tools that our engineers depend on every day.

What We Need You To Do And Be

Bring your ideas. Challenge ours. Grow with us.


You will take ownership of the AI and developer tools our R&D organisation adopts — administering, configuring, and managing them at scale across 300–400 engineers
You will monitor tool usage, costs, and anomalies, and put the guardrails in place that keep our AI tooling safe and cost-efficient
You will research how our tools can be configured and evolved to give engineers as much freedom as possible within sensible boundaries
You will stay current on the rapidly-changing AI tooling landscape and bring well-reasoned recommendations to the team
You will work with vendors, internal stakeholders in R&D Operations and Business IT, and the wider engineering community to ensure we are delivering what the organisation needs


What You'll Bring To The Table

Our five values are our playbook. We speak up when things don't make sense. We stop doing what doesn't add value. We stay curious, back each other up, and make space for both innovation and laughter.


Hands-on experience owning or administering at least one AI or developer tool at organisational scale — e.g. ChatGPT Enterprise, GitHub Copilot, Claude / Anthropic, Cursor, or similar
Technical depth: you understand how these tools actually work under the hood, not just as an end user
A self-management mindset — you take ownership, drive things forward without being told what to do, and are comfortable being the person with the most domain knowledge on the team
Ability and willingness to build structure in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment
People skills to navigate stakeholders and develop the vendor management side over time
Comfortable with using AI in your everyday work and developing the use of AI tools as a larger organisational strategy
Fluent in written and spoken English
As a bonus, we consider the experience of working with an international team, plus great communication and organisational skills


What We Offer In Return

You'll be supported and given the space to thrive. We offer meaningful work, flexibility, and a culture that backs you to share your voice and be authentic.


An international career and learning opportunities
A rapidly growing and financially robust software company with exciting opportunities for career growth, also in the future
Flexibility in how and where you work
Room to thrive in a fast-growing global company with a good market position
Practical benefits that support work, family, wellness, and everyday life
Annual leave
Diverse, inclusive, and supportive work culture
Work that meaningfully reduces waste and improves sustainability
Ownership, autonomy and flat hierarchies
Two paid volunteering days each year
Real strategic ownership — you are the person who decides how our AI tools are administered, configured, and evolved. This is not a support function.
You'll be working at the absolute forefront of where software development is going, in a company that has fully committed to the AI transformation
