Why join us?

Joining Avaliance means becoming part of a firm in full growth, supporting its clients on high-impact projects within complex and stimulating environments.

We value expertise, autonomy, and collaboration, while offering a supportive and demanding work environment.

Your role at Avaliance

As a Configuration Manager, you will work on digital transformation projects for multi-sector clients, collaborating with multidisciplinary teams (Cloud, Data, Cyber, Consulting).

Your main responsibilities


Maintain an up-to-date, consistent, and reliable CMDB
Improve the quality of impact analyses during changes and contribute to the change management process
Implement regular CMDB quality controls and audits
Enhance the efficiency of the Change process through better control of CIs


Your profile


Strong command of ITIL / ITSM best practices, particularly Configuration Management
Ability to structure and maintain a usable and reliable CMDB
Contribution to the preparation of Pre-CAB / CAB
Understanding of interactions between CMDB, Incident, Problem, and Change Management
Experience with an ITSM tool integrating a CMDB (EasyVista, ServiceNow, or equivalent)


What we offer you


High value-added projects
Stimulating and collaborative environment
Opportunities for growth and skill development
Training, certifications, and support
Company culture based on trust and excellence
