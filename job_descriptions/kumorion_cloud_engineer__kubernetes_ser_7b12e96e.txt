Help build the future of sovereign cloud infrastructure




At Kumorion, we help cloud service providers, enterprises and data centers build and operate secure, scalable and sovereign cloud infrastructure. Our automation-first approach enables reliable operations and scalable day 2 infrastructure management. We believe infrastructure should be automated, repeatable and easy to operate at scale.




We are now looking for a Cloud Engineer to join our Kubernetes Services team.




In this role, you will help build, automate and operate Kubernetes platforms used in demanding production environments. You will work with cloud-native technologies, infrastructure automation and platform engineering practices to create reliable and scalable services that enable our customers to focus on their own business.




This is an opportunity to work on challenging infrastructure problems, collaborate with experienced engineers and contribute to the development of Kumorion’s Kubernetes service offering.




What you'll do




Design, develop and maintain services and APIs for provisioning, scaling, and lifecycle management of Kubernetes clusters.
Build automation for cluster upgrades, patching, backup/restore and self-healing across multi-tenant environments.
Develop Kubernetes operators, controllers and custom resources (CRDs) to extend platform capabilities.
Implement observability (metrics, logging, tracing), alerting and SLO-driven reliability practices.
Develop automation that improves reliability, scalability and operational efficiency of CI/CD pipelines.
Build and maintain infrastructure as code and GitOps based workflows.




What we're looking for




We know that excellent engineers come from different backgrounds and experiences. If this role sounds interesting to you, we encourage you to apply even if you do not meet every qualification listed below.




You may be a great fit if you have:




Experience working with Kubernetes and containerized environments.
Experience with at least one cloud platform such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, OpenStack or similar.
Experience developing software or automation using Go, Python, TypeScript or another modern programming language.
Experience with infrastructure as code, automation and DevOps practices.
Familiarity with tools such as Terraform, Ansible, ArgoCD and CI/CD platforms.
Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with others.
Professional working proficiency in English.




The following are beneficial but not required:




Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).
Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD).
Experience operating production Kubernetes platforms.
Experience with OpenStack.
Hands-on experience with Talos Linux.




Why Kumorion?




At Kumorion, you will join a team of experienced cloud and platform engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges together. We combine deep technical expertise with a pragmatic approach and a culture of knowledge sharing.




We offer:




The opportunity to work with large-scale cloud and Kubernetes environments.
Meaningful technical challenges and ownership of your work.
A strong learning environment with experienced colleagues.
Support for professional development, certifications and continuous learning.
Hybrid working model and flexible working hours.
A low-hierarchy culture where your ideas and expertise are valued.
An international and inclusive team representing multiple backgrounds, nationalities and perspectives.




In addition, we offer a comprehensive benefits package including:




Private occupational healthcare and insurance.
Lunch, culture, exercise, wellbeing and massage benefits.
Public transportation benefit and city bike season pass.
Home internet benefit.
Paid training, courses and certification bonuses.
Personnel fund and bonus program.
Work equipment of your choice, including phone, headphones and other work-related tools.
