About The Role

We're seeking a Release Manager to coordinate software releases. You'll manage release schedules, coordinate with teams, and ensure smooth deployments to production.

Responsibilities


Plan and coordinate software releases
Manage release schedules and timelines
Coordinate with development and QA teams
Oversee deployment processes
Manage release documentation
Track and mitigate release risks
Conduct release retrospectives
Improve release processes


Requirements


5+ years of release management experience
Strong understanding of software development lifecycle
Experience with CI/CD tools and processes
Knowledge of agile methodologies
Excellent project management skills
Strong communication and coordination abilities
Experience with version control (Git)
Technical background preferred
