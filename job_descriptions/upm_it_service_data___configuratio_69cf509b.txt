We are now looking for an IT Service Data & Configuration Process Owner to join the UPM IT Operational Excellence team. This position can be located in Tampere or Helsinki, Finland.

Greetings from your future manager

“Our ambition in IT Operational Excellence is simple: make IT easier to consume, more resilient and continuously better. Trusted service data is fundamental to that ambition, helping us make better decisions and unlock the potential of ServiceNow, automation and AI.

You will have the opportunity to shape this foundation, challenge how we work today and help turn our ambition into practical, measurable improvements for our users, IT teams and businesses.” - Adrien Lemiere, Director, IT Operational Excellence

What You Will Do


Own the IT Service Data, CMDB and CSDM product vision, strategy and roadmap
Establish governance, ownership and quality standards for service and configuration data across UPM IT
Enable trusted operational reporting, KPI governance and service performance insights through high-quality service data
Drive adoption of the Common Service Data Model (CSDM) and standardized ServiceNow data practices
Partner with service owners, architects and suppliers to improve data quality, service transparency and operational decision-making
Enable automation, AI and observability use cases by ensuring trusted and governed service data foundations
Lead continuous improvement of service data products, reporting capabilities and data-driven governance practices


Who You Are


You have experience with IT service management tools such as ServiceNow, service data, CMDB and configuration management
You understand CSDM principles and their application in service management, reporting and data quality
You have experience in data governance, ownership models, quality controls and KPI reporting
You collaborate effectively with service owners, architects, technical teams and external service providers
You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills and can translate data into actionable insights
You are comfortable establishing governance models, processes and quality standards
You communicate clearly and build productive relationships with internal and external stakeholders
You are motivated to drive continuous improvement and develop in a changing technology environment; ITIL knowledge or certification is considered an advantage
