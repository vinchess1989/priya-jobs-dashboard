Hae 
Tallenna
Technical writers build expert-level evaluation and training data for frontier AI labs. AfterQuery partners with frontier AI labs to build expert-level evaluation and training data. We are hiring technical writers as contractors to review, create, and rank documentation that teaches AI models how to explain complex systems clearly. You will evaluate AI-generated API references, how-to guides, release notes, and user documentation for accuracy, structure, and clarity, and create expert demonstrations drawn from your own practice. Contract and pay The work is paid hourly, fully remote, and fully async - you choose when and how much you work.

Responsibilities


Evaluate AI-generated documentation for accuracy, structure, and clarity
Create expert demonstrations of API references, how-to guides, and release notes
Rank and label model responses against documentation best practices


Required Qualifications


2+ years of professional technical writing experience
Portfolio of documentation you have owned (API docs, guides, manuals, or release notes)
Ability to explain complex technical systems clearly and accurately


Preferred Qualifications


Software, API, or developer documentation experience
Docs-as-code fluency (Markdown, Git, static site generators)
STC certification or a technical degree


Why apply


Fully remote and async - work on your own schedule
Competitive hourly pay for your writing expertise
Shape how frontier AI models explain technical systems
