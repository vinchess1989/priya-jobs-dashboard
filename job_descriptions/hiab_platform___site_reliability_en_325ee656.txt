Your mission

As a Platform & Site Reliability Enfineer you will be a member of the SRE team under Information Management Infrastructure in HIAB. The SRE team consists of consultants and HIAB internal employees, we value them all the same. The team manages and operates all HIAB cloud environments and also participates in operations regarding data centers all over the globe. The position is responsible for developing cloud governance and also supporting internal teams who operate on cloud.

In Finland’s scene our scale is not probably unique, but rare, crossing hundreds of accounts in two public clouds. This forces us to operate in scalable and smart ways, constantly developing our ways of working; both technical and non-technical.

This position is fully remote with occasional travels to Tampere and/or Helsinki, Finland.

Key Responsibilities


Manage, operate, and scale HIAB’s multi-cloud environments (spanning hundreds of accounts across two public clouds) while contributing to global data center operations
Develop and enforce cloud governance frameworks while actively supporting and empowering internal teams operating within the cloud
Drive both technical and non-technical improvements in ways of working to ensure smart, scalable operations across a complex, large-scale infrastructure.


What We Are Looking For


Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field
3+ years of experience in cloud related roles such as SRE specialist, DevOps specialist or cloud specialist
Proven experience managing and developing cloud infrastructure and services, with hands-on expertise in AWS; experience in GCP is a plus
Experience working with software development teams
Strong knowledge of building and managing cloud environments and CICD pipelines.
Experience of writing IaaC with CDK and/or Terraform
Experience in scripting or coding (Bash, Python, Go or something else)
Familiarity with Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) principles, monitoring, automation, and operational best practices.
Experience in containering applications and running them, k8s experience is a plus
Experience in AI assisted development
High degree of self-motivation, initiative, and adaptability in a dynamic environment.
