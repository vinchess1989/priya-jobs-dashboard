Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Are you ready to take real ownership of Smart Grid products – and shape what they become as we scale internationally?

At Advian, we are looking for a Product Lead for GRIDLYTICS® to take ownership of the direction and development of our Smart Grid products.

This is not a fine-tuning role. GRIDLYTICS® is entering an exciting phase of growth and product development, giving you a real opportunity to influence what we build, which customer problems we solve and how we turn them into scalable products.

You will work closely with electricity distribution companies, combining your understanding of electricity networks with customer insight and commercial thinking to drive product priorities and roadmap. As GRIDLYTICS® grows, you will play a key role in taking our products to international markets.

ADVIAN

Advian builds advanced data and technology solutions for the energy and manufacturing sectors. We combine deep industry expertise with data, analytics, AI and software development to solve complex operational challenges and turn data into better decisions.

Our energy business focuses on helping electricity distribution companies make better use of their data and develop smarter, more resilient networks. Our flat and self-directed culture gives you the autonomy to make an impact while working with highly skilled colleagues.

THE ROLE

As Product Lead, you will own the GRIDLYTICS® product direction and roadmap, turning customer, project and market insights into clear priorities for development. You will balance immediate customer needs with building scalable products for the longer term.

Your responsibilities include:


Owning and prioritizing the product roadmap
Identifying and validating customer needs and product opportunities
Translating electricity distribution network domain needs into development priorities
Working closely with customers, developers, data specialists and other experts
Supporting sales, customer meetings and product presentations
Keeping sales, marketing and development aligned on product direction and priorities



What We Are Looking For

We would fit each other perfectly if you have:


Experience in product ownership, product management or a similar role
Experience in gathering and prioritising requirements, combined with strong commercial thinking
Strong understanding of electricity networks, including their technical aspects, operations, maintenance and planning
Understanding of the energy sector, including flexibility, security of supply and the regulatory environment
Good customer-facing skills and experience in pre-sales and customer relationship management
A proactive, commercially minded approach and the ability to make progress in a fast-moving, evolving environment
Fluent English



It is considered a plus if you have:


Knowledge of Finnish or German is considered an advantage
Understanding of data and analytics
A software development background or other strong technical understanding



WHY ADVIAN

This is an opportunity to take real ownership of a Smart Grid product family at a stage where your decisions can still have a major impact on what the products become. You won’t be stepping into a finished roadmap – you will help define it, working directly with electricity distribution companies to decide which problems we solve, what we productize and how we scale GRIDLYTICS® internationally.

We also offer you, among other things:


Real influence over the direction and development of GRIDLYTICS®
The opportunity to work with electricity distribution companies across our target markets
Hybrid and flexible ways of working
A low-hierarchy and self-directed culture
A supportive team of highly skilled professionals



Read more about Advian and co-Advians on our website.
