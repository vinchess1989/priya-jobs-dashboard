We are looking for a Systems Integration Manager to join our client Cimcorp and operate at the intersection of software development, hands-on delivery, and change leadership. You will be a part of a Cimcorp’s Customer Support (CS) Project Management team and have an opportunity to shape how modern software delivery and agile ways of working are driven across diverse industrial automation projects. If you are interested, read more and apply today.

About the role

As a Systems Integration Manager, you act as the central operational bridge between project management, software development, site operations, and subcontractors. Your core mission is to establish and maintain structured agile practices across multiple simultaneous delivery projects, ensuring transparent tracking and smooth handovers. You will work closely with project managers, software engineers, and automation leads to plan software tasks, resolve resourcing bottlenecks, and support commissioning and testing phases.

We are looking for a person who thrives at the intersection of software development and hands-on delivery and has proactive and collaborative mindset.

You are offered

The opportunity to work in an innovative and global company that serves customers around the world
Opportunity to build and harmonize ways of working
Support of high competent and experienced team
Flexible hybrid work and work community and culture that supports your well-being and professional development

Work tasks

Acting as a Scrum Master across delivery projects: facilitating sprint planning, leading ceremonies, tracking progress, and managing backlogs
Building, configuring, and maintaining the Jira environment in close cooperation with team stakeholders
Preparing and updating project commissioning plans and breaking project functionalities into concrete software milestones
Participating in testing and supporting site operations during commissioning phases
Driving continuous improvement and change management by mentoring developers and harmonizing ways of working across project deliveries

We are looking for

Advanced practical knowledge of agile methodologies with a proven track record of facilitating sprints, backlogs, and agile delivery within software teams
Good practical experience with Jira, including setup, issue tracking, and workflow configuration
Practical experience working in software development or software delivery environments
Excellent interpersonal skills and collaborative, solution-oriented and proactive mindset
Fluent communication skills in English, both written and spoken (Finnish language skills are considered an asset)
