Tallenna
Blend Technologies is a high-growth Technology and Innovation powerhouse. We orchestrate bespoke solutions for the most complex challenges in the global tech landscape.

Our expertise is deepened across six critical pillars designed to modernize and protect mission-critical systems:


Cloud & Infrastructure: Building resilient, scalable foundations for the hybrid era
Next-Gen Networks: Powering connectivity with speed and architectural precision
DevOps & Automation: Accelerating delivery cycles through intelligent workflows
AI & Data: Transforming raw information into predictive, actionable intelligence
Cyber Security & Resilience: Moving beyond defense to proactive, stress-tested immunity
Managed Services & IT Operations: Ensuring seamless performance so you can focus on your core mission


We possess a profound understanding of the technologies shaping the future—not just their capabilities, but their nuances and hazards. Our mastery lies in integration: the right combination, in the right sequence, at the right time. We don't just bridge the gap between today and tomorrow; we ensure our clients organization's journey is secure, sustainable, and strategically sound.

We are now looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer with strong technical skills and solid working experience to work in new challenging projects.

Responsibilities


Perform analysis of best practices and emerging concepts in DevOps, Infrastructure Automation
Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines to automate application deployments and testing.
Manage containerized environments for deploying applications using technologies such as Docker.
Set up monitoring and logging solutions for proactive issue detection and troubleshooting.
Maintain and optimize monitoring and alerting systems.
Implement security best practices throughout the DevOps processes.
Collaborate with development and operations teams to streamline workflows and resolve technical challenges.
Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for infrastructure and configurations.
Ensure documentation is up-to-date and accessible to relevant teams


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, or related field (or equivalent work experience).
Minimum 4 years of hands-on experience in DevOps, System Administration or even Development background.
Experience working on a docker environment with tools (Jenkins, Bitbucket, Gitlab, Nexus)
Experience in automation tools and scripting languages (e.g. Bash, PowerShell).
Knowledge of Cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP)
Knowledge of Configuration Management tools (Ansible, Chef, Puppet)
Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform or equivalent)
Knowledge of Docker/Kubernetes
Strong understanding of networking, security, and compliance.
Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting abilities

Medical plan and life insurance for you and your family.
Continuous training & learning opportunities via personalized seminars.
The opportunity to work in a diverse environment with talented colleagues
