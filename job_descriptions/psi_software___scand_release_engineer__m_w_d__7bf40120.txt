The PSI Group develops software products for optimizing the flow of energy and materials for utilities and industry. As an independent software producer with more than 2,300 employees, PSI has been a technology leader since 1969 for process control systems that ensure sustainable energy supply, production and logistics by combining AI methods with industrially proven optimization methods. The innovative industry products can be operated on-premises or in the cloud.

We are looking for you to join our test & release team of the Grid & Energy Management business unit.

Tasks inspiring you

As a Release Engineer (m/w/d) your mission is to ensure the reliable, secure and repeatable delivery of software releases across one or more product lines or system components. You create transparency around release content, coordinate technical and organizational processes and ensure controlled, quality-assured delivery of product increments to customers and internal stakeholders. You operate across teams, components, and environments, safeguarding the release process end-to-end.


Plan and manage end-to-end release processes and schedules
Coordinate build and deployment pipelines with DevOps and Engineering
Conduct release readiness and go/no-go meetings
Ensure compliance with security, quality and documentation standards
Manage release risks and continuous process improvement


Qualifications That Inspire Us


At least 5 years of professional experience in Release Engineering, DevOps or related roles
Experience with CI/CD systems (e.g., GitLab CI, BitBucket, Jenkins)
Knowledge of artifact repositories and security scanning tools (SAST/DAST/SCA)
Experience working in multi-team, complex system environments
Strong communication and coordination skills
