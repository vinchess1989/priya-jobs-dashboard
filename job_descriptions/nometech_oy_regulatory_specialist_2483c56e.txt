CEO | Medical Device Quality & Regulatory Compliance Expert | FDA, EU MDR

Company Description Nometech Oy supports companies in managing quality, product development, and regulatory challenges for medical technology and other products. The company brings strong expertise in U.S. FDA markets, ISO 13485, MDSAP, CE marking under EU MDR, EU IVDR and medical device development and validation. Nometech Oy also serves organizations in other industries by implementing and optimizing ISO 9001 quality management systems. Its services integrate training, quality consulting, regulatory compliance, and product development into a seamless process guiding projects from concept to market and beyond. Nometech Oy offers practical, client-focused support to help organizations strengthen regulatory strategies and quality systems.




Role Description The Regulatory Specialist will work in a hybrid arrangement based in Oulu, with flexibility to work from home. In this role, the specialist will interpret and apply regulatory requirements, prepare and review technical documentation, and support regulatory submissions for medical devices and other products. Day-to-day responsibilities include monitoring regulatory changes, advising clients on compliance strategies, and contributing to the implementation and improvement of quality management systems such as ISO 13485 and ISO 9001. The role involves collaborating with internal teams and clients, supporting audits and inspections, and contributing to training materials and regulatory guidance. The Regulatory Specialist will help ensure that client projects move efficiently from concept to market in alignment with applicable regulations and standards.




Qualifications

Candidates should possess strong skills in Regulatory Compliance and Regulatory Requirements, with experience interpreting for example FDA, EU MDR, EU IVDR and relevant ISO standards.
Candidates should possess solid Analytical Skills to review technical documentation, assess risk, and evaluate regulatory strategies.
Candidates should possess effective Communication skills to explain complex regulatory topics clearly and collaborate with clients and cross-functional teams.
Candidates should possess experience in Regulatory Affairs, including preparation of submissions, support for audits, and interaction with regulatory bodies.
Relevant qualifications such as a degree in engineering, life sciences, pharmacy, or a related field are beneficial.
Experience with medical device development, validation, and quality management systems (ISO 13485, ISO 9001)
Ability to work independently, manage multiple projects, and maintain attention to detail in a client-focused environment is preferred.
Professional proficiency in English; Finnish language skills are considered an asset.
