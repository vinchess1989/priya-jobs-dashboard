Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to help deliver solutions for business-critical banking applications, particularly with a newly formed Availability Layer platform, which relies on existing building blocks of Communication Layer (Kafka), Snowflake and AWS S3. This is a full-time permanent position that may require participation in the on-call rotation.

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We're not just a bank, we're a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

Meet the Integration SRE team. Our role is to provide, automate, and support IT infrastructure platforms based on a modern IT toolset to drive highly available and cost-efficient applications.

What You'll Be Doing

Main responsibilities in this role:


Building new infrastructure components, based on cloud platforms and tools, such as AWS EKS and S3, Terraform Enterprise and Snowflake
Automating infrastructure deployment and configuration
Acting as a Site Reliability Engineer to support service issues, including a potential participation in an on-call rotation
Working closely with team members and other stakeholders across multiple countries
Expanding your knowledge and horizons by having access to a large environment and learning from fellow technology-obsessed colleagues


Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you have a good mix of deep technical and soft skills and strive to continuously improve our services.

Your background and skills include:


Experience with various DevOps solutions
Knowledge of cloud platforms, particularly AWS
Experience automating infrastructure and processes using tools such as Terraform, Jenkins and Argo CD
Understanding SQL and data structures and formats, such as Apache Iceberg
Source control using Git
Monitoring with Prometheus/Victoria Metrics/Grafana
Python, Bash, or another scripting language
Good English skills


It would be ideal if you also:


Prior experience with Kafka
