Why This Role Matters

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you’ll design and operate the core platform foundations that power ICEYE’s products, enabling secure, scalable, and reliable services across cloud and on-prem environments.

Who We Are

ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE’s world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world’s largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy, day or night and in any weather, supported by ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits.

As a trusted partner for defense, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE’s near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. Designed for dual use, the platform also serves civil protection and commercial users for natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring including oil-spill detection, and finance, contributing to global security and community resilience.

ICEYE is headquartered in Finland and operates globally across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We have more than 900 employees, united by a shared vision: improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation.

Your Day-to-day Responsibilities


Design, operate, and evolve core platform components including Kubernetes, ingress, service mesh, and secrets management
Build and maintain reusable infrastructure modules using Terraform across AWS and on-prem environments
Define and implement observability standards, including metrics, logs, traces, dashboards, and alerting
Partner with product teams to guide platform adoption and enable scalable, platform-aligned architectures
Automate platform lifecycle processes including provisioning, upgrades, and scaling
Improve platform reliability, performance, and security through continuous iteration
Support and mentor engineers on best practices in Kubernetes, networking, identity, and observability
Reduce operational overhead through tooling, automation, and improved platform abstractions


Must Haves

What we’re looking for


5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering roles operating production systems
Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes, including cluster operations, networking, and lifecycle management
Solid understanding of AWS fundamentals including networking, IAM, compute, and storage
Experience with infrastructure as code using tools such as Terraform
Practical experience with observability, including SLOs, metrics, logs, traces, and alerting
Proficiency in at least one backend language such as Python or Go
Strong Linux systems knowledge and troubleshooting skills
Ability to collaborate across teams and communicate effectively in English


Nice To Haves


Experience with Rancher, Istio, External Secrets, or Vault
Experience operating in restricted or air-gapped environments
Experience with identity systems such as FusionAuth or SSO integrations
Experience in regulated or mission-critical environments
Experience with hybrid infrastructure across AWS and on-prem platforms
