We are now looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join Aufwin Defence Systems

In this role, you will play a key part in designing and developing next-generation defence software platforms that enable seamless interoperability between multiple systems, organizations, and operational domains.

You will contribute to the development of open architecture solutions that connect sensors, command-and-control systems, autonomous platforms, simulation environments, data services, and decision-support capabilities into unified and scalable operational ecosystems.

The role combines software architecture, hands-on development, systems integration, DevSecOps, and advanced engineering practices. You will work in a multidisciplinary environment where software is a critical enabler of mission effectiveness, resilience, and operational agility.

As a senior member of the engineering team, you will help shape technical direction, mentor colleagues, and contribute to building reusable capabilities that can be deployed across multiple defence programs and customer environments.

You will work closely with software engineers, systems engineers, security specialists, AI developers, customers, and our strategic partners.

You are offered

A unique opportunity to contribute to the development of next-generation defence and security capabilities
A senior role with significant technical influence and architectural responsibility
The opportunity to work on advanced multi-domain technologies and real-world operational challenges
Support from highly experienced engineering and defence professionals
Competitive compensation and benefits package including a possibility for partnership
Long-term career opportunities in a growing and innovative technology company
Access to modern development tools, technologies, and engineering practices

Work tasks

As a Senior Software Engineer, your responsibilities will include:

Designing and architecting open architecture software platforms and System-of-Systems solutions
Developing reusable software components, APIs, middleware, and integration services
Building scalable software frameworks that support multiple products, programs, and customer environments
Designing interfaces between command-and-control systems, sensors, autonomous platforms, Battle Management Systems  and external partners
Developing distributed, cloud-native, edge-computing, and mission-critical software solutions
Working with real-time data streams, data fusion, messaging frameworks, and decision-support systems
Developing and maintaining DevSecOps and CI/CD pipelines for secure and reliable software delivery
Collaborating with systems engineering teams to translate operational requirements into technical solutions in close collaboration with very experienced defence specialists
Ensuring cybersecurity, resilience, and interoperability are integrated into system architectures
Supporting system integration, verification, validation, and deployment activities
Troubleshooting and solving complex technical challenges across large-scale integrated systems
Contributing to software quality, coding standards, architecture governance, and engineering best practices
Supporting and mentoring other engineers while contributing to a strong technical culture
Participating in customer engagement, technical workshops, demonstrations, and proof-of-concept activities

 We are looking for

Proven experience designing and architecting large-scale software solutions intended for reuse across multiple products, programs, or operational environments
Strong hands-on software engineering skills and the ability to write, review, and debug production-quality code
Experience with software architecture, distributed systems, and service-oriented or microservice-based architectures
Strong programming skills in Python, C++, React and TypeScript, and similar modern software development languages
Experience in developing APIs, integration platforms, middleware, and software frameworks
Knowledge of cloud-native technologies, containerization, and orchestration platforms such as Docker and Kubernetes
Experience with DevSecOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and modern software delivery processes
Understanding of systems engineering principles and complex systems integration
Experience working with data-intensive, mission-critical, or high-availability software environments
Familiarity with cybersecurity principles and secure software development practices
Experience with message-oriented architectures, event-driven systems, and real-time data processing is considered an advantage
Experience in defence, aerospace, security, autonomous systems, telecommunications, industrial systems, or other highly regulated technology sectors is considered an advantage
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities
Excellent collaboration and communication skills
Full working proficiency in English
Ability to obtain and maintain a Finnish security clearance

About Aufwin Defence Systems

Aufwin Defence Systems develops advanced software and systems solutions that enable interoperability, resilience, and mission effectiveness across modern operational Defence environments. Our focus is on Open Architecture All-Domain System-of-Systems capabilities that allow Defence organizations, Homeland Security, Critical Infrastructure providers and varying Government entities to integrate diverse technologies into a coherent operational ecosystem.

We believe the future belongs to adaptable, software-defined capabilities that can evolve rapidly as operational requirements change. Our multidisciplinary teams combine deep engineering expertise with a strong understanding of real-world operational challenges to deliver innovative and effective solutions.
