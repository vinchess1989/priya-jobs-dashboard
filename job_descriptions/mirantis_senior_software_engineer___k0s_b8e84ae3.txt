About Mirantis

Mirantis is the Kubernetes-native AI infrastructure company, enabling organizations to build and operate scalable, secure, and sovereign infrastructure for modern AI, machine learning, and data-intensive applications. By combining open source innovation with deep expertise in Kubernetes orchestration, Mirantis empowers platform engineering teams to deliver composable, production-ready developer platforms across any environment—on-premises, in the cloud, at the edge, or in sovereign data centers. As enterprises navigate the growing complexity of AI-driven workloads, Mirantis delivers the automation, GPU orchestration, and policy-driven control needed to manage infrastructure with confidence and agility. Committed to open standards and freedom from lock-in, Mirantis ensures that customers retain full control of their infrastructure strategy.

Job Description

We’re seeking a Software Engineer with a passion for shaping the future of k0sproject. As a CNCF Sandbox project, k0s is an open source, all-inclusive Kubernetes distribution, which is configured with all of the features needed to build a Kubernetes cluster and packaged as a single binary for ease of use. We’re tackling an interesting problem: How do we package and operate Kubernetes itself in the simplest possible way but still allowing enough flexibility for varying use cases.

Within Mirantis, you’ll join a remote team of fellow maintainers for k0s and related project as well as the larger OSS ecosystem maintainers. We’re looking for someone within European timezones.

As part of Mirantis, you’ll also help shape our Kubernetes and container ecosystem products that rely on various upstream projects as well as collaborate on other upstream projects.

Responsibilities:


Develop and maintain upstream k0s related projects under the CNCF umbrella
Design and implement
Integrating new features from the cloud native technologies landscape that compliment the Mirantis Kubernetes products
Following the latest developments in the container technology ecosystem and identifying matters that could impact the product roadmap
Act as a cloud native software and technologies subject matter expert and evangelist within the company and broader community


Qualifications


Strong Kubernetes experience, the deeper the better
Experience in Linux, VMs, networking, containers and the general tech stack that makes cloud-native tick
Ability to write clean and maintainable (Golang) code
Strong engineering background with meaningful track record on Github
You value pragmatism and result-oriented team and project work
Good communication skills in English


The tech stack you’ll be working with includes things like:


Kubernetes
Golang
Linux
VMs (Lima, qemu, kvm, etc.)
GitHub Actions
k0s (https://k0sproject.io/)
