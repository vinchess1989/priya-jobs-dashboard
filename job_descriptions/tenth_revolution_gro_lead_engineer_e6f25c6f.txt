Lead Software Developer (Industrial Systems & SCADA) - Helsinki




We're supporting a major Finnish organisation undergoing a significant transformation of its Operational Technology (OT) and SCADA software development capability.




This is not a traditional software development role and it's not a typical SCADA engineering position either.




The organisation has spent the last few years building the foundations of a modern software engineering function around critical industrial systems. They are now looking for a Lead Software Developer who can take ownership of how software is designed, developed, tested and maintained across their SCADA and operational environments.




Many industrial environments still rely heavily on vendor-led development, manual processes and limited software engineering standards. This role is about changing that.




You'll play a key role in bringing modern development practices into a highly business-critical environment, helping shape everything from source control and testing processes through to development standards, documentation, architecture and long-term technical direction. The systems you work on directly support industrial operations and critical infrastructure, making this a unique opportunity to combine modern software engineering with real-world operational impact.




Responsibilities:

- Provide technical leadership for SCADA and operational software development initiatives.

- Help define software development best practices, quality standards and engineering processes.

- Design, develop and maintain software components, integrations and operator-facing applications.

- Drive improvements around testing, version control, software quality and maintainability.

- Collaborate closely with operational stakeholders, control room teams, automation specialists and business users.

- Work with external technology vendors while helping strengthen internal development capability.

- Contribute to long-term architecture decisions and future technology direction.

What We're Looking For

We're interested in candidates who combine modern software engineering experience with exposure to industrial, automation or operational technology environments.




You'll likely bring experience in areas such as:

- SCADA systems

- Operational Technology (OT)

- Industrial software development

- Process automation

- Manufacturing systems

- Energy, utilities or industrial environments

- Industrial integrations and communication protocols




You should also have experience with modern engineering practices including:

- Git-based version control

- Automated testing

- CI/CD pipelines

- Software documentation and governance

- AI-assisted software development tools

- Modern development workflows

- Knowledge of industrial communication protocols such as OPC UA, IEC-104, ICCP or Modbus would be highly beneficial.




Additional Advantage

- Siemens automation technologies

- WinCC OA

- Control room systems

- Industrial HMI development

- Databricks

- Data platform integration

- Critical infrastructure environments

- Energy production systems







Success in this role requires more than technical expertise. You'll be someone who can:

- Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Challenge existing ways of working and introduce improvements.

- Create structure where processes may not yet exist.

- Influence technical direction without relying on formal management authority.

- Work comfortably in a flat organisation with high levels of collaboration.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to move beyond pure development and become a key technical leader in the evolution of a modern industrial software engineering function.
