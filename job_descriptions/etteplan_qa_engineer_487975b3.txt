We are continuously looking for QA Engineers who can handle manual testing and test automation, to join our Tampere office and work with our industrial customers.

We operate in a hybrid working model, with approximately three days per week either at the customer site or at an Etteplan office.

Key Responsibilities


Define test cases based on end-customer requirements
Support project delivery and implementation activities
Ensure proper documentation, reporting, and change management processes


Skills And Experience


At least a few years of experience in software testing
Relevant technical degree: B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Engineering or equivalent
Ability to understand complex technical systems and support subject matter experts in project execution
Proactive and self-driven working style, with the ability to work independently while also building strong relationships with various stakeholders
Working as a part of scrum team
Fluent written and spoken English is essential, Finnish language is not required but can be an advantage
Familiar with embedded device testing


We also appreciate


Broad technical background or experience across multiple engineering disciplines
Experience with test automation and CI/CD pipeline management
Working knowledge of at least one programming language commonly used in automation or testing environments, such as Python or C#
Knowledge of Robot Framework
Familiarity with Azure DevOps
AI-assisted software development / quality assurance


Why join us?


Opportunity to work with leading industrial customers
A key role in a large-scale software development project
Excellent opportunities for professional growth and career development


The salary range is quite wide, reflecting our openness to both earlier‑career and more experienced professionals. What matters most to us is finding the right competencies and fit, and aligning the role and compensation to match.
