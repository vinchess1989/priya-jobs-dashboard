Job Overview

We build software solutions to enable future Arm silicon products by adapting and integrating multiple open-source and internal components. To enable fast-paced progress, we operate in a tight release cycle built on a sophisticated CI/CD framework. We are looking for an experienced release manager to own the release automation process, supported by a small team of test engineers, working together with the development teams.

Responsibilities


Deliver SW releases on schedule with defined scope and quality. Transition to continuous delivery.
Ensure reliable operation of the CI/CD pipelines and improve their efficiency.
Contribute to the test strategy and coordinate its execution.
Drive the development of test frameworks and automation tools.
Provide technical leadership to the QA and testing team.


Required Skills and Experience :


Experience with test and release automation, continuous integration and delivery.
Detailed understanding of cloud-based CI/CD implementations, preferably GitLab CI.
Confident with python and shell scripting, able to read C.
Practical understanding of test methodology and implementation.
Strong interpersonal skills, ability to mentor junior colleagues.


“Nice To Have” Skills and Experience :


Specific experience with Yocto, embedded Linux and firmware.
Contributions to open-source projects.
People management is optional in this role.


In Return:

Join us and make a real impact on the quality of Arm's products! This is your chance to grow, develop new skills, and build a successful career at a leading technology company.
