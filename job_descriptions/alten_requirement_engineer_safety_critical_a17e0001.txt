Requirement Engineer – Safety Critical Software (External)

Role Overview
Requirement Engineer responsible for defining and managing safety-critical software requirements in the elevator domain, ensuring traceability and compliance with functional safety standards.

Key Responsibilities
- Define, analyze, and maintain SW/system requirements
- Ensure traceability (requirements <-> design <-> tests)
- Perform impact analysis and support requirement reviews
- Maintain high-quality, testable requirement documentation
- Support audits and certification activities

Required Competence
- Experience in requirement engineering (embedded or safety-critical domain)
- Knowledge of IEC 61508 (or similar safety standards)
- Experience with requirement management tools
- Experience in elevator domain or similar safety-regulated industrial systems
- Strong analytical and documentation skills
