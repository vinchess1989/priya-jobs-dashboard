As the product security lead, you will help us to develop highly secure positioning products and make sure that our chips, modules and SW products are meeting our customers’ expectations being the best-in-class what comes to the security features. You will be in active co-operation with our embedded SW and HW R&D teams across Europe, have the responsibility to oversee that our R&D addresses the security features with diligence, and you ensure that the deliverables meet the expected security requirements. Your involvement with the platform and products starts already from the drawing board and ends when the product line has reached the end of the cycle.




In this role, you will be working also with the other u-blox Product Centers and the Corporate Security Team for holistic e2e-security solutions and strategies.




This role can be located in Espoo or Tampere, Finland or Thalwil, Switzerland.




Your Responsibilities

Own and lead product security and related activities for the Business Unit
Roll out, implement, and support product security policies, including CRA and ISO21434 compliance.
Guide product teams on security requirements across the full product lifecycle
Represent the Business Unit in corporate and cross‑product security forums
Lead security classification, architecture, and risk assessments (TARA)
Support product and platform security gate reviews
Interface with customers and suppliers on product security topics
Track and drive resolution of product security risks and issues
Promote security awareness and provide hands‑on guidance and training




Your Skills and Experience




Soft skills

Clear communication and negotiation skills
Ability to create a vision and a break-down plan to achieve it
Ability to work in a team




Technical/Hard Skills

Knowledge in product security
Knowledge in defining and ramping up tool chains for test and development




Leadership skills

Autonomous and transparent coordination of cross-functional tasks and activities




Other

Experience in testing techniques
Good English skills, oral and in writing

A start-up and innovation mindset while in the process of scaling-up processes and efficiencies
