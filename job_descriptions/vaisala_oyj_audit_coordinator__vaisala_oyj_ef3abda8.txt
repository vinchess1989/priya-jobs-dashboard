Join a world-class quality team at Vaisala as an Audit Coordinator. In this dynamic role, you will gain a holistic view of our operations and drive continuous improvement across the organization.

About the role

As an Audit Coordinator, you will play a coordinating role in our quality team, ensuring that internal and external audits run smoothly. You will collaborate closely with various departments, guiding them through audit findings and continuous improvement projects.

You are offered
We offer a highly autonomous role within a supportive team, featuring a flexible hybrid work model based in Vantaa. This position provides an opportunity to learn the core principles of quality assurance and continuous improvement while gaining a deeper understanding of global business operations.

Work tasks

This role focuses on coordinating, planning, and executing internal and external audits, facilitating quality improvement processes, and maintaining close contact with internal stakeholders across the organization.

Coordinate internal and external audits with external partners and internal stakeholders.
Manage audit planning, scheduling, arrangements, and facilitate audit meetings.
Follow up on audit findings and support auditees with root-cause analysis.
Liaise internally across departments to ensure smooth audit execution.
Support and monitor compliance with standards like ISO 9001, HSE, and security.
Manage audit documentation and corrective actions in English in collaboration with audit participants.

We are looking for

Educational background in Industrial Engineering, International Business, or a related field.
Professional fluency in English, both written and spoken.
Prior experience working within a corporate environment.
Excellent communication, coordination, and interpersonal skills.

It is meritorious if you have

Knowledge of Finnish
Previous experience in auditing or quality-related roles.
Familiarity with quality standards such as ISO 9001.

Energetic
