Recruitment Manager at Akkodis – My team is finding the best talent to join Akkodis in Aerospace & Defense, Life Sciences & Healthcare and…



We are looking for a Configuration and Data Management Specialist to join our Akkodis Aerospace team, working onsite at the European Space Agency in the Netherlands.




Akkodis has proudly supported the Aerospace & Defence industry in the Netherlands for decades. Our partnership with the European Space Agency (ESA) has been one of the many success stories allowing our people to expand their career horizons. Akkodis experts are contributing to some of the most important technical advances in Aerospace and Defence which impact the way we live our lives today, through specialised engineering expertise and other required skillsets. This could be your future. Become part of our successful team!




Role overview:

As CADM Specialist, you will manage Configuration and Data Management activities within ESA programmes and projects. You will ensure the integrity of project baselines, support reviews and configuration boards, and contribute to the development of CADM processes, tools and standards.




About the team:

The service provides project management and business support to ESA's Commercialisation and Industry Partnership (CIP) Directorate, covering project administration, planning and scheduling, risk management, configuration and data management (CADM), documentation management, reporting, and governance activities.

The selected candidates will support ESA programme and project teams throughout the planning, execution, monitoring and control of space-related programmes and projects, ensuring compliance with ESA processes, standards and performance objectives while contributing to the successful delivery of Directorate activities.




Tasks to be performed:

Configuration Planning & Management:

Act as a focal point for configuration management interfaces with project management, engineering, product assurance, and safety teams.
Ensure effective management of project documentation, engineering requirements, and configuration baselines.
Support change control processes and prepare Configuration Control Board (CCB) meetings.
Maintain records of Change Requests, Contract Change Notices, and other relevant configuration management documentation.
Assist in management audits and configuration verification processes.
Support project teams with CADM processes and industry interfaces.




Review Coordination & Support:

Coordinate and support review logistics, ensuring a harmonized CADM process across projects.
Act as a focal point for review logistics, interfacing with project teams, suppliers, and reviewers.
Manage electronic Review Item Discrepancy tracking systems (eRID), ensuring compliance with security requirements.
Verify completeness and registration of data packs, ensuring timely distribution and issue resolution.
Provide technical support during review cycles, including RID tracking, disposition, and follow-ups.
Compile review close-out reports and capture lessons learned for CADM improvements.




CADM Process Development & Training:

Define and update CADM plans, ensuring standardization across projects.
Develop CADM implementation requirements for industrial contracts.
Create and maintain CADM processes, standard operating procedures, and user guides.
Provide training to users and stakeholders on CADM processes and tools.
Support automation and efficiency improvements in CADM processes.
Contribute to working groups on CADM methodologies and emerging technologies (e.g., MBSE, AI).




To successfully perform the above mentioned tasks, the following background / experience is required:




Bachelor’s Degree in a relevant discipline with at least 7+ years of experience in Configuration Management within the space industry or a similar multinational engineering industry.
Experience with documentation and baseline management systems.
Experience with information management systems.
Experience with ESA Configuration Management requirements and standards (e.g., MS-ESA-RQ-100, MS-ESA-RQ-108, ECSS-M40) would be an asset.
Knowledge of space programs, configuration management procedures, and supporting tools would be an asset.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to interact with international engineering, technical, and business teams.
Highly organized with strong attention to detail, flexibility, and ability to manage priorities under high workload conditions.
Proficiency in English (both written and spoken).







About ESA-ESTEC




The European Space Agency (ESA) has sites in several European countries.

The European Space Research and Technology Centre (ESTEC), has grown into ESA’s largest establishment and the technical and organizational hub of Europe’s space sector. Missions designed and tested at ESTEC are circling Earth, have landed on planetary bodies and have probed far into the Solar System.

The infrastructure and equipment assembled here, together with the expert knowhow of its 2800 personnel, make ESTEC a unique resource for Europe. The work done here by teams skilled in every aspect of engineering for space has enabled the creation of novel communication, navigation and information services, creating new jobs and growth while improving the lives of European and world citizens.
