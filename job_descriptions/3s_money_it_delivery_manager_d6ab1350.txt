IT Delivery Manager

Riga

Hybrid – 2 days working from home.




We’re looking for an IT Delivery Manager to join our growing office in Riga. You’ll be an essential part of our growing IT team, helping us develop new technical solutions for multiple digital touch-points across the business.




Our mission

3S Money is reinventing international business banking. Founded in 2018, our mission is to help growing businesses send, receive and exchange money in over 190 countries. With offices in seven countries and over 200 employees, you’ll be joining a high-energy, high-impact team that’s achieved profitability and has a wealth of opportunity ahead.




Our culture

At 3S Money, we believe in caring personally, staying curious and making an impact through collaboration not competition. Our culture is genuinely transparent with minimal hierarchy and a commitment to each employee’s professional growth.




What you’ll be doing

Lead the end-to-end delivery of products, integrations, and system changes.
Manage projects from requirements and planning through development, UAT, release, and production rollout.
Coordinate cross-functional teams including Product, Engineering, QA, Operations, Compliance, and external partners.
Manage delivery plans, priorities, dependencies, risks, and blockers.
Ensure solutions meet business, regulatory, compliance, and operational requirements.
Drive release planning and ensure production readiness and successful go-live.
Track delivery progress and provide clear status updates to stakeholders.
Support incident resolution and continuous improvement of existing products and processes.
Improve delivery processes, team collaboration, and overall delivery efficiency.




About you, you have

3–5+ years of experience in Delivery Management, Project Management, or a similar role.
Experience in fintech, payments, banking, or financial services.
Proven experience delivering IT/software projects from requirements through production release.
Experience working with cross-functional technical and business teams.
Understanding of Agile/Scrum delivery methodologies.
Experience managing multiple projects, priorities, dependencies, and stakeholders simultaneously.
Experience working with external technology providers, banks, or payment partners is an advantage.
Good understanding of APIs, system integrations, and software development lifecycle; coding experience is not required.




Tech:

Strong technical background in software/IT systems.
Understanding of APIs, integrations, databases, and data flows.
Experience working with Developers, QA, DevOps, and Architects.
Fintech/payments technical experience is highly desirable.




In return for your hard work:

💰 Salary starting from €4,000 + plus stock options (after 12 months) & benefits

💻 Hybrid working setup in our wonderful city-centre office

📚 We foster non-BAU projects in our team, we want you to explore new avenues and expand your expertise to benefit your professional pathway & our teams.

+ And much, much more….




Our commitment to you

We are an equal opportunities employer.

We’re creating services for everyone, so it’s imperative we work with everyone. Our diverse team has been the foundations to our success in building more efficient products, making better decisions and establishing an all-inclusive place to work. Applicants of all backgrounds are hugely encouraged to apply and discover the 3S way.
