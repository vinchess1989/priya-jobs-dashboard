Developer Documentation Specialist at BairesDev

As a Developer Documentation Specialist, you will produce high-quality technical content that empowers developers to integrate and use complex software solutions effectively. You will streamline the flow of information by transforming intricate architectural details into clear, actionable documentation.

What You'll Do


Author and maintain comprehensive API documentation and developer guides to ensure a seamless onboarding experience.
Design and structure internal knowledge management systems to improve information discoverability across engineering teams.
Create clear, concise technical content using Markdown and modern documentation frameworks.
Collaborate with engineers and product managers to capture technical nuances and system requirements accurately.
Standardize documentation workflows and versioning practices.
Review and refine existing technical content to maintain accuracy, consistency, and alignment with industry standards.


What We Are Looking For


4+ years of experience in technical writing or a related field.
Proven expertise in creating technical documentation, including API docs, user guides, and internal knowledge management systems.
Advanced proficiency in documentation tools such as Confluence, GitBook, or MkDocs.
Hands-on experience with Markdown and documenting complex software architectures.
Advanced proficiency in English.
