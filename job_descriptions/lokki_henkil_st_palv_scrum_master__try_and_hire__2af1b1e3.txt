We are looking for a Scrum Master for our client in the banking sector. This is Try & Hire assignment.

Detailed description of work task to be carried out:

What youll be doing:
· Manage dependencies across multiple Scrum teams located in different agile trains across the organization.
· Be on top of the team work status and give updates to various stakeholder on the progress
· Assist and be the "right hand" of our RTE
· Facilitate scrum team events, agile ceremonies and workshops
· Coach and mentor team members, establish feedback loops and encourage constant learning
· Motivate people and create a fun and energizing work environment built on trust and inclusion
· Develop a strong culture of knowledge sharing and drive relentless continuous improvements
· Steer problem-solving efforts, nurture stakeholder collaboration and guide conflict resolution

Must-have knowledge and experience:
-Outstanding communication and stakeholder management skills
-Excellent planning and organization skills, being able to coordinate across several teams
-Strong facilitation and coaching ability
-Dedicated, dependable and committed to empowering others
-Highly competent, adaptable and creative person who is open to change and continuous learning
-Demonstrable experience in the Scrum Master role, building high-performing teams in agile and distributed settings
-Firm grasp of agile methodologies (SAFe/Scrum), lean principles and project management, including risk and dependency management
-Ability to collaborate effectively with talented teams, take initiative and procure results, as well as suggest practical actions to improve ways of working and implement best practices
-Knowledge of software development lifecycle, QA processes, CI/CD pipelines
-Solid knowledge of release & rollout process
-Ability to collaborate with external software vendors discussing software delivery, raising defects and expectations in resulting patching
-Completed Scrum master training (SAFe certification preferred)
-Eager to integrate use of AI in the scrum team work

Nice-to-have knowledge and experience:
-Payments knowledge

Answer these questions separately in a few sentences and attach them to your application! (Mandatory!)
- Have you worked as a Scrum Master for at least 3 years in a SAFe-based enterprise environment?*
- Have you coordinated dependencies between multiple Scrum teams?*
- Have you provided regular delivery status updates to senior stakeholders or management?*
- Do you have experience with software release and rollout processes?*
- Are you available for a Try & Hire assignment, with the possibility of transitioning to a permanent position with the client?*

Please apply with your updated CV, that clearly demonstrates your skills and experience relevant to this role.

Note! This application is only for individuals already residing in Finland, and relocation is not possible.
