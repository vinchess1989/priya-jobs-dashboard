This is us

At Avenga, we believe that human creativity empowers technology that matters. Operating globally, our 6000+ specialists provide a full spectrum of services, including business and tech advisory, enterprise solutions, CX, UX and Ul design, managed services, product development, and software development.

This is the job

In Bratislava within the Banking industry, we are actively seeking a professional to strengthen our team. As a DevOps Engineer, you will be responsible for building, automating, securing, and operating cloud infrastructure supporting a Payment Solution. You will work closely with other DevOps Engineers, Developers, and cross-functional Agile teams to ensure reliable software delivery and stable production environments.

The role combines AWS cloud infrastructure, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, containerization, automation, security, and production operations.

This is you


Experience with Source Code Management (Git)
3+ years of DevOps experience and knowledge of DevOps principles
Knowledge in building, managing, securing CI/CD pipelines
Basic AWS knowledge (advanced is a plus)
Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code
Scripting (Bash and Python)
Ability to cooperate in an agile environment
Experience in product or agile project development in the banking or fintech industry
Good knowledge of Atlassian products (JIRA, Confluence)
Knowledge of at least one programming language (Python preferred)
Experience in AI-assisted development
English - Advanced (C1)


This is your role


Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines with GitLab CI/CD
Provision and manage AWS infrastructure using Terraform
Deploy and operate containerized microservices on AWS ECS
Work with AWS services including IAM, Cognito, ECS, RDS, S3, CloudFront, Route 53, API Gateway, MSK/Kafka, ALB, and VPN
Ensure the availability, reliability, and security of the Payment Solution
Participate in on-duty/on-call production support and incident resolution
Automate operational tasks using Bash and Python
Apply DevSecOps and security best practices
Document infrastructure, processes, and operational procedures
Collaborate with DevOps Engineers and cross-functional Agile teams
