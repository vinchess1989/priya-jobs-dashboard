Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
SSH Communications Security is a global cybersecurity company specializing in securing access and communications between systems, applications, and people. Trusted by more than 5,000 organizations worldwide, including many Fortune 500 companies, we help businesses protect their critical digital assets and prepare for the security challenges of tomorrow.

We're looking for a motivated and collaborative DevOps Engineer to join our Network Encryptor team. You'll help maintain and improve the Linux infrastructure, virtual environments and development workflows that support our products.

The role focuses primarily on infrastructure and automation, with backend development as an important part of the work. Alongside improving CI/CD and engineering environments, you'll develop services, integrations and tooling in Python or Go.

You'll bring practical experience and the ability to work independently in familiar areas, while collaborating with experienced colleagues on more complex technical decisions.

What you'll be doing


Maintain and improve Linux systems, including configuration, permissions, software updates and troubleshooting.
Configure and troubleshoot networking, including DNS, routing, connectivity and firewalls.
Provision and maintain virtual machines, primarily using libvirt.
Maintain and improve GitLab CI/CD pipelines and runners, automating build, test and delivery workflows.
Write maintainable Bash scripts to automate administration and reduce manual work.
Develop backend services, integrations and engineering tools in Python or Go.
Investigate issues across infrastructure, pipelines and software components, working with colleagues to resolve them.
Contribute to testing, code reviews and technical documentation, and help improve development practices.


What we're looking for


Practical experience in DevOps, Linux systems administration or a related engineering role.
Confidence working with Linux systems and investigating configuration, service and permission issues.
Understanding of networking fundamentals and experience troubleshooting connectivity problems.
Experience with CI/CD pipelines and an understanding of automated build, test and delivery processes.
Bash scripting skills, including error handling, debugging and writing maintainable scripts.
Programming experience in Python or Go, with the ability to develop and maintain services, integrations or tooling.
Familiarity with Git and collaborative development practices.
A methodical approach to problem-solving and the ability to work independently while recognising when to seek support.
Clear communication skills and good written and spoken English.


Additional Experience We Value


GitLab CI/CD pipeline development and runner maintenance.
Virtual machine administration, particularly with libvirt, QEMU or KVM.
Integrating systems through APIs and services.
Reading, debugging or making small changes to C/C++ code.
Effective use of AI coding assistants, including the ability to understand, review and test generated output while following company rules for confidential information and source code.


Interested? Apply now and help us improve how we build, test and deliver secure network encryption products.

Please note: As part of our commitment to security, all employees are required to undergo a security clearance conducted by the Finnish Security and Intelligence Service (Supo).
