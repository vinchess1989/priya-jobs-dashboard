Documentation Engineer, Evac Oy, Espoo
Apply

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

You will create and maintain technical documentation such as manuals, data sheets, user instructions and spare parts documentation for products and delivery projects.
You will manage document control, assist the Lead Documentation Engineer with workload and assignments, and develop best practices to improve Evac's documentation system.
The role requires a Bachelor's or Master's in environmental, mechanical or related engineering, experience in product and project documentation, fluent English, and proficiency in MS Office.
The position is based in Espoo at Evac Oy, an international, sustainability-driven company, and the recruitment is handled by Academic Work with personality and cognitive tests in a continuous selection process.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



We are looking for a proactive and organized Documentation Engineer to join our client Evac. As a Documentation Engineer you will be a part of an international company and contribute to exciting projects. If you are interested and looking for new challenges, keep reading and apply today!

About the role

In this role, you will work with versatile documentation tasks and your responsibilities are for example to create manuals, data sheets, user instructions, and other technical documents for both standard products and delivery projects, as well as spare parts. You will play a key role in ensuring the documents meet project demands and are delivered to clients on time. Additionally, you will proactively develop Evac's documentation system and procedures in close collaboration with a highly skilled documentation team.

As a working place Evac fosters an inclusive and safe culture where colleagues know and trust each other. The recently renewed purpose and vision are driving Evac towards a zero-waste future and modern and stylish office premises in Espoo offer you a new level of well-being at work!

Work tasks

Technical writing for projects, products, and spare parts
Document control for projects and collaborating with other document controllers
Assisting the Lead Documentation Engineer in workload management and work assignments
Developing best practices and improving the documentation system

We are looking for

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in environmental, mechanical, or a related engineering field
Experience in product and project documentation
Experience in technical documentation, reporting and other engineering tasks
Commited to provide excellent customer service
Excellent communication skills and proactive approach to work
Fluent written and spoken English
Proficiency in MS Office tools

It is meritorious if you have

Working knowledge of JIRA, Windchill, SharePoint and AutoCAD

Our recruitment process

This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
