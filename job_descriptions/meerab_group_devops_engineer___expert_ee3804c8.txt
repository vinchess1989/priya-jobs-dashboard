We are seeking a DevOps Engineer – Expert to join our team. You will design and implement cloud environments and automation for our assets.

Key Responsibilities


Design and implement new technologies and tools.
Perform deployments using Jenkins and Digital AI.
Develop and manage deployment automation for Middleware, Database and Web products using Terraform.
Apply DevOps concepts such as Continuous Integration, Continuous Deployment, Continuous Delivery, Continuous Testing, and Process Automation.
Support Build & Deployment Automation using Ansible and related tools.
Manage Release Orchestration, including application packages, runbooks, release pipelines and workflows.
Enable and maintain tooling supporting Development, Testing, Quality and Collaboration.
Work with infrastructure components across On-Premise and Cloud environments.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field.
Minimum 6 years of relevant experience.
Expert knowledge of Linux, Shell scripting and Python.
Experience with Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible, Kubernetes and Cloud technologies.
Strong practical knowledge of production environments, On-Premise and Cloud.
Excellent communication, analytical, and teamwork skills.


Nice to have


Experience with Spring Boot, Node.js, and Digital AI.
Knowledge of IBM BNPP Cloud (dMZR) and Helm Charts.
Familiarity with Agile methodologies.
Proficiency in Dutch or French languages.
