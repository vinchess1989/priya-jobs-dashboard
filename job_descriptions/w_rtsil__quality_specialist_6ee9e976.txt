Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

The Quality Specialist will support quality assurance and improvement activities within R&D and Engineering, working primarily in Vaasa with hybrid remote work.
You will lead and support quality improvement initiatives, corrective-action follow-up, quality performance management using KPIs, and work with quality-related data and control processes.
The role requires a Bachelor's or Master's in a relevant field, at least three years' experience in quality or process improvement, familiarity with ISO9001 and tools like MS Office (PowerBI/SAP advantageous), fluency in English, and occasional travel.
We seek a proactive, analytical team player with strong stakeholder communication—experience in R&D, auditing or Lean Six Sigma is an advantage—and applications must be submitted via the Careers portal by 03/12/2026.

We are looking for a Quality Specialist to support quality assurance and -improvement activities within Research & Development and Engineering. In this role, you will work closely with stakeholders to strengthen ways of working, support compliance with quality requirements and contribute to a culture of continuous improvement. The preferred location for this position is Vaasa. We offer a possibility to hybrid remote work.  

Your role and responsibilities 

As a Quality Specialist, you will participate in quality improvement initiatives, help the organization identify improvement needs and support the creation and follow-up of corrective actions. You will also contribute to maintaining alignment with quality standards and control processes as well as supporting with quality related performance management through working with KPI’s and other process indicators. In addition, working with quality related data will be an essential part of your responsibilities as well as developing this field further.

You will work as a part of Quality & Management Systems team in an international working environment. As the team operates across multiple sites, openness to traveling is expected.

Requirements for the position:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant field, such as Engineering, Quality Management or Industrial Management, or a related discipline
Minimum 3 years of relevant work experience in quality, process improvement or operational development with proven experience in contributing to quality improvement initiatives
Good skills in managing tasks with deadlines in a structured manner
Understanding of quality management systems, preferably previous experience in applying ISO9001 into practice
Good written and spoken English, fluency in Finnish and or Swedish is seen as advantage
Willingness to travel occasionally for work-related purposes
Competences in using MS Office tools. Previous experience in using PowerBI, QHSE related tools, SAP and other relevant tools is considered as advantage

As an ideal candidate, you would:

Be proactive, curious and willing to take ownership of assigned tasks
Have good collaboration and communication skills and enjoy working with different stakeholders
Use a structured and analytical approach to problem solving and continuous improvement
Enjoy teamwork and be comfortable supporting meetings, workshops and improvement activities
Have previous experience working in R&D operations, which is considered as advantage
Have experience in auditing and certificate in Lean Six Sigma are seen as advantage
