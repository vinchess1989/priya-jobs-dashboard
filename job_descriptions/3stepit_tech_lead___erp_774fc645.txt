The role:

Tech Lead – ERP

Reporting to:

Director, Business Design & Applications




Job role and purpose:




The Tech Lead will play a crucial role in managing the technical delivery of projects, mentoring team members, and ensuring the overall quality, performance, ownership, and timely delivery of IT solutions. You will work closely with stakeholders, including software engineers, project managers, and business/product owners, to define requirements, align priorities, and ensure successful delivery outcomes. The Tech Lead helps stakeholders define the “what” in detail and translates it into the “how” together with other IT stakeholders.




In this role, you are responsible for ensuring that all requirements are well documented, ticketed, and prioritized for the development team(s). Success in this role requires strong project leadership capabilities, excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, and a solid technical understanding of enterprise applications, integrations, and solution architecture.




At 3stepIT, the Tech Lead also acts as the technical owner for named solution(s), such as ERP, AssetIQ, CRM, ServiceNow, Finance, and other business-critical systems. You understand the big picture of all 3stepIT products and services while maintaining detailed functional and architectural expertise within your designated solution area(s).




This role requires the ability to drive cross-functional initiatives, balance business and technical priorities, and collaborate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Occasional travel may be required based on business needs.




Main responsibilities & tasks:




Tech Leadership: Lead technical decision-making, provide guidance on best practices, and ensure consistent implementation across all projects in a focus area based on technology competence and background. Tech Lead owns and ensures the delivery of technical outputs for projects.
Architecture & Design: Lead and ensure together with architects and other stakeholders a robust and scalable design, and secure IT solutions that meet business requirements.
Collaboration: Work closely with project managers, business analysts, vendors and other teams to align technical initiatives with business objectives. Communicate technical concepts and progress to non-technical stakeholders and participate in project/sprint planning and estimation activities.
Performance & Scalability: Drive optimal performance, security, and scalability of the applications and systems being developed.
Problem Solving: Lead the resolution of challenges to ensure that project risks are identified and mitigated effectively.
Dev/partner coordination: Ensure all relevant technical documentation is created, and user stories for development teams and roadmap/backlogs are up to date.
System/process ownership: Owns the named solution(s) on a technical level, understands how solution(s) are built and how the process(es) are run through the solutions. Help defining named solution(s) strategy and vision together with stakeholders.




What you bring with you:




Qualifications and experience: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Management, Data Science, or a related field
10+ years of experience in IT, including at least 3+ years in a Tech Lead, Solution Lead, or similar role within a complex business environment
Proven experience leading technical teams and delivering large-scale IT projects and cross-functional initiatives
Strong understanding of software architecture, system integrations, design patterns, and development best practices
End-to-end business process understanding and the ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions
Experience working with enterprise applications and business-critical systems
Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) and modern development practices such as CI/CD
Good understanding of Agile, Scrum, and IT service management frameworks such as ITIL
Knowledge of cybersecurity principles, data protection standards, and secure software development practices
Strong leadership, stakeholder management, communication, and problem-solving skills
Ability to collaborate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders across different organizational functions
Ability to balance short-term business priorities with long-term system health and maintainability
Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver results in a fast-paced environment
Ability to work independently while fostering teamwork and collaboration
Strong English communication skills are required; Finnish language skills are considered an advantage




Please note that the person selected for this role needs to pass the Finnish security clearance (perusmuotoinen turvallisuus-selvitys).
