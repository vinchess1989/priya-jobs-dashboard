Manual QA Analyst (WebRTC & Postman) at BairesDev

You will be the Manual QA Analyst who validates real-time communication experiences. You will test audio and video quality along with the services that support them.

What You'll Do


Test real-time audio and video features across browsers and devices.
Evaluate call quality under different network conditions.
Design scenarios covering connection setup, drops, and reconnection.
Verify supporting APIs and signaling behavior.
Document defects with detailed steps and observations.
Retest fixes and support release readiness.


What We Are Looking For


4+ years of experience in manual software testing and quality assurance.
Hands-on experience testing real-time communication features built on WebRTC.
Proven expertise validating APIs with Postman.
Solid knowledge of networking basics, browser tools, and defect tracking.
Advanced proficiency in English.
