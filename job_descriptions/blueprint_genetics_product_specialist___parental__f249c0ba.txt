Job description
Are you an enthusiastic Product Specialist ready to take on new challenges? Do you have experience in working in life science and healthcare industry? If so, then we'd like to chat!

Blueprint Genetics, a Quest Diagnostics company, is a clinical genetic testing company aspiring to bring world-class genetic knowledge to mainstream healthcare. We want to improve the lives of individuals and families impacted by inherited diseases. We are looking for a Product Specialist to join our global Product Management team, which plays a pivotal role in our mission to provide clinicians and their patients with comprehensive and high-quality tools and resources for the diagnosis of genetic conditions.

We are looking for those who want to make a difference

As a Product Specialist, you will be the right hand of our product managers and support new solution development and life cycle management for Blueprint Genetics. You will act between business, technology, operations and marketing and work closely with internal and external users of the global offering. You will support through-out the idea to market product journey as well as give product support on assigned products.

You will join our cross-functional Product Management team and report into the Director, Product Management based in Finland. We hope you can join our office in Keilaniemi, Espoo, but you have the flexibility to work in a hybrid setting. This is a full-time, fixed-term parental leave replacement.

Your main tasks will include:
Working with the Product team to identify, assess and deliver new products and services addressing unmet customer needs.
Working closely with marketing to create a compelling narrative, including key benefits, and transforming technical capabilities into clear and compelling stories.
Coordinating life-cycle management projects across the global offering through cross-functional teams.
Providing in-depth product support to all parts of the organization and training users on product and services, as well as assisting Sales and Business development partners in RFP requests.
Drive the growth and success of assigned product categories
Conducting market research and analyzing industry trends. Gather competitor and market insights from internal and external sources.
Defining and managing product metrics; Analyzing business, client, and scientific data to develop product insights and recommend actions.
Updating and creating product management related documents, such as product requirement documentation, test specifications and post-market surveillance.
Participating in continuously improving our product making processes and simplifying ways of working for increased efficiency together with key stakeholders.
Ensure all work is performed in compliance with company policies/QMS including Privacy/HIPAA and other regulatory, legal, and safety requirements.

What we expect from you

To be successful in the role you should have an understanding of the life science and healthcare sector and a passion for expanding your knowledge in clinical genetics products. We hope you have a commercial mind-set, excellent project management skills, and you communicate and collaborate effortlessly with our diverse team of experts. You are a versatile product professional with an analytical and proactive mindset and you are comfortable with giving presentations. Furthermore, you are well-equipped to work in a rapidly changing, global environment, where ways of working and playbooks are evolving. Last but not least, we aspire to find a colleague who has the eagerness to grow with us and who fits the inclusive and evolving culture of Blueprint Genetics and Quest Diagnostics.

Our ideal candidate has
Solid experience in R&D, marketing or product management in a similar role
Good understanding of healthcare and/or life science industry
BSc or MSc degree in a relevant field
Proactive and self-driven team worker with strong cross-functional collaboration skills
Strong problem-solving skills
Excellent communication skills in English both verbally and in writing
Strong analytical skills with Excel and Power BI or similar
Strong presentation skills with PowerPoint or similar
Ability to organize long-term project plans and meet assigned targets and deadlines
Occasional flexibility with working hours to meet different time zone requirements.

What we offer

At Blueprint Genetics you'll be a part of a team driven by a meaningful purpose - making a real difference by improving the lives of patients and families affected by inherited diseases. We offer you a friendly, flexible, and multicultural work environment with great people from different fields of expertise.

You will have the opportunity to take ownership of global customer service processes and make a tangible impact on how our organization operates across regions. You will work in a highly collaborative and evolving environment where you can influence ways of working, develop your expertise, and collaborate with colleagues across the organization and around the world.

Our employee benefits include participation in performance-based bonus payment system, extensive occupational healthcare, sports, culture & wellbeing benefit, mental wellbeing with Auntie, on-site gym membership, team events & office parties.

How to apply

If you are interested in hearing more, please submit your application and CV at the latest on December 7th 2026.

If you have any further questions about the position, you can contact our Director, Product Management, Solveig Sjöblom by phone on 040 525 1108 26th November from 12:00 - 13:00 EET and 2nd December from 10:00 - 11:00 (EET). Not for agencies please.

Who we are

Blueprint Genetics is a Quest Diagnostics company. Quest Diagnostics, A Fortune 500 company, is the world's leading provider of diagnostic information services. www.questdiagnostics.com
