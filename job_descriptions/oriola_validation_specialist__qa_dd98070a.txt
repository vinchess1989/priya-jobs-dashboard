About the position
Validation Specialist, Quality Assurance

We are now looking for a Validation Specialist, QA, to join our Quality team. In this role, you will act as a key expert in validation and qualification activities, helping to ensure compliance with GxP requirements across our operations.

What you'll do

As a Validation Specialist, you will support and develop validation and qualification activities related to processes, systems, equipment and facilities. You will work closely with operational teams, system owners and quality professionals to maintain a compliant and effective validation framework.

Your responsibilities will include

Reviewing and approving validation and qualification documentation
Supporting the planning and execution of validation and qualification activities
Providing expert guidance on validation strategies and approaches
Investigating deviations and troubleshooting validation-related issues
Supporting risk assessments and quality improvement initiatives
Performing internal audits and supporting external audits and regulatory inspections
Skills & Requirements

Skills & Requirements

Personal and experience Requirements
You have a solid background in validation within regulated GxP environment and enjoy working in a role that combines technical expertise, collaboration and continuous improvement.

Other Requirements

We are looking for a professional who has:

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
High level of accuracy and attention to detail
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities
The ability to work effectively in a deadline-driven reporting environment
A proactive approach and strong sense of ownership
A continuous improvement mindset and a genuine interest in developing processes and ways of working

To succeed in this role, we expect you to have:

Strong knowledge of validation and qualification practices in GxP-regulated environments
A Master's degree in engineering, life sciences or another relevant field, or equivalent experience.
Experience in a similar validation or qualification role.
Experience with EU GDP and relevant national regulations.
Familiarity with EU GMP requirements, including, GAMP5, Annex 11 and Annex 15
Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken

You are a structured and analytical professional with a strong quality mindset. You enjoy working with others, communicating across different functions and turning regulatory requirements into practical solutions.

You are also:

Curious and motivated to stay up to date with GxP validation requirements

About the company

Oriola is a Finnish listed company with a solid expertise within the pharmaceutical sector. We have more than one hundred years of experience in medicine distribution in Sweden and over 70 years in Finland, and we unite all actors in the field from pharmaceutical companies to consumers in a unique way. With the help of our professional and committed employees, we promote the wellbeing of people and animals. We ensure that medicines as well as health and wellbeing products are delivered in a safe and customer-friendly manner to pharmacies, veterinarians, retailers, or our dose dispensing customers. Our expert services support pharmaceutical companies throughout the entire lifespan of a product. With us, you will have access to a wealth of expertise and inspiring opportunities in all our businesses.

Our purpose is ‘Health for life’. With us, you will join an open and collaborative culture. We support and care for each other and our customers. To thrive with us, you will need to be open, proactive and responsible – and we will help you to develop your strengths and progress along your professional journey. At Oriola, we cherish openness, taking initiative and responsibility, and working together.
