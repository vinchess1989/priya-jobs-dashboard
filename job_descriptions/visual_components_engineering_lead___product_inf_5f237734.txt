Get to know Visual Components




At Visual Components, we build simulation technology that powers the future of manufacturing. More than 2,400 professionals worldwide, from small factories to major global manufacturers, use our software to design, visualize, and optimize tomorrow’s production lines.




With over 20 nationalities represented in our Espoo HQ and offices in the US, Germany, and Japan, we’re truly international. We grow together, we enjoy our work, and we inspire one another. Join us and experience the difference of working with a team that’s passionate about building high-quality engineering solutions.




The role and the team




We are looking for an Engineering Lead to establish and guide a new team, Product Infra. You will focus on improving the architecture of our products and improving infrastructure around it. You will ensure that our product customization, dependency management, deployment pipelines, and test infrastructures are streamlined. Your focus will be on creating reliable, scalable solutions that support a growing number of customers and use cases.




You will collaborate closely with DevOps and Quality Assurance teams while focusing on product-specific needs. This includes improving our product to support a growing number of platform customers. You will also drive improvements to our build architecture, dependency management, and test infrastructure.




We work in a hybrid model and expect team members to be onsite few days a week to support collaboration, onboarding, and team cohesion.




Your impact

As an Engineering Lead, you will:

Create an environment where people and products flourish
Encouragingteam workwithintheteam and with other teams
Working closely with Product Owner to define work for theteam in backlog




Key Responsibilities

Lead and mentor the Product Infra team through regular 1:1s, focusing on wellbeing and professional growth
Drive improvements in:
Product customization and variation for our desktop products
Product deployment - improve product distribution, software update mechanism
Desktop application build system – help to improve build speed, modularity and reliability together with DevOps
Dependency management - maintain up‑to‑date and well‑organized dependencies, and enable Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) reporting
Test infrastructure - help to improve our custom test infrastructure for the 3D simulation platform together with QA
Collaborate with other teams on cross-functional dependencies
Facilitate team Scrum activities and maintain momentum toward shared goals
Provide technical and quality oversight, including design reviews and testing strategies
Champion continuous improvement indevelopmentprocess, tooling, and product quality




While the Engineering Lead role focuses on leading and supporting the team, we value strong technical involvement. You will stay close to the code through architectural design, reviews, and selected hands-on contributions, especially in complex or high-impact areas.







TECH STACK

Languages: C++, C++/CLI, C#, WPF, Python
Build & Deployment: AzureDevOpsPipelines,nugetandvcpkg, Advanced Installer
Tools: Visual Studio, GIT, Azure DevOps, GitHub Copilot




The skills you will bring




Experience leading a software development team (Team lead/Scrum master)
Hands-on experience with C# and C++
Strong understanding of build and dependency management
Experience on modernizing legacy monolithic software projects
Excellent communication, coaching, and people skills
Familiarity with Agile and Scrum processes and the ability to effectively navigate and work in an agile environment.
Experience working with AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot) to improve coding efficiency, code quality, and developer productivity.
Experience with robotics, simulation systems, or data analysis is highly beneficial
Proficient English communication skills (spoken and written)
