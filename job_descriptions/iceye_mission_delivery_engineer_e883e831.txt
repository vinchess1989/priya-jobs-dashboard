Why This Role Matters

As a Mission Delivery Engineer you will support the delivery of complex Satellite Mission programs end-to-end with deep technical ownership, ensuring customer success, on-time execution, and scalable delivery excellence.

You will work across the Portfolio focusing on the technical thread of customer missions—from requirements capture through system acceptance and delivery—acting as the technical authority/interface between the customer and ICEYE's engineering, product, and operations teams. Your role bridges program governance and system engineering: ensuring mission requirements are understood, flowed down, tracked, and validated, while maintaining configuration control and driving cross-functional technical alignment throughout the program lifecycle.

This role directly contributes to ICEYE's ambition to become the leading provider of space-based ISR infrastructure by scaling reliable, high-impact mission delivery globally.

The role will be based in Finland or Poland at the ICEYE facility. Travel to customer sites is required.

Who We Are

ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.

ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.

ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.

Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

Expected Outcomes & Requirements

Outcomes


Customer technical requirements are accurately captured, baselined, and flowed down to engineering teams, ensuring all mission commitments are fully traceable from contract to delivery.
Customers experience a consistent, high-trust technical partnership, with a single accountable interface that understands their systems and operational needs throughout the program lifecycle.
System integration, verification, and validation activities are planned and executed rigorously, ensuring all delivered capabilities are compliant with mission and contractual requirements.
Configuration and interface control is maintained across all mission segments—satellite, ground, networks, and user interfaces—minimising risk from upgrades, changes, and inter-system dependencies.
Technical risks, discrepancies, and gaps are identified early, escalated effectively and resolved proactively through structured coordination across software, hardware, operations, and QA teams.
Cross-functional technical teams operate in alignment, with clear ownership and system-level perspective maintained across all reviews, delivery milestones, and readiness checks.
Technical documentation—including CONOPS, ICDs, verification matrices, mission analysis, and acceptance reports—is delivered on time and kept current with the actual system implementation.


Role-Specific Competencies


5+ years' experience delivering complex, multi-stakeholder technical programs in advanced technology environments (e.g. space, defence, telecom, automotive, IT)
Proven ability to own the technical customer interface, managing senior stakeholders and translating operational requirements into engineering deliverables
Hands-on experience with requirements management, traceability, and configuration control across hardware, software, and system-level domains
Demonstrated involvement in system integration, verification and validation (V&V) planning and execution, including acceptance testing campaigns
Strong cross-functional technical coordination skills across engineering, product, and operations disciplines
Experience of carrying out analysis and mission design using software packages such as STK, SaVoir, MONTE or GMAT
Excellent communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences, including formal reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR, DRR)
Degree in engineering, systems engineering, computer science, or a related technical field
Must have or be able to obtain required security clearances


Nice-to-have


Experience in Earth Observation (EO), SAR, or remote sensing
Background in space mission systems or satellite ground segment development
Experience working with government or defence customers on classified or sovereign programs
Knowledge of hardware/software integration, API control, and embedded systems
Familiarity with ESA or equivalent program documentation frameworks
Advanced degree (Master's) or relevant certifications (e.g. PMP, PgMP, INCOSE CSEP)
