Job Description

We're looking for a

(Senior) Configuration & Automation Specialist (Microsoft Dynamics Focus) (m/f/d)

(permanent, full-time) to join our Automation & Configuration Team at one of our locations in Berlin, Verl, or Baden-Baden, with flexible working conditions available.

In this role, you will take ownership of the day-to-day operation and continuous improvement of Riverty’s Microsoft Dynamics 365 Contact Center platform.

As the Dynamics specialist within our Automation & Configuration Team, you will translate business requirements into scalable platform configurations while balancing operational stability with continuous feature delivery. Working closely with Product Owners, Operations, Automation Engineers, Central IT, and external partners, you will help build reliable, maintainable, and future-oriented customer service solutions using a configuration-first approach.

Your Responsibilities


Configure, maintain, and optimize Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Contact Center capabilities
Design and manage case management, queues, routing rules, workstreams, SLAs, business rules, forms, views, and agent experiences
Maintain and improve omnichannel customer service capabilities across voice, chat, and email channels
Own daily operational platform support, including complex configuration issues and second-level troubleshooting
Translate business requirements into scalable Dynamics configurations and support solution design activities
Configure and deliver new features, process improvements, and country rollouts from development through production readiness
Collaborate on automation initiatives using Microsoft Copilot Studio and Power Automate
Support integration testing and change activities together with Automation Engineers and Central IT
Create and maintain documentation, configuration standards, runbooks, and best practices


Qualifications


Experience configuring Microsoft Dynamics 365, ideally within Customer Service, Contact Center, or similar CRM environments
Experience with customer service processes such as case management, routing, queues, SLAs, and agent workflows
Ability to translate business requirements into scalable and maintainable platform configurations
Strong problem-solving skills and experience troubleshooting platform-related issues
Structured, analytical, and solution-oriented way of working
Professional communication skills in English and German


Nice to Have


Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Contact Center or Omnichannel for Customer Service
Experience with Copilot Studio, Power Automate, or similar low-code technologies
Basic understanding of integrations, APIs, and connected enterprise systems
Microsoft certifications or equivalent practical experience


What Makes You Successful


You take ownership of both platform stability and continuous improvement
You prefer configuration-first solutions over unnecessary custom development
You enjoy collaborating across Product, Operations, Engineering, and IT teams
You actively share knowledge and contribute to team growth and cross-skilling initiatives
