Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

We are looking for an experienced BMC Discovery Expert to join IT Service Configuration Management team on a full-time employment agreement. Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We’re not just a bank; we’re a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

Meet the IT Service Configuration Management team. Our role is to provide centralised ICT Asset and Configuration process and operations within BMC Helix ITSM system.

Main responsibilities in this role:


Review, analyse, coordinate and maintain efficient operations between BMC Discovery and Helix CMDB
BMC Discovery monitoring, troubleshooting, housekeeping
BMC Discovery TPL development, synchronization and reconciliation
Application Modelling development and maintenance
Identify improvements potential and develop technical solutions
Cooperate with data owners, architects, and various subject matter experts including BMC vendor


The role is based in Poland - Gdańsk/Gdynia or Finland - Helsinki.

Who You Are

This is the right role for you if you:


Are self-driven and proactive with a positive attitude and integrity
Have analytical mind and curiosity
Are quality oriented
Collaborate and connect with others easily
Are ambitious and structured problem solver that demonstrates good judgement & practical sense with limited information at hand
Demonstrate experience IT Service Management Operations


Your background and skills include:


Professional and hands on experience with BMC Discovery/ServiceNow Discovery
Experience with upgrades to the BMC/ServiceNow Discovery tool
Experience in building automations and workflows
Experience in scripting/writing patterns (BMC/ServiceNow Discovery and SQL)
Experience in Service Modelling (CAM, SAM or Blueprint)
Knowledge of architecture and configurations of IT infrastructure components
Experience working with ITSM tools (Remedy, ServiceNow, Helix)
High skills in communication
Fluency in written and spoken English


It would be ideal if you also:


Are familiar with Agile methodology
Have experience in ITSM processes and understanding of ITIL methodology
