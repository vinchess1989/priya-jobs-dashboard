JOB DESCRIPTION

Drive the development of reliable, user-friendly and compliant digital quality systems

At Orion Pharma, our mission is to build well-being through innovative medicines and solutions. We are now looking for two professionals for our new Digital Quality System team: a Digital Quality System Lead, who will act as the team’s people manager, and a Digital Quality System Manager, who will work as a specialist in the team. Both positions combine ownership with active hands-on contribution to the development, lifecycle management and continuous improvement of digital systems and tools used in Quality Management. 

These are full-time positions based in Espoo or Turku, Finland. Orion supports a hybrid working model, with employees working onsite three days per week and remotely for up to two days per week.

Your role

In both roles, you will manage and develop digital quality system capabilities in close collaboration with Quality Management, Information Management, system owners, process owners and external partners. Together with your colleagues, you will ensure that our digital quality systems are reliable, user-friendly, compliant and aligned with evolving business and regulatory needs. You will translate quality management priorities and process needs into practical system improvements, drive the implementation of enhancements and help build more efficient, digitally supported and increasingly automated ways of working across Quality Management, including the appropriate use of AI-enabled solutions. The Lead will additionally provide people leadership, set the team’s direction and priorities, and establish effective ways of working. 

Responsibilities

Responsibilities shared by both roles

Work proactively with stakeholders and users to understand business needs, pain points and improvement priorities
Translate user needs, quality process requirements and regulatory expectations into practical system functionalities and improvements
Identify and prioritize opportunities to improve usability, data quality, automation, AI-enabled support, reporting and overall system performance
Lead, coordinate and make hands-on contributions to system development, implementation and continuous improvement activities
Take part in and oversee system lifecycle activities, including requirement definition, testing, validation support, change requests, release coordination and issue resolution
Ensure that deliverables are implemented in a compliant, well-documented and user-oriented manner
Support adoption through communication, training, structured user support and practical guidance
Create and maintain system documentation and user guidance for compliant and effective system use
Drive cross-functional decision-making and prioritization to ensure that digital quality system initiatives are delivered effectively and within agreed scope

 

Additional responsibilities of the Digital Quality System Lead

Lead and develop the Digital Quality System team in line with agreed objectives
Set clear priorities, responsibilities and expectations, and monitor progress towards team goals
Coach, support and empower team members and foster their professional development
Build a collaborative, service-oriented and improvement-driven team culture
Establish effective ways of working and stakeholder forums to support alignment, prioritization and decision-making

 

What you can expect from us

We offer you a unique opportunity to shape the future of digital quality management at Orion and contribute to solutions that enable efficient, compliant and user-friendly quality processes across the organization. As part of a newly established team, you will be able to influence its ways of working, priorities and solutions from the outset while developing your expertise in digitalization, automation and AI-enabled solutions in a highly regulated environment. You will work with experienced colleagues across the organization in an open and collaborative culture, where your ideas are valued and continuous learning and professional growth are actively supported.

At Orion, your work creates true impact and well-being for our customers, patients and society at large. ​ ​Our culture of friendliness, respect, mutual appreciation and diversity creates a safe working environment where you can strive for excellence. We offer a wealth of career paths and development opportunities that support the development of innovative solutions and improving the quality of life.

Please visit our website to find further information about our values and Orion as an employer https://www.orion.fi/en/careers/orion-as-an-employer/.

What are we looking for?

Requirements for both roles

A relevant degree in information systems, engineering, life sciences, pharmacy or another applicable field
At least five years of experience with digital business systems, preferably quality management systems or other regulated systems
Experience in system development, implementation, testing, validation support, change management or lifecycle management in a GxP or otherwise regulated environment
Ability to translate business, user and process requirements into practical system solutions and prioritised development actions
A structured, hands-on and improvement-oriented approach, with the ability to resolve issues and deliver concrete outcomes
Strong collaboration, communication and influencing skills and the ability to work effectively with vendors, IT/IM teams, business users and senior stakeholders in a matrix environment
Proven track record in applying automation, analytics and AI-enabled solutions to improve processes and user experience in a regulated environment
Ability to work effectively under pressure and respond appropriately to changing situations
Experience with pharmaceutical quality processes is considered an advantage
Excellent written and spoken English
Sufficient Finnish language skills for effective workplace communication

 

For the Digital Quality System Lead role, we are also looking for

Ability to set direction, clarify priorities and create alignment
Motivation and potential to lead, support and develop team members towards shared goals
Previous people management experience is an advantage

Your new team

Quality management (QM) is responsible for ensuring the quality of materials, products and operations, as well as maintaining and developing the quality system and monitoring the regulatory environment. Pharmacovigilance and Patient Safety unit is responsible for the global pharmacovigilance system of Orion Corporation and for the continuous safety surveillance, regulatory reporting and safety risk management activities of human and veterinary medicinal and non-medicinal products.

The QM function consists of about 400 experts who work in Quality Control, Quality Assurance, Quality System, Method Development and the Pharmacovigilance & Drug safety unit. We are located in Espoo, Turku, Salo and Kuopio plants, in addition to the Quality Assurance team in India reporting dotted-line to QM.
