DevOps Engineer




Role: DevOps Engineer

Location: Finland (Tampere, Helsinki, or fully remote across Finland)

Allocation: 100% (37.5 hours per week)

Assignment Period: 02 December 2026 – 02 March 2027 (Initial 3-month contract with expectation of a multi-year extension)

Language: Fluent English (verbal and written)

Setup: Recann is acting as the employment business for this specific role.




Role Overview:




We are currently seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to join an enterprise assignment in Finland. The role focuses on private cloud infrastructure management, automation, configuration management, and maintaining high-performance CI/CD pipelines within an agile delivery environment.




Key Responsibilities & Requirements:




Cloud & OS Administration: Proven hands-on experience deploying and configuring infrastructure on Private Cloud environments, backed by strong Red Hat Linux administration capabilities.
Automation & Scripting: Proficiency in Ansible, Python, and Git.
Monitoring & Databases: Working experience with Prometheus and a basic understanding of MariaDB.
Data Formats: Basic knowledge of XML and JSON formats.
Methodologies: Experience working according to agile, Scrum, or SAFe principles, alongside hands-on CI/CD pipeline management.
Education: University degree in Computer Science or an equivalent technical field.




Preferred Advantages:




Working experience with on-premise ServiceNow environments including deployments.
Exposure to GitHub, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar tooling.
Coding experience extending beyond simple scripts.
Knowledge of ITIL frameworks and TM Forum Frameworx.
Working experience from Grafana environments.
Previous background or experience in the telecommunications industry.




Preferred Certificates:




Certified System Admin (CSA) with bridge training for the latest version completed across all modules.
ITIL Foundation (Release 4).




If you are interested, or you know someone that could be, please reach out asap and we can speak soon?
