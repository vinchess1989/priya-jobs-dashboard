The Role

As a member of SERDES Group, you will help develop layout designs. As a member of the team, you will work closely with the architecture, IP design, Physical Design teams to achieve first pass silicon success.

The Person

A successful candidate will work with senior silicon design engineers. The candidate will be highly accurate and detail-oriented, possessing good communication and problem-solving skills.

Key Responsiblities


Implementation Layout of blocks and macros
Own and deliver the high-quality macros with help of team
Mentor and groom the juniors in the team
Work with contractor team and the planned work done as per aligned schedule
Enable release flows as required



Preferred Experience


15+ yrs of hands-on experience in layout design
Experience with Cadence virtuoso, Caliber and other verification tools as required for layout development
Preferrable experience in high speed SERDES blocks



Academic Credentials

Bachelors or Masters degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent
