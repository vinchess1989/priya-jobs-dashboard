Your Role

We are seeking an experienced Platform Release Manager to lead the planning, coordination, governance, and delivery of releases across a complex technology landscape. You will serve as the central coordination point for cross-functional teams, ensuring that applications, data platforms, integrations, and infrastructure components are released in a controlled, compatible, and production-ready manner.

This role requires strong stakeholder management, release governance expertise, and a solid understanding of modern DevOps practices. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Testing, DevOps, Support, and Business teams to ensure successful deployments while minimizing risk and maintaining operational stability

Key Responsibilities


Own and maintain the integrated release calendar for feature releases, maintenance releases, hotfixes, and deployment rollouts.
Coordinate dependencies across applications, data products, integrations, pipelines, and platform services.
Manage release scope, ensuring only approved and validated changes are included.
Oversee release readiness activities and drive progression through development, testing, validation, and production approval stages.
Facilitate go/no-go decisions by consolidating testing results, deployment readiness, business approvals, and risk assessments.
Lead deployment planning, execution coordination, rollback preparedness, and incident escalation when required.
Ensure release artifacts, versioning, and production deployments are fully traceable and governed.
Coordinate multi-site rollouts and ensure consistent adoption of approved release baselines.
Communicate release status, risks, timelines, deployment outcomes, and post-release learnings to stakeholders.


Your Profile


5+ years of experience in Release Management, Project Management, Program Management, Release Train Engineering, or a related delivery leadership role.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business, Project Management, or a related field.
Strong knowledge of project and program management principles, including planning, risk management, stakeholder management, dependency management, and governance.
Experience managing complex releases across multiple teams, applications, data platforms, integrations, and infrastructure environments.
Working knowledge of Agile delivery methodologies, with experience in the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) highly preferred.
Experience serving as a Release Train Engineer (RTE) or working closely within a SAFe Agile Release Train environment.
Strong understanding of DevOps and CI/CD practices, including branching strategies, version control, release tagging, artifact management, and environment promotion.
Experience with release planning, deployment governance, production readiness assessments, rollback strategies, and change management processes.
Knowledge of software testing practices, including integration testing, regression testing, System Acceptance Testing (SAT), and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
Strong facilitation, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the confidence to challenge readiness assessments and drive informed release decisions.
Proven ability to manage risks, resolve cross-team dependencies, and coordinate successful production releases in complex enterprise environments.


Preferred Experience:


SAFe® Release Train Engineer (RTE), SAFe Agilist, PMP, Prince2, Scrum Master, or equivalent certification.
Experience working in regulated, highly governed, or business-critical environments.
Familiarity with industrial data platforms and technologies such as Cognite, Snowflake, SnapLogic, HighByte, MQTT, and Unified Namespace (UNS) architectures.
