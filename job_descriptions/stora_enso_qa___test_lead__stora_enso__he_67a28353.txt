Job description
We are looking for an experienced QA & Test Lead to take ownership of quality assurance and testing across a large-scale program for our client Stora Enso in Helsinki. In this key role, you will build and govern the QA framework across all workstreams in a complex, multi-vendor environment. If you have a strong track record in leading program-level testing and quality management, read more below!

About the role

As a QA & Test Lead, you will oversee the planning, design, implementation, and execution of all quality assurance and testing activities across the program, ensuring high-quality delivery through comprehensive QA governance and testing management.

The position is based in Helsinki, with the option to work mainly remotely if you prefer. The duration of the employment is from 1/2027 to 12/2028.

Work tasks


QA strategy & governance: Defining, maintaining, and overseeing the QA process, framework, guidelines, and quality gates across all program workstreams, including satellite projects.
End-to-end quality oversight: Managing development process quality from requirements to production and ensuring all project parties establish agreed quality controls.
Team & resource leadership: Onboarding, training, and managing testing resources, as well as defining key skills needed for testers.
Test management & tooling: Defining testing tools, templates, best practices, and standards, and overseeing the maintenance of test plans, cases, scripts, and reports.
Test automation & CI/CD: Implementing and overseeing test automation tools, their configuration, and execution to support continuous delivery.
Defect & metrics management: Governing bug/defect tracking and resolution, and overseeing quality metrics, KPIs, and status/risk reporting to program leadership.
Analytics & corrective actions: Analyzing test results to draw actionable insights and leading remedial and corrective actions when deviations occur.
Continuous improvement: Driving ongoing enhancements in quality management and delivery processes by collecting feedback and applying lessons learned.


We are looking for


Proven QA & testing experience: Strong track record in QA governance, test design, and leading testing processes in large-scale programs.
Technical & process expertise: Solid understanding of SDLC, IT processes (ITIL), operating models, test automation tools, and cybersecurity standards and controls.
Modern delivery mindset: Familiarity with continuous improvement, CI/CD practices, and leveraging modern technologies such as AI in quality assurance.
Leadership & communication: Demonstrated ability in resource management, vision-setting, and clear stakeholder communication across teams and leadership.
Analytical & detail-oriented approach: Strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills combined with high attention to detail and a result-oriented mindset.
Fluency in English, Finnish and Swedish are considered as an advantage.

Our recruitment process

This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client's wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
