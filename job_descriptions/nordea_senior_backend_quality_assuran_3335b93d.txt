Welcome to Group Technology, where we pride ourselves on engineering solutions and direct Nordea’s transformation by providing a holistic technological view and structured understanding of the bank, and its surrounding environment to enable the Customer Vision and the Business Strategy.

Would you like to contribute to Nordea’s digital strategy within Customer Engagement? We are looking for an experienced Backend Quality Assurance Engineer to join the Digital Technology Engineering team in Oulu.

At Nordea, we know our customers’ needs are evolving — and we are committed to delivering the financial solutions of tomorrow. You will join an agile environment where technology is at the core, and where you can make a real impact. Bring your skills, ideas, and unique background — here you will have opportunities to grow and contribute to something bigger.

Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. We’re not just a bank; we’re a tech employer on a mission to evolve finance securely and responsibly. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry.

About Our Team

We create value for private customers by developing personal finance management tools, leveraging transaction data and analytics to help customers better understand and act on their financial situation. We also enable customers to access and utilize data from other banks (PSD2) within Nordea Mobile channels, and support advisors with digital services.

Our team is based in Oulu, Finland, and works with a modern backend stack including Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Kafka, and cloud platforms.

What You’ll Be Doing


Drive quality practices in daily work – including test automation, validation strategies, and engineering best practices
Design, implement, and maintain automated API test suites using REST Assured and Java-based testing frameworks
Take responsibility for quality assurance and reliability of backend services, ensuring high standards across new features and existing solutions
Drive release processes and support release activities.
Monitor service health and performance using tools such as Grafana and Prometheus, proactively identifying issues and ensuring system stability
Analyse logs and production behaviour in tools like Splunk to troubleshoot incidents, identify patterns, and support data-driven improvements
Contribute to development activities (design, implementation, and maintenance of microservices) in a modern architecture integrating with legacy systems, AWS/GCP, databases, and third-party providers as part of the team, with a strong focus on quality and maintainability
Collaborate closely with backend and front-end developers and business experts to ensure robust, scalable, and well-tested solutions


This role is based in Oulu with a hybrid setup, 3 days per week in the office on average.

Who You Are

We believe you are:


A quality-driven engineer with a strong sense of ownership over reliability and production stability
Proactive and structured, with the ability to identify risks and drive improvements independently
A collaborative team player who enjoys learning and improving
A technically capable developer with interest in the financial domain


Your background and skills include:


4+ years of experience in Quality Assurance and test automation with focus on backend
Expertise in Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, REST Assured or similar
Good understanding of Git, CI/CD (e.g. Jenkins).
Knowledge on monitoring tools like, Splunk, Grafana and Prometheus.
