Why This Role Matters

As a Test Engineer, you will deliver software to defend Europe from space. Your scope covers the full verification stack: unit and integration test infrastructure, software-in-the-loop simulation of the Attitude and Orbit Control System (AOCS), hardware-in-the-loop test rigs running against real sensors and actuators, and the CI/CD pipelines that tie it all together. Your work is what allows us to perform software updates in orbit with confidence and short regular intervals while also protecting satellite availability. You will directly enhance customer satisfaction and enable ICEYE to execute most critical, value-generating

Who We Are

ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE’s world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world’s largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy, day or night and in any weather, supported by ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits.

As a trusted partner for defense, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE’s near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. Designed for dual use, the platform also serves civil protection and commercial users for natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring including oil-spill detection, and finance, contributing to global security and community resilience.

ICEYE is headquartered in Finland and operates globally across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We have more than 900 employees, united by a shared vision: improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation.

Your Day-to-day Responsibilities


Faster and safer AOCS releases: Automated verification runs on every change, so the AOCS team gets trustworthy feedback in minutes. Release candidates are qualified and published through a repeatable pipeline, which means higher quality software reaches the fleet more frequently.
Defects found on the ground, not in orbit: Test coverage extends beyond nominal cases into the off-nominal scenarios: sensor dropouts and degraded measurements, actuator saturation, eclipse, etc. Failure modes are reproduced in simulation and on hardware benches before they can cost us orbits.
Test suite across product variants: Comprehensive test pipelines cover all ADCS generations for different satellite products and peripheral configurations. Engineers trust the test and release pipeline, and the test infrastructure scales with the number of satellite variants and software configurations we support.
Improved pipeline status monitoring: Test results, coverage, and AOCS performance metrics are published automatically and are easy to read. Release quality is shared transparently across the organization enabling data-driven decision-making for fleet wide release deployment.
Reusable hardware-in-the-loop capability: HIL rigs are treated as a product, not a one-off lab setup: automated, monitored, well documented, and available to the whole team rather than dependent on one person’s presence in the lab.


Must Haves

What we’re looking for


Proven experience building test automation for embedded, real-time, or otherwise reliability-critical software.
Strong practical coding skills in Python, plus the ability to read, debug, and extend C/C++ application software
Hands-on CI/CD experience: pipelines, build tooling, containerisation, artefact and test reporting, and the discipline to keep pipelines fast and green.
Experience testing software against simulators or hardware test benches, including comfort working in a lab with real hardware, interfaces, and instrumentation.
Sound test strategy judgement: knowing what belongs in a unit test, what needs software-in-the-loop simulation, and what genuinely requires hardware-in-the-loop.
Thinking from a first-principles mindset. Reproducing an in-orbit or on-bench anomaly is detective work, and you will need to tackle it in a systematic manner.


Nice To Haves


Analysis and visualisation of telemetry and test data.
Embedded Linux, cross-compilation, and remote target debugging.
Simulation and modelling toolchains, including Matlab/Simulink or model-in-the-loop test harnesses.
