Job description
We build things that work, scale, and matter

At Futurice, we bring together software engineering, cloud infrastructure, design, and emerging tech to create lasting impact. We solve hard problems for prominent Nordic organizations-not through dogmatic processes, but through trust, pragmatic engineering, and continuous learning.
About the role
We are looking for a Senior Cloud & DevOps Engineer with deep public cloud automation expertise. In this role you will be helping our clients navigate high-scale cloud transformations and production AI initiatives in complex, potentially highly regulated environments.

You don't need to be an AI researcher or machine learning scientist. If you have solid enterprise cloud foundations, love automating platform infrastructure, and want to build the operational backbone that takes generative AI applications from prototype to regulated production, this role is for you.

What you'll be doing
Automating enterprise cloud platforms: Design, deploy, and maintain secure, production-grade public cloud infrastructure (hub-and-spoke topologies, landing zones, networking, and zero-trust identity).
Treating everything as code: Build and standardize modular Infrastructure as Code using Terraform or Bicep, managed through automated CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions or Azure DevOps or similar).
Powering enterprise AI enablement: Help build and run shared developer platforms-such as managing API gateways for centralized routing, quota tracking, logging, and access control.
Streamlining developer workflows: Enable client product teams by providing reusable infrastructure templates, CI/CD blueprints, and automation scripts in Python or TypeScript.
Bringing production rigor to AI (LLMOps): Collaborate on operationalizing generative AI systems-setting up observability, tracing, cost monitoring, and security guardrails.



What we hope you bring:
Deep public cloud experience (AWS, Azure or GCP): Practical experience with enterprise public cloudenvironments, virtual networking, private endpoints, access management, and landing zones.
Strong IaC & CI/CD mastery: Extensive, hands-on experience deploying infrastructure using Terraform or Bicep through automated pipelines.
Software & scripting skills: Practical programming ability in Python (or TypeScript/Go) for automation, API integrations, and internal tooling.
Operational mindset: Familiarity with modern observability, log aggregation, and monitoring in mission-critical environments.
Curiosity for modern AI tooling: You are eager to roll up your sleeves with tools like Azure OpenAI, API Management, RAG pipelines, or LLM observability frameworks. (Direct prior experience is a huge plus, but strong fundamentals and willingness to learn take priority.)
A consultant's pragmatism: You enjoy helping teams solve practical problems, communicate trade-offs clearly, and care about developer experience.
Language skills: Professional working proficiency in English is required, as it is our primary working language. Professional proficiency in Finnish is considered a strong advantage when collaborating with local enterprise and public-sector clients.

Futurice offers you
We support your growth with a wide range of projects, tools, and learning opportunities. Our client portfolio is diverse, offering a variety of challenges and problem spaces that give you room to explore new areas and develop your skills in meaningful ways. You'll be encouraged and inspired throughout your journey by a community of motivated, multidisciplinary colleagues across development, design, data, AI and strategy.

Perks include:
A hybrid work model that supports flexibility and collaboration
Free choice of work gear - laptop, phone, tablet, headphones, and more
Sports and lunch benefit
Comprehensive healthcare and additional health insurance
Futurice clubs and free-time activity groups
Learning events and internal communities
Certification programs to support your continued development


You'll get a thorough local and global onboarding, plus your very own Futubuddy to support you throughout your journey with us. And to top it off, you'll have the chance to join our Onboarding Camp.
Compensation
Salary range for this role is: 4 900 - 6 500euros / month. Depending of your seniority and fit against our evaluation criteria.

We offer a fixed salary with the possibility of performance-based bonuses and additional benefits. Our salary model is designed to offer a range of levels based on three factors: skills, responsibilities and impact. We are dedicated to fostering an inclusive and supportive work environment where every employee has the opportunity to thrive and advance in their career and become better versions of themselves.
Next steps
If this sounds like the opportunity you're looking for, please submit your application.

Our form is simple: share your contact details and upload your attachments. We'd love to see your CV and a few lines about yourself and why you want to join Futurice. You can also include links to your LinkedIn, GitHub, or portfolio or any other materials you find relevant.

After we receive your application, we'll be in touch. If you move forward, the process includes an initial conversation to get to know each other, a deeper discussion with future colleagues about your skills, and a general chat about potential projects. Reference calls are also a standard part of our recruitment process.
What's it like to work here
Ever since Futurice was founded, we've focused on creating a workplace people genuinely enjoy, Mondays included. We've built a welcoming culture where trust, kindness, and well-being matter as much as great work. Our atmosphere is open and warm, whether you're in the office or working remotely. You'll quickly feel part of the community where coffee chats, virtual fikas, or after-work hangouts happen naturally.

Because our culture is shaped by all of us, every day, you'll have an opportunity to influence how we work, connect, and evolve as a community. Across countries, teams, and time zones, everyone brings their own character, helping shape how we work, connect, and evolve together.

At Futurice, new joiners aren't expected to fit into a mold. Come as you are, whatever your background. We believe in the power of different perspectives, and diversity is a key part of our success at delivering customer value when they trust us with some of their most challenging and inspiring challenges. You can read more about our DE&I commitment here .
