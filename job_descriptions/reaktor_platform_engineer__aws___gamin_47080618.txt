Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Reaktor's award-winning tech community is looking for a Platform Engineer to join our Gaming & Entertainment Business Unit in Helsinki.

If you enjoy designing and building large-scale cloud platforms, working hands-on with IaC, CI/CD, security and observability, and collaborating closely with product teams and stakeholders, you might be just what we are looking for.

As a Platform Engineer at Reaktor, your job is to design, build and operate secure, scalable and cost-efficient cloud platforms that support product teams and our client’s business needs.

In this role, you will be working as part of a Reaktor team consulting the client and supporting the product team with infrastructure automation and DevOps practices. The work may include building new infrastructure and platforms, or help to integrate the existing infrastructure with what our clients might already have.

You will join our Gaming & Entertainment Business Unit, where you will support clients in this domain.

Your Daily Tasks Include


Designing, implementing, and maintaining cloud environments that use the latest best practices and technologies to help product teams deliver the best possible solutions.
Making sure security and compliance are built into everything you create.
Enabling scalable and resilient infrastructure that can grow with demand and keep the digital services running reliably.
Driving cost-efficiency by seeking our opportunities to reduce operational costs without compromising performance or reliability.
Automating as many routine tasks as possible to free up engineers time to focus on higher-value work.
Promoting standardization through common tools, processes and configurations to reduce complexity, improve maintainability, and accelerate development cycles.
Acting as a trusted advisor by working closely with product teams and other stakeholders to understand the requirements and provide the infrastructure support they need.


You enjoy turning complex, fragmented infrastructure into streamlined, scalable platforms that let teams move quickly and reliably.

To Thrive In This Position, You’ll Need


Hands-on experience in cloud/ infra/ DevOps roles. We don’t have a hard limit with years of experience, but we’ve noticed that these competencies often take 7+ years to accumulate.
Experience in AWS (preferably combined with GCP), including production experience with multi-account setups, networking, security, IAM, monitoring.
Experience with at least one IaC tool (e.g. Terraform, AWS CloudFormation)
Solid scripting / programming skills for automation (e.g. Python and/or Go; Bash/PowerShell)
Strong CI/CD experience (e.g. GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins or similar)
Practical experience with observability (e.g. CloudWatch, Prometheus, ELK/Opensearch)
Strong understanding of application architectures and the ability to read and reason about code to debug platform issues
Hands-on experience with cloud cost optimization and FinOps practices
Experience implementing security best practices in cloud environments following industry standards and compliance
Customer oriented mindset, with experience in partnering closely with clients/ stakeholders.
Fluency in English


Why join us?

For two decades, we’ve been developing qualities and capabilities for adapting, reacting, and supporting change – building Reaktor to hold up in a fast-paced environment while ensuring the well-being of our people.

While Working At Reaktor, We’ll Provide You With


The ability to impact how you work. Together with the client, your team chooses the approach, technologies, and methodologies you think will work best in any given situation
A community with as much support as your heart desires
A team that’s not only experienced but considerate as well – they all want you to succeed
A sustainable work-life balance and support for your daily life outside of work. (e.g., free moving day, Reaktor car share, sick child care services, office space to use for your private events, etc.)
An opportunity to grow as a professional. In addition to the day-to-day work, we offer internal training courses, community events, and 15-minute coffee breaks to discuss hot topics in tech and design
A possibility to take part in more extended academy-like studies like Cloud Academy
300+ hobby clubs, from winter swimming and running to knitting and archery, that bring people together outside of (and sometimes inside) office hours. Many of these are supported by Reaktor
