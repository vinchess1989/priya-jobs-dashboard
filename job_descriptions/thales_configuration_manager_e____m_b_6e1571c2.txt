Mission:

Support of Configuration management team

The configuration manager E- & M- BOM (Engineering & Manufacturing BOM) ensures the proper creation of the bill of material for new products in our portfolio but also the maintenance of the actual portfolio. He/she will be responsible to verify completeness of the BOM’s before officializing it in the PLM system and communicating it towards the ERP system (OCM).

Assure the necessary product baselines (configuration) will be registered during the development and validation of the product. These baselines are linked to the development process (DDQS) defined by Thales.

He/she coordinates and monitors the execution of E- & M-BOM changes from development, verification and validation up to industrialization.

Mission Overview:

Due to the number of projects, we need reinforcements in the Configuration team. The PLM tool used is PTC's Windchill, linked to the CAD tool Creo.


Verify the content of the E- & M-BOM: structure, presence of BOM articles, necessary sub-assembly levels inclusive their documentation (DRW’s, production instructions, process inspections, production tools, …)
Perform impact analysis of product & process changes with the different departments (logistics, quality , Methods, purchasing, bid & projects)
Assure that all documents are validated (approvals) by the respective services before officializing them in the PLM system
Follow up in ECR and their related Change tasks, assure progress and completion within the desired time frame.
Fine tuning change implementation with logistics, production and purchasing (activation date, product obsolescence’s => scrapping of stock, retrofit, …)
Creation of Baseline BOM linked to project or contract mile stones
Manage all product AND process changes according internal & customer regulations
Manage M-BOM variants due to customer specific requirements



Mission Objectives:


Officialization and maintenance of E & M-BOM
Manage product and process changes
Create and implement standards, process flow improvements



Profile requirements:


>5 years of professional experience in configuration management role
Experience in a product and process development team
Experience with a PLM system, ideally Windchill
Rigorous, meticulous, and organized
Strong soft skills, effective communication
Language: French proficient , English proficient
