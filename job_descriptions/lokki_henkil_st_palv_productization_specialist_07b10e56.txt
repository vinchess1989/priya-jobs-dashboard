Productization Specialist
Hae paikkaa

We are looking for a Productization Specialist / Product Lifecycle Engineer to join a New Product Introduction (NPI) team responsible for bringing new products successfully from development into production.

Note! This application is only for individuals already residing in Finland, and relocation is not possible.

In this role, you will act as a key link between R&D, production, sales, and business operations, ensuring that new products, features, and solutions are implemented efficiently and according to agreed processes. You will play an important role in developing productization practices, supporting projects, and helping the organization scale new products into successful business.

Key Responsibilities:
Coordinate the implementation of new products into production
Develop and maintain productization concepts and best practices
Support R&D, production, and sales throughout different project phases
Ensure adherence to agreed business processes and transfer-to-business procedures
Gather and specify requirements for new functionalities and process improvements
Participate in testing and validation activities

What Were Looking For:
B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Engineering or a related field
Minimum 2 years of experience in productization, NPI, manufacturing engineering, or a similar role
Understanding of electronics and software product development environments
Knowledge of manufacturing operations and order-to-delivery processes
Experience with Azure DevOps or similar tools
Ability to define requirements, follow development work, and participate in testing (coding experience is not required)
Strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills

Answer these questions separately in a few sentences and attach them to your application! (Mandatory!)
- Do you have at least 2 years of experience in Productization, NPI (New Product Introduction), Manufacturing Engineering, or a similar role?*
- Have you worked with introducing new products, features, or solutions into production?*
- Are you familiar with electronics and/or software product development environments?*
- Do you have experience using Azure DevOps or similar lifecycle management tools?*
- Have you participated in testing, validation, or implementation activities for new products or processes?*

Please apply with your updated CV, that clearly demonstrates your skills and experience relevant to this role.
