About the role

As Service Manager, you will have responsibility for Fortum’s business-critical energy asset management application service built around IBM Maximo Application Suite.
You will ensure stable operations while driving the service roadmap, continuous development backlog with sprint model and continuous improvement together with business stakeholders, key users and technology partners.
Your responsibilities include governing the application management supplier, monitoring operational performance, coordinating changes and releases, planning development sprints and ensuring that lifecycle, security, access, recovery and licensing activities are managed effectively.
You will also develop demand-management and service-management practices using tools such as Jira, Confluence and ServiceNow.

 

About you

You have experience managing or coordinating enterprise applications, digital services or complex development activities, for example as a Service Manager, Product Owner, Project Manager or Scrum Master.
You understand IT service management, agile development and supplier collaboration, and you can translate business needs into clear priorities and executable plans. 
Experience with ERP or enterprise asset management solutions such as IBM Maximo, SAP or IFS is expected.
Familiarity with Jira and ServiceNow is valuable.
You work systematically, communicate clearly and build trust across business, IT and supplier organisations.
You take ownership, remain composed in a business-critical environment and are motivated to improve established ways of working.
Fluent English is required; Finnish or Swedish is an advantage.

 

We offer you

At Fortum, you will have a visible role in developing applications that support critical asset-management processes. You will receive support from an experienced senior team members, close collaboration with business and technology specialists, and structured onboarding into the application environment and operating model.

You will join Fortum’s experienced Asset & Locations Applications team and work closely with another Service Manager, internal IT specialists and international delivery teams. The position offers an opportunity to deepen your expertise in enterprise asset management, supplier governance and the development of large-scale, business-critical application services.

We offer a flexible hybrid working environment based in Espoo, opportunities to expand your expertise and responsibilities, and the chance to influence how a strategically important application service develops over the coming years.

We are committed to building diverse teams where everyone feels included and is treated equally. More information about our culture and benefits can be found here.
