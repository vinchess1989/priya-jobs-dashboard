You have a passion for technology and want to make the world a greener place?




Join the forefront of energy transition with E.ON's Digital Technology organization, as part of our technology in-house consulting, Expert Services. As a professional services organization, we collaborate closely with our business units, providing pivotal services that accelerate E.ON's ambitious Digital Transformation strategy.




If you have a passion for leading the green transition with digital innovation, become a playmaker in our energetic and diverse team of more than 500 Expert Services consultants.




It’s on us to make new energy work!




The Team – your impact




You will analyse deployment performance to define and embed policy-as-code guardrails that increase quality, efficiency and compliance across multiple CI/CD tools and platforms. Working with Product Owners and cross-functional teams, you will help monitor and standardise complex delivery pipelines, evolve release calendars to give visibility and explore safe use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to improve automation, observability and governance.




Your Role – meaningful & rewarding




Define and maintain standardised release and deployment practices, ensuring compliance with security, audit and enterprise architecture requirements.
Design, implement, and operate policy-as-code guardrails integrated into CI/CD pipelines to improve quality and control.
Establish and manage release calendars, deployment communications ensuring stakeholder visibility.
Monitor deployment performance and operational metrics; define KPIs, dashboards and reporting to drive continuous improvement.
Partner with Product, Engineering, Operations and Security to define deployment strategies, minimise risk, and improve release outcomes.




Your Profile – authentic & open-minded




Demonstrable experience in release management, deployment governance and DevOps in a complex enterprise environment.
Hands-on experience operating or improving CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Azure DevOps, GitLab, Jenkins) including artifact management.
Ability to design and implement policy-as-code controls and integrate them into delivery pipelines.
Strong analytical and data-driven mindset; confident using data to identify issues and drive measurable improvements.
Excellent stakeholder management, documentation and communication skills; able to coordinate across Engineering, Product, Operations, and Security.
Exposure to integrating AI/ML into DevOps workflows to improve automation, observability, and governance.
Container and orchestration ecosystem knowledge (Docker, Kubernetes) and GitOps practices.
Experience with observability tooling (metrics, logs, traces) and post-incident analysis.
Fluent in English; German is a plus.
