Why This Role Matters

ICEYE operates at the intersection of commercial space technology and sovereign defence, across a multi-country, multi-regulator footprint that includes Finland, Germany, Spain, Poland, the UK and Greece. We have an ISO 27001 management system, and multiple regulatory frameworks affect us: CRA, NIS2, indirectly DORA, their national implementations and national laws. Our customers include governments and defence agencies, and each brings its own national security requirements. Our compliance posture has to be credible to all of them: we have to turn requirements into things our products and teams do, and we have to be able to prove it.

We are enhancing our GRC capability in the ICEYE Security team that is responsible for this as a whole, across four areas: ISO 27001 and other standards compliance, regulatory compliance, interpreting product compliance requirements against engineering facts, and building compliance and governance as code. We are hiring Senior or Staff GRC engineers into this team. Our candidates are not expected to cover all areas below, but instead will cover these areas as a part of the larger Security team with GRC as their main responsibility. Successful candidates bring depth in a combination of them and work as individual contributors, closely with the other Security team focus areas (Security Engineering, Security Operations, Physical Security), IT, Legal, Engineering, Product Management and Lines of Business.

Who We Are

ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.

ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.

ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.

Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

Your Day-to-day Responsibilities

The position will evolve according to the needs of the company and the growing team. Depending on your profile, it is likely to include a combination of the following areas.

Compliance


Run the ISMS. Own the day-to-day operation of ICEYE's ISO 27001 ISMS, which the CSO formally owns, as one system: assessments, expansion, policy updates, and the certification and surveillance audit cycles.
Manage security risk: keep the risk register current and take each risk to its owner for treatment or acceptance.
Report to leadership on significant risks, unmet requirements, progress and decisions needed.
Seek and maintain other accreditations, for example, national ones.
Improve continuously: use lessons from incidents, audits and feedback, and create patterns that stop whole classes of findings from coming back.
Run the lifecycle of information security policies, standards and supporting documentation, from drafting and review to publication and retirement, so that they stay practical and enforceable rather than shelfware, offered in a self-serve fashion to stakeholders.


Regulatory compliance


Track and operationalise NIS2, the CRA, DORA where it reaches ICEYE through financial sector customers, and other EU security obligations across ICEYE's in-scope entities.
Set supplier and engineering security requirements and run the security assessments these obligations call for.


Product Compliance Requirements Against Engineering Facts


Ensure clients' and their national security requirements are translated clearly and accurately into ICEYE internal requirements across product, IT and other ICEYE functions, and that we can communicate those clearly to existing and new clients.
Support Product, the Missions business line, PMO and Sales through reusable, evidence-backed answers so that Sales, bids and programmes can answer recurring customer security questions themselves.


Compliance as code and governance as code


Ensure ICEYE has evidence of fulfilling its requirements as compliance as code whenever possible: collected automatically from the component that enforces the control, in a form a customer or auditor can verify.
Build governance as guardrails: policy-as-code checks and release readiness gates in CI/CD and infrastructure code, implementing policy-aligned “paved paths” as the default, and machine-readable constraints that design reviews and coding agents can use.
Operate what you build: keep the systems that audits and customer assurance depend on reliable and their data correct.


Must Haves

What we're looking for:


Eligible for being cleared for a Personal Security Clearance as necessary, as well as citizenship or permanent residency in the European Union.
Enough technical understanding to judge for yourself how a control is implemented: you can discuss modern software architecture and development, deployment and release concepts as well as security features with the engineers who implement them.
Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence without top-down authority.
Being self-directed and a self-starter.
Depth in a combination of the four areas above. You do not need all of them; tell us which ones are yours. For compliance and regulatory compliance, we are especially keen to find people who have driven compliance in their own organisation - not just audited others.


Nice To Haves


Relevant certifications such as CISSP, CISM, CISA, CRISC, ISO 27001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor.
Experience in a regulated industry or formal information security classifications, ideally with customer accreditation or acceptance.
A background as an engineer or system administrator, or a hobby coder (prior to LLMs making everyone one).
Practical use of language models and automation in your own work.
