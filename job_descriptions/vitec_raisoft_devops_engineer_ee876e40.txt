This role is based in Kokkola, Finland, with a hybrid working model that includes regular presence at our office.




Build the platform behind secure and reliable software delivery

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to take a leading, hands-on role in developing our Azure DevOps platform and delivery capabilities.




You will work close to development, architecture, and operations, with real ownership of the tooling, automation, and governance that enable our teams to build, test, release, and operate software efficiently and securely. This is a strong specialist role for someone who wants to improve engineering productivity, platform reliability, and security at the same time.




Our environment includes Azure DevOps, Jira, Terraform-managed platform configuration, Azure resources, secure service connections, CI/CD pipelines, access governance, and documentation-as-code practices. You will have ownership of both our Azure DevOps and Jira environments, ensuring they effectively support software delivery, collaboration, governance, and continuous improvement.




What you will do

Own and develop our Azure DevOps platform, including projects, repositories, pipelines, permissions, and governance.
Own and administer our Jira environment, including project configuration, workflows, permissions, automation, integrations, reporting, and governance.
Design, improve, and standardize CI/CD pipelines for build, test, release, and deployment flows.
Manage Infrastructure as Code with Terraform for Azure DevOps and related platform services.
Develop secure integrations for identities, secrets, service connections, artifact management, and container workflows.
Support and improve development and test environments running on Kubernetes and virtual machines.
Drive platform security, auditability, and operational quality together with internal stakeholders.
Troubleshoot incidents, remove bottlenecks, and continuously improve developer experience.
Maintain clear technical documentation, standards, and operating practices.




What we expect from you

Bachelor’s degree in computer science, software engineering, or equivalent experience.
Several years of experience in DevOps, platform engineering, cloud infrastructure, or CI/CD engineering.
Strong hands-on experience with Azure DevOps or a similar engineering platform.
Strong hands-on experience with Jira administration and governance.
Strong experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
Good understanding of Azure, identities, RBAC, secrets management, and secure cloud operations.
Experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
Strong understanding of Git-based workflows and pull request practices.
Ability to work independently, make sound technical decisions, and improve ways of working across teams.
Good communication skills in English.




It is a plus if you have

Experience with Azure Key Vault, workload identity federation, and service principals.
Experience with artifact repositories, package management, and container registries.
Experience in security-focused or compliance-driven environments.
Experience supporting multiple teams through shared platform services and standards.
Solid scripting skills with Bash, PowerShell, Python, or similar.




What we offer

A key technical role with real ownership and influence.
Opportunity to shape a modern Azure DevOps platform and engineering practices.
Meaningful work in a security-conscious SaaS environment.
Support for continuous learning and professional growth.
Competitive salary and benefits package.




About us

Vitec Raisoft develops SaaS solutions for healthcare and social care organizations around the globe. Our products help professionals assess care needs, support decision-making, improve quality of care, and create better outcomes for individuals. We work closely with our customers to develop solutions that are reliable, user-friendly, and make a meaningful difference in society. Read more at vitec-raisoft.com.




Vitec Software Group is a leading provider of Vertical Market Software and has its origin and headquarters in Umeå, Sweden. Our products are developed to meet the specific needs of various niche markets in society. The group consists of many independent business units, all sharing the ambition to contribute to society through business-critical software. Read more at vitecsoftware.com.
