We are looking for a   

 

Product Development Lead 

 

As a Product Development Lead, you will lead a cross-functional team that turns new machine capabilities into working, safe and release-ready solutions. Your team brings together software, electrical, hydraulic, and mechanical expertise, and you are responsible for planning the work, integrating the pieces and delivering the committed scope. You will work closely with Systems Engineers, NPD Engineering, Product Management, and Release Management, so that what your team builds follows the platform architecture and is ready for verification and release. You lead through ownership and collaboration: team members stay in their engineering organization, while you guide their daily work. The role is preferably based in Tampere, Finland, but other Normet locations can be considered. 

 

Main responsibilities and tasks:  

 

Lead the planning, prioritization and delivery of the team’s scope, including features, subsystems and product increments, ensuring commitments are delivered on time, within agreed effort and budget. 
Provide day-to-day leadership for a cross-functional development team, fostering ownership, accountability, and effective collaboration. 
Work closely with NPD Engineering to ensure the team has the right allocation and competencies required to achieve business and development objectives. 
Contribute to the performance and professional development of team members in cooperation with NPD Engineering. 
Drive execution in alignment with the defined system architecture and technical direction, while proactively identifying and escalating architectural impacts, risks and trade-offs. 
Lead integration, validation and testing activities, including simulation, SIL/HIL and prototype testing, ensuring readiness for verification and release. 
Identify, manage, and mitigate technical, resource and delivery risks, providing transparent status updates and escalation when needed. 
Ensure the team follows agreed engineering practices and quality standards, including code reviews, testing, documentation and CI/CD processes. 
 

To shine in this position, we hope you to have:   

 

B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Engineering or equivalent technical education. 
At least 8 years of experience in product development or engineering, ideally within mobile machinery, robotics, automotive or related industries. 
Demonstrated ability to lead and coordinate cross-functional teams in a matrix organization. 
Solid understanding of multidisciplinary system development and integration, including software, electrical, hydraulic and/or mechanical systems. 
Experience driving development activities, managing dependencies, and delivering results according to agreed schedules and objectives. 
Strong interpersonal and influencing skills, enabling effective collaboration without formal people management responsibility. 
Clear communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with technical experts and business stakeholders alike. 
A structured, pragmatic and hands-on approach to problem solving. 
Familiarity with safety-critical product development is an advantage. 
Fluent written and spoken English.
