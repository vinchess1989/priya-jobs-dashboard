SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT · FINLAND, SPAIN · FULLY REMOTE
Senior Cloud Engineer
Hae nyt

Easor is a financial management platform company that connects entrepreneurs with accounting firms. We build easy-to-use, highly automated software that streamlines financial processes and frees up entrepreneurs to focus on what matters most to them.

Our platform serves over 15,000 companies and hundreds of accounting firms across Finland, Sweden, Spain and Italy.

The Role

We are hiring a Senior Cloud Engineer with Azure experience to join our newly established DevEx team.

As Senior Cloud Engineer, you will bring deep Azure expertise to a high-trust team responsible for building and running the Azure infrastructure, and the broader developer experience: pipelines, tooling, and workflows other engineers depend on every day.

You will be expected to make judgment calls on infrastructure architecture and governance, bringing clarity and consistency to the team's technical decisions, while mentoring and growing the engineers around you. An immediate priority for this role is bringing the Azure tenant to full operational governance, with Infrastructure as Code adopted across the board.

What You Will Be Doing

Build and operate the landing zone. Design, implement, and run the Azure landing zone according to enterprise-scale best practices. Define and enforce Azure policies for consistent governance, monitor system health and set up alerting for early issue detection, and manage backup and restore procedures across all resources.

Automate with Infrastructure as Code. Lead the effort in infrastructure automation, provisioning and configuration management, using IaC tools such as Bicep and Terraform.

Operate core Azure services. Work hands-on with the resources that make up our platform: App services, Azure SQL, Service Bus, Event Hub, API management and Application Gateway.

Take part in architecture decisions. Work with our partners on major projects, bringing infrastructure and platform expertise into technical architecture decisions.

Manage identity and access. Administer Microsoft Entra ID, including access management and RBAC, in line with the team’s governance standards.

Manage network connectivity. Configure and maintain VNets, subnets, and private connectivity across the Azure tenant.

What We Are Looking For

Enterprise-scale Azure Engineer. You have worked hands-on with Microsoft Azure in real enterprise environments, managing multiple subscriptions and complex governance, for production workloads.

Hands-on Azure practitioner. You have experience running production-scale Azure workloads, including compute, data, messaging, and networking services.

Automation mindset. Infrastructure as Code is your default way of working. You have adopted it for production environments.

Detail-oriented. You care about getting the fundamentals right, naming, tagging, and policy structure, and hold the line on them as the environment scales.

Vocal in architecture discussions. You speak up and take a clear position when architecture decisions are being made, especially alongside partners for major projects.

Strong communicator. You explain technical decisions clearly, in writing and in conversation and document outcomes as a matter of habit.

Natural mentor. You enjoy helping other engineers around you grow and take the time to do it well.

Fluent English communication skills, Finnish or Spanish is a plus

What We Offer

The salary is 5000-6500 €/month depending on experience and location, plus country specific employee benefits, plus possible equity/stock incentives.

We also provide comprehensive employee benefits, including extensive occupational healthcare as well as lunch, phone, and wellness benefits.

Flexible location: Finland or Spain.

What We Value

At Easor, work is built on trust. You are given responsibility and freedom, along with the expectation that you take initiative and move things forward independently. We support one another as a team and share knowledge in our daily work. We continuously develop the way we work together by experimenting, learning, and improving as we go. Decisions are made close to everyday operations, and things don’t get left waiting.

Our work is guided by three core values:

Courage – we dare to try new things, challenge the status quo, and do things differently.

Drive – we move things forward with determination and take responsibility for results.

Care – we look after each other, our customers, and our partners.

We hope these are also reflected in the way you work.
