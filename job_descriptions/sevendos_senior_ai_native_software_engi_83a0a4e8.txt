Olenko sopiva tähän työhön?
Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Aimbition, part of Sevendos Group, helps software engineering organisations change how they build software with AI. We don’t bolt chatbots onto products. We work across the major hyperscaler platforms and build sovereign AI solutions, where models and data stay on the client’s own infrastructure. Our goal is always the same: when we leave, the client’s own engineers can run and extend what we built.

What You’ll Do

You’ll work in two of our core services.

Forward Deployed Engineering: you join a client’s engineering team and change how they build, test and ship software.


Bring AI into daily development: coding agents, code review, testing, CI/CD and documentation
Lead discovery and architecture discussions, and propose a pragmatic plan and platform, hyperscaler or sovereign
Run workshops and pair with developers so the new way of working sticks


AI Platform Foundation: you build the foundation that makes AI dependable in production.


Design and build LLM gateways, evaluations, observability, cost monitoring, knowledge layers, agents and MCP servers
Modernise development platforms and pipelines for AI-assisted work
Hand over working systems with documentation and runbooks


What we’re looking for


5+ years of hands-on software, DevOps or platform engineering. You’ve built real systems and can show how.
Solid engineering fundamentals: CI/CD, containers and Kubernetes, cloud (Azure, AWS or GCP) and private or on-prem environments, infrastructure as code, GitOps, refactoring and migrations.
AI is part of how you work. You use coding agents daily, know where they shine and where they fail, and check what they produce.
Client-facing experience, ideally as a consultant. You ask good questions before proposing solutions, can explain technical choices to developers and managers alike, and take ownership of the work.
Fluent English. Finnish is a strong advantage, as most of our client work is in Finnish.


Nice to have


Internal developer platforms (e.g. Backstage)
Agentic systems, knowledge layers, MCP, RAG or evaluations, or running open-weight models on private infrastructure
Tech lead or team lead experience
Talks, writing or open-source work


This role is probably not for you if…


you want to focus purely on coding without client contact
your AI experience is mainly demos or chatbots, without a strong engineering background
you’re early in your career (we’ll open junior roles separately)


How we hire

Two conversations. The first is about you, us and working with clients. The second is technical: you walk us through something you’ve built yourself.

The practicalities

We work hybrid from our Tampere and Helsinki offices, and travelling to client workshops is part of the job. You must be based in Finland and have the right to work here.

Why Aimbition


Real impact: see from the inside how AI changes software development across many organisations
Learning built in: the field moves weekly, and we give you time and support to keep up and get certified
Small company, solid backing: low hierarchy and fast decisions, with the stability, network and benefits of Sevendos Group
Technology is our craft, but people are our core. We work on trust, openness and respect.


Interested? Send your CV and a link to something you’ve built, such as your GitHub, a code sample or a project write-up. If you’d like more information about the role, feel free to contact us at recruitment@aimbition.com
