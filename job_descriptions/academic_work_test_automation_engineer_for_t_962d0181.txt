Test Automation Engineer for the banking sector, Helsinki
Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

Academic Work is hiring a Test Automation Engineer to support a banking-sector client in Helsinki, responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining automated test suites for payment systems.
The role requires at least one year of test automation experience and advanced skills in Python, Robot Framework, and Jenkins, plus ISTQB-level testing knowledge and fluent English.
The position offers a supportive team, flexible remote work two days a week, Epassi lunch deduction, and the assignment lasts at least six months with the client.
A credit check will be conducted for the selected candidate and the recruitment process includes a personality and a cognitive test.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



Here's an exciting opportunity to shape the future of testing in the banking sector! By joining Academic Work, you'll support our client in the banking sector, strengthen your skills in a complex and regulated environment, and grow within a forward-thinking team. Your prior experience in test automation, combined with your proactive and collaborative approach, will guarantee your success in this role.

About the role

As a Test Automation Engineer, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the quality and reliability of our client's payment systems. You will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining automated test suites, contributing to a robust and efficient testing process.

You are offered

Interesting and challenging scope to work with
A supportive team supporting your professional growth
Flexible remote work (2 days a week)
Epassi lunch deduction

Work tasks

Specializing in payment systems, this role drives the overall quality, reliability, and continuous improvement of software delivery through technical understanding and hands-on execution.

Key responsibilities include designing and implementing scalable test automation frameworks, formulating comprehensive test plans, and performing detailed test analysis, case design, and execution. The position requires deep analytical skills to conduct root cause analysis on complex defects within payment workflows, alongside strong stakeholder management to clearly communicate risks, testing progress, and quality standards across cross-functional teams.

We are looking for

At least 1 year of prior work experience with test automation within a corporate or enterprise environment
Advanced knowledge of Python, Robot Framework and Jenkins
Good general testing knowledge to the level of ISTQB Foundation Level
Experience with test tools and test management tools
Fluent English skills both written and spoken
Excellent problem-solving skills

It is meritorious if you have

Experience and/or knowledge of payments or financial domain
Experience managing complex systems consisting of components based on various technologies and diverse types of interfaces
Experience with AI-assisted test automation

To succeed in the role, your personal skills are:

Social
Responsible
Creative problem-solver
Intellectually curious

For at least the first 6 months, you will work on an assignment for our client in the banking industry. A credit check will be conducted for the selected individual.

Our recruitment process

This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
