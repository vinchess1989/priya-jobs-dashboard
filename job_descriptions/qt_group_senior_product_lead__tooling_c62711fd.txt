About Us

Ever wondered what powers the products people rely on every day? Chances are, we've helped build it.

Our software and development tools help build products for cars, hospitals, factories, homes, and industries worldwide. We help engineers solve complex challenges and create technology people can depend on.

Rooted in Nordic values and trusted by innovators worldwide, we combine deep technical expertise with a culture of curiosity, craftsmanship, and collaboration. With decades of engineering experience and a shared passion for technical excellence, we're helping shape the future of software and embedded development.

We take pride in building tools for people who build the world around us.

About The Role

We are looking for an experience Product Lead to execute and expand on our developer tooling strategy. The focus resides on creating an excellent Developer Experience, leading to faster development cycles and turn-arounds in an AI-first environment. Furthermore, alignment with internal and external stakeholders will drive our business cases to success.

The location for this role is Espoo or Oulu in Finland.

What You Will Do


Execute and steer the Developer Tooling strategy.
Enhance the existing developer experience for new and existing products.
Constantly assess our tooling products against customer expectations in a rapidly changing market.
Collaborate with our portfolio management and streamline our offering based on focused ROI and KPI definition.
Research and implement insights strategy to maximize value for our users.
Manage internal stakeholders including PM, R&D, IT, Marketing and Sales to ensure the success of the tooling strategy.


Who You Are


Collaborative: Happy working in a cross-functional team that spans time zones.
Analytical: Data-driven approach to take effective decisions and prioritization.
Results-oriented: Experience with outcome-based targets and objectives.
Organized: Excellent problem-solving, organizational, and analytical skills; ability to work effectively and manage stakeholders in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
Articulate: Able to simplify technical concepts clearly for non-technical stakeholders and decision-makers, both internal and external.


What You Bring To The Table


5+ years of experience in technical product management or similar roles in the software industry
Strong knowledge of software development processes and environments, IDEs, and tools
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. Master’s degree is a plus
Excellent written and verbal communication skills (proficient/native-level English)
Willingness to travel
