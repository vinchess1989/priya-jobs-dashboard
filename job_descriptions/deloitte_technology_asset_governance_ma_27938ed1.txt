Job Description

We are looking for a Technology Asset Governance Manager to join our Nordic CIO Office.




The Technology Asset Governance Manager is responsible for ensuring that technology asset information remains accurate, complete, reliable and fit for purpose throughout the asset lifecycle. You will own and maintain the asset database governance framework, translating policies and standards into effective processes, controls and data quality requirements to support operational, governance, risk and compliance needs.




You will oversee asset data quality, certification and facilitate or coordinate remediation of related governance and compliance issues. By maintaining relevant metrics, dashboards and KPIs, you will provide visibility into asset health and ensure reliable information is available to support activities such as incident response, patching, compliance monitoring, asset lifecycle management, and continuous improvement.




Working closely with stakeholders across the organization, you will continuously strengthen asset management processes and ways of working in alignment with ITIL and other recognized best practices.




Your team

You will be part of the Nordic CIO Office Team working from one of our Nordic offices with an option to work from home when required. You will be part of a fun and collaborative cross-functional team with extensive experience that is truly passionate about our work in securing Deloitte and our clients. We are currently a small team of 6 employees, spread across the Nordic countries.




Qualifications

Minimum of 3 years of relevant experience in IT Service Management (ITSM), ITIL Configuration Management, IT Asset Management (ITAM), application/service management, or technology governance.
Degree in Information technology, Information systems or related areas.
Practical experience working with a CMDB or technology asset/application inventory, ideally ServiceNow.
Experience establishing or operating data governance and data-quality controls, including ownership, mandatory attributes, validation, remediation and periodic reviews.
Experience monitoring and improving data against measures such as completeness, correctness and compliance.
Experience working across multiple stakeholder groups, for example application owners, service owners, infrastructure teams, security, risk, and platform teams.
Experience turning policies or standards into practical controls, KPIs, dashboards and remediation activities.
ITIL 4 Foundation and/or relevant ServiceNow training or certifications.
You write and speak English fluently.
Experience working locally in one of the Nordic markets is a must.




Desirable experience

Hands-on exposure to ServiceNow CMDB, including CMDB Health, CI classes/attributes, relationships, lifecycle management, identification/reconciliation and data certification/attestation.
Knowledge of ServiceNow CSDM (Common Service Data Model).
Experience with ServiceNow Discovery, Service Mapping or integrations/data feeds.
Experience supporting audit, risk, compliance or technology assurance activities involving technology inventory/configuration information.




Interpersonal skills

Stakeholder management - strong communication together with the ability to establish governance and influence stakeholders across technology teams and the business.
Analytical - strong data-analysis and problem-solving capability. Ability to identify trends, and comfortable working with dashboards, reports and large datasets.
Proactive and collaborative - strong sense of responsibility and ownership, combined with the ability to take initiative and work effectively as part of a team.
