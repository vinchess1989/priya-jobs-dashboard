Työpaikkakuvaus

Us

Voima is a Finnish, founder-led group building an ecosystem around precious metals: trading, financial services, refining, and security and logistics.

At the centre is Voima Account. Our aim is to make owning, buying, selling and storing physical gold and silver as easy, efficient and secure as possible. Everything else we build exists to support that.

The Role

As our Senior DevOps & Architecture Engineer, you take hands-on ownership of Voima's self-hosted platform infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, internal developer tooling, and security stack. This is a financial platform, customers hold real gold and silver on it, so security, reliability, and clean documentation are not extras. They are the job.

You'll own and evolve our deployment workflows, run our self-hosted services, manage secrets, and keep the virtualisation environment secure and reliable. Concretely: self-hosted GitLab CI/CD, Mattermost, and container registries; a Linux server virtualisation environment (VMs, Docker, NAS storage) automated with Ansible; HashiCorp Vault together with the public/private PKI and SSL/TLS certificate lifecycle; and administrative oversight of a few legacy and staging applications on Heroku.

This is a role built on trust. You'll work with a least-privilege model and clear access boundaries, and security-first thinking is the default here.

You'll also set how we work with any external partners, their access, security boundaries, and workflows, and own the standards that keep that clean.

You'll keep architecture, network flows, deployment pipelines and disaster-recovery runbooks documented and current.

You'll work in a small, senior, in-house IT team alongside our full-stack and mobile engineers, close to the people making product decisions.

We're looking for someone who owns problems end to end, works pragmatically and security-first, documents as they go, and finishes what they start.

What you'll own and build

Self-hosted GitLab CI/CD, Mattermost, and container registries, and secure deployment workflows for internal and agency developers

A Linux virtualisation environment (VMs, Docker, NAS) automated with Ansible

HashiCorp Vault, and the public/private PKI and SSL/TLS certificate lifecycle

Oversight of staging and PaaS-hosted applications, including selective migration into self-hosted setups where it makes sense

Clear, maintainable documentation: system architecture, network flows, deployment pipelines, disaster-recovery runbooks

Owning infrastructure and tooling, and setting the standards and workflows around them

Who this suits

You'll likely bring most of the following:

Strong experience administering self-hosted GitLab, building CI/CD pipelines, and managing Docker registries

Hands-on experience deploying, upgrading, and maintaining open-source, self-hosted web applications (Mattermost, wiki tools, monitoring stacks) on Linux

Solid Linux systems administration: virtual machines, host networking, and storage (NAS/SAN)

Practical infrastructure automation with Docker and Ansible

Practical experience setting up, configuring, and integrating HashiCorp Vault, alongside SSL/TLS certificate lifecycle management

A demonstrated ability to write clear, maintainable runbooks and network/architecture diagrams

Nice to have

Heroku account administration, or experience migrating applications off PaaS into self-hosted environments

Infrastructure as Code (Packer, Terraform, or the broader HashiCorp stack)

Experience coordinating access rights, security boundaries, and workflows for external agencies

What we offer

End-to-end ownership of the infrastructure behind a live financial platform

A small, senior team and direct proximity to product and leadership decisions

A real mandate to bring things in-house and build them properly, not just firefight

A stack worth owning: self-hosted GitLab, Docker, Ansible, HashiCorp Vault, Linux virtualisation

A salary of approximately €5,500–6,500 gross per month, depending on experience

Based in Helsinki city centre

If you can own critical infrastructure end to end, take security seriously, and want a real mandate to build it properly rather than hold it together, get in touch.

We don't wait for a ready-made path. We build it.
