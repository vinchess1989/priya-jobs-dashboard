This is a Hybrid role that will be based out of our Maynard, MA, San Jose, CA or Holmdel, NJ locations.

Meet the Team

Optics Operations Product Engineering team partners with cross-functional team of development engineers to design and sustain Optical transceiver components and modules throughout their product lifecycle. Product Engineers are self-motivated, result oriented, and strong-minded engineers who embrace creativity and new ways of thinking to deliver innovative solutions.

Your Impact

Optics Operations is an organization that enables and accelerates innovation to create an unrivaled customer experience & competitive advantage for Cisco through end-to-end operation leadership and product lifecycle management. In this role, you will be a key Optics Operations Engineering team member supporting the Cisco Optics Operations next generation product portfolio.

Your Responsibilities Include (but Not Limited To)

Collaborate with foundry to drive failure analysis yield loss and improve yield through process optimization


Collaborate with internal DFT and test teams to bring up and optimize test flow, set up specs.
Analyze ATE/WAT data and evaluate process corners, yield sensitivity.
Collaborate with internal DFT and engineering to Improve test coverage to screen downstream failures.
Correlate ATE test with bench test and system level test
Own overall yield roadmaps, cost analysis, and continuously yield improvement
Engage with wafer equipment suppliers, foundries, contract manufacture and test vendors to address issues of quality and performance
Establish manufacturing metrics to drive continuous improvement of equipment performance and process to meet wafer product specification
Drive the characterization of NPI & sustaining product yields including benchmark correlations to meet and/or exceed product cost and equipment capacity targets


Minimum Qualifications


MS or PhD Electrical Engineering and/or related engineering fields
proven experience working CMOS/Memory foundries or fabless design companies
Proven track record of transitioning products from NPI to High volume production through a contract manufacture partner
Experience in advanced CMOS technology nodes 7nm & beyond
knowledge and experience with design for manufacturability (DFM) during product development
Analytical skills on troubleshooting and JMP Scripting, Python, or AI models for automated yield prediction and monitoring
Background with product development tasks: Bill of Materials & Change Management


Preferred Qualifications


Strong experience with Advantest 93k ATE for high-performance semiconductor devices.
Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and problem-solving skills.
Optics or Silicon Photonics experience is a plus.
Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal skills with experience presenting senior leadership.
