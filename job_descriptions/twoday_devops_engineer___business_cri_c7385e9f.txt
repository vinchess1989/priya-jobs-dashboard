DevOps Engineer – Business-Critical Platforms

Digital Engineering | Copenhagen

Make software delivery more reliable, secure and efficient

We are looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to join Twoday and contribute to a large-scale digital healthcare environment where reliable software delivery and stable operations are essential.

You will work close to the development teams and help create the technical foundation that allows software to move safely and efficiently from development into production.

The environment involves several development teams and suppliers, so we are looking for someone who combines strong technical capabilities with a collaborative mindset.

What You Will Be Doing

As a DevOps Engineer, you will work across development, platform and operations, with a strong focus on automation and continuous improvement.

Your responsibilities will include:


Developing and improving CI/CD pipelines
Automating build, test, release and deployment processes
Working with Infrastructure as Code and configuration automation
Supporting containerised application environments
Improving monitoring, logging and observability
Troubleshooting issues across applications, infrastructure and deployment processes
Contributing to capacity management, backup, recovery and operational resilience
Supporting development teams with tooling, automation and delivery practices
Working systematically with vulnerabilities and security requirements
Contributing to the standardisation and continuous improvement of platforms and engineering processes


The technical environment may include Azure DevOps, Jenkins, Docker, Ansible, Infrastructure as Code, Linux, Git, Grafana and automated deployment tooling.

What We Are Looking For

You have several years of professional experience within DevOps, platform engineering, infrastructure engineering or a comparable discipline.

We are particularly interested in experience with:


CI/CD and automated software delivery
Azure DevOps, Jenkins or comparable technologies
Infrastructure as Code
Docker and containerised environments
Ansible or other configuration automation technologies
Linux
Git
Monitoring, logging and observability
Troubleshooting in complex production environments
Security and secure software delivery


Experience with Kubernetes and cloud platforms is an advantage, but we are more interested in the depth of your overall DevOps and platform engineering experience than whether you have worked with every technology in our environment.

Experience with business-critical public-sector solutions or digital healthcare is particularly relevant. The same applies to experience working across multiple development teams and suppliers.

Professional fluency in Danish, both spoken and written, is required. You must also be able to work onsite at the customer location in Copenhagen when required.

DevOps as part of engineering

At Twoday, we see DevOps as an integral part of software engineering rather than a separate function that becomes involved once development is complete.

You will work closely with developers, testers and architects to make software easier and safer to build, test, deploy and operate.

At the same time, you will become part of Twoday's wider professional community, with opportunities to share knowledge and develop your expertise alongside specialists across engineering, cloud, architecture, data and AI.

About Twoday

Twoday operates from modern offices across 30 locations in Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, and Lithuania. We have a strong and unique social environment with highly engaged employees. If you are the candidate we are looking for, you will play a central role with exciting opportunities to grow within an international and innovative group, working on cutting-edge technology and software solutions. You will also have the flexibility to occasionally work from one of our main offices in Oslo, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Helsinki or Vilnius.
