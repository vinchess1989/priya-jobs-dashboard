Integration Product Specialist





Why join us?


Be part of a key product team at the center of our company's digital transformation. We are investing in self-service applications stay ahead in the market, and you will play a big role in shaping its future.


As a Integration Product Specialist, you'll be working on a modern, high-performing platform that serves more than 70 000 clients and 3 million customers across 20 countries. You will be a key part part of delivering scalable, cloud-native applications using the latest technology, working in an agile, product-driven environment where your ideas matter.


If you enjoy working on complex challenges, want to make an impact on a global scale, and grow your skills in a modern tech stack, we would love to hear from you.


Why this role exists


We are looking for an Integration Product Specialist to support the successful implementation of our applications across multiple concurrent implementation projects, as we are rolling out our self-service applications across Intrum's markets. This role acts as the primary interface between product, engineering, and implementation teams, ensuring integrations are efficient, scalable, and do not become a bottleneck. You will be the key contact point for integration-related queries both between our different Group teams (Product, Mulesoft, Data) and for our markets who are active in the project and need to plan and conduct the necessary data and IT development on local side.


Responsibilities


Act as the main contact for integration-related questions
Proactively reach out and keep dialogue open between Group and Local teams
Support local teams' understanding of integration related work
Identify bottlenecks, recurring issues and drive improvements
Support implementation teams with:
API integrations (REST)
event-driven flows (Confluent Kafka)
data models and system behavior
Analyze and validate payloads and data
Investigate discrepancies between expected and actual outcomes
Support testing and go-live activities
Maintain and improve integration documentation
Identify recurring issues and drive improvements
Filter and escalate relevant issues to development teams
Support multiple parallel implementation projects

Requirements

Experience with system integrations (APIs and/or event-driven systems)
Ability to troubleshoot and analyze integration issues
Strong understanding of data structures and system flows
Experience working with SQL for data analysis and troubleshooting
Familiarity with PostgreSQL or similar relational databases
Ability to query and validate data to investigate integration issues
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
Structured and proactive way of working
Comfortable working in a multi-project environment
