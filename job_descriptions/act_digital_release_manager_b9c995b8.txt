Requirements

9+ years of professional experience in IT Release Management, Release Coordination, IT Operations, Deployment Management, or a related role.
Expert-level knowledge of Git, including branching strategies, repository management, merges, and complex merge conflict resolution.
Strong hands-on experience with Jira for release planning, ticket management, workflow coordination, and release tracking.
Strong experience working in Agile environments.
Proven experience managing production releases and deployment processes in complex, business-critical environments.
Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
Proven ability to make decisive, risk-based decisions under pressure, including postponing or aborting releases when required.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to coordinate technical and non-technical teams.
High level of rigor, precision, and attention to detail.
Proactive and solution-oriented mindset.




Responsibilities

Oversee release planning and compliance, ensuring Jira tickets, dependencies, approvals, and release activities follow established processes.
Manage Git branch merges, version control, and package compilation, ensuring stable and conflict-free deliveries.
Coordinate and lead release alignment meetings across development, operations, and other relevant teams.
Prepare production releases by compiling final delivery manifests, deployment requirements, dependencies, and operational procedures.
Assess release readiness and proactively identify technical, operational, and business risks.
Make authoritative decisions on release execution, including the ability to postpone, stop, or abort releases when production stability is compromised.
Act as the primary technical point of contact for internal and external teams regarding release status, dependencies, risks, and specifications.
Maintain full visibility of the release landscape, ensuring multiple concurrent release workflows are properly coordinated.
Identify and resolve release bottlenecks, conflicts, and operational issues.
Continuously improve release management processes, governance, and operational efficiency.
