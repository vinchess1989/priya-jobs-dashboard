Job description
Are you a proactive problem-solver with a background in engineering and an interest in cutting-edge AI solutions? We are now looking for a AI & Technical Documentation Specialist to join AI-Priori Global Oy in Tampere to help build their growing Finnish operations and bridge the gap between industrial machinery and modern AI!

About the role

As an AI & Technical Documentation Specialist, you will play a key role in delivering AI-driven solutions for global manufacturing industry clients. Your primary focus will be on customer projects where AI tools are used to streamline technical documentation-such as updating commissioning and maintenance manuals based on change specifications.

Rather than deep coding, this role is about understanding customer needs, gathering the right technical data, and applying AI-Priori's proprietary AI tools in practice. You will work closely with the Finnish country lead in Tampere while collaborating daily with an international team across the UK, Europe, the US, and India. As one of the first hires in Finland, you will get to work with the latest AI technologies, receive comprehensive training on the tools, and have the opportunity to shape your own career path as the local team grows.

Work tasks


Client Discovery & Communication: Act as the main point of contact for manufacturing clients during project kick-offs, gathering necessary information, change papers, and technical documents.
AI Tool Application: Utilize and apply AI-Priori's proprietary AI tools to process, update, and verify technical manuals and documentation.
Testing & Reporting: Identify potential bottlenecks or issues in the workflow and report findings clearly to the internal software development team for further tool optimization.
Stakeholder Collaboration: Work seamlessly with both local Finnish industrial clients and international colleagues across different time zones.


We are looking for


An educational background and relevant experience in mechanical, electrical, or software engineering. Familiarity with the manufacturing industry, machinery, or production lines is a strong advantage.
Fluency in English (written and spoken) is essential, as it is the daily working language. Fluency in Finnish is a major plus for communicating with local clients, though not strictly required for the right profile.
You are a self-starter who isn't afraid of uncharted territory. You take initiative without needing rigid instructions, ask for help early when needed, and enjoy interacting with customers.
Genuine interest in AI and modern digital tools. You don't need to be a programmer-full training on AI-Priori's platform will be provided.
Readiness to work on-site at client locations during project start-ups (approx. 2 weeks) and travel occasionally in Finland and abroad.

Our recruitment process

This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client's wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.

AI-Priori Global Oy

AI-Priori Global Oy is a technology company specializing in AI-assisted data analytics, automation, and machine learning solutions for B2B clients. With an established international presence across the UK, Europe, the US, and India, the company is now actively expanding its footprint in Finland to help manufacturing industry leaders optimize their operations and decision-making.
