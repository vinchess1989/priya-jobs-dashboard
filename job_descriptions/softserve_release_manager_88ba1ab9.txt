About The Role

In this role, you will be responsible for overseeing the release management process, ensuring smooth, predictable, and high-quality software releases across the organisation. You will coordinate Release Trains, manage deployment readiness, maintain operational procedures, and drive continuous improvements to release management practices, helping teams deliver software efficiently and reliably.

Responsibilities


Plan and manage Release Trains and release schedules across multiple teams and workstreams
Coordinate release readiness activities and support go-live execution
Create, maintain, and continuously improve deployment Runbooks, rollback plans, and release checklists
Facilitate production deployments and coordinate post-release support activities
Identify, manage, and communicate release risks, issues, dependencies, and mitigation plans
Ensure operational readiness and alignment between development, QA, DevOps, and business stakeholders
Drive continuous improvement initiatives within release management processes and practices
Support stakeholder communication throughout the release lifecycle
Monitor release performance and contribute to delivery excellence and operational stability



Requirements


3+ years of experience in Release Management, Technical Project Management, DevOps, or related roles
Experience managing Release Trains and release planning activities
Strong understanding of Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), Agile methodologies, and DevOps practices
Experience creating and maintaining deployment Runbooks, rollback procedures, and operational documentation
Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and release automation tools
Strong coordination, communication, and stakeholder management skills
Experience with Azure DevOps, Jira, Confluence, or similar delivery and collaboration tools
Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliverables in a fast-paced environment
Strong organisational, problem-solving, and risk management skills
Availability to travel up to two weeks per month
