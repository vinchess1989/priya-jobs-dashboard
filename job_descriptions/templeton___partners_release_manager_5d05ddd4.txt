ERP Platform Release Manager

We’re looking for an experienced ERP Platform Release Manager to own the planning, coordination, and governance of our ERP platform release lifecycle. You’ll ensure that enhancements, configuration changes, integrations, and projects are delivered in a controlled, predictable, and business-aligned way—minimising risk and disruption while maximising value.




About the Role

As the central point of coordination for all ERP releases, you’ll work across functional teams, infrastructure, integration specialists, business stakeholders, vendors, and project managers to drive successful deployment and adoption of platform changes.

You’ll be responsible for:

Planning and coordinating the end-to-end release lifecycle for the ERP platform and associated applications, integrations, and technology components
Managing deployment of ERP enhancements, patches, fixes, and projects in line with IT change management processes
Owning release schedules, dependencies, risks, and conflicts—and communicating proactively with all stakeholders
Overseeing release communications, stakeholder notifications, and go/no-go decision points
Ensuring robust rollback and contingency plans are defined, documented, and tested
Serving as the primary contact for release coordination across business, IT, and external vendors/system integrators
Providing regular, clear status reporting to leadership and key stakeholders on release progress, risks, and outcomes

What You’ll Bring

Proven experience as a Release Manager, Release Coordinator, or similar within complex ERP environments (e.g. SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, or similar)
Strong understanding of ITIL/ITSM change and release management practices and how they apply to ERP platforms
Demonstrable ability to coordinate cross-functional teams, manage dependencies, and resolve scheduling conflicts
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with the confidence to engage senior business and IT leaders
Experience working with external vendors and system integrators in a release governance capacity
A structured, risk-aware mindset with strong organisational and documentation skills
Familiarity with release automation, CI/CD concepts, or DevOps practices in an ERP context is a plus
