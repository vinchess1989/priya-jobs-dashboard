Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
How You Will Make a Difference

Our R&D team is looking for an R&D Project Manager

You will lead the R&D projects and coordinate the cross-functional team assigned to them. You will be responsible for defining and maintaining its scope, financial plan, schedule, and risk management plan. In this role, you will report to the R&D Manager.

What Is Involved

You will ensure that the project is executed in line with best practices and STERIS policies, while effectively monitoring progress and making efficient use of resources. You will also build and maintain strong relationships with internal stakeholders and keep all stakeholders informed through clear, timely communication.

We Value Competence And Experience In The Following Areas


DFMEA (Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis): leading or facilitating DFMEA work to identify, assess, and mitigate design risks early in product development.
Product Quality Assurance Plan: creating and following up a quality assurance plan that ensures the product meets its requirements throughout the project lifecycle.
Logical and modular product structures: understanding how products are built from logical structures and modules to support configurability, reuse, and efficient manufacturing.
Experience with product data management is a strong advantage – for example, EBOM, MBOM and SBOM structures and PLM/PDM tools such as PTC Windchill. We are building a governed product architecture that supports logical product structures, module reuse and configurable products, and creates a seamless digital thread from customer requirement to delivered equipment. As R&D Project Manager, you ensure that our projects deliver product data in line with this target.


Our ideal colleague

As a colleague, you are persistent, able to convince colleagues at different levels of the organization, and able to prioritize the needs of different stakeholders. We do serious business, with a good vibe!

Why STERIS

STERIS Tuusula's long and reliable history ensures positive prospects for your career. We have many professionals from different fields in long-term employments, which shows that skilled people enjoy working with us. We offer you the opportunity to become part of a successful community that works as a team on demanding projects as part of a large international group. In your work, you can utilize the group's international network – experts, best practices and collaboration with colleagues in different countries. At STERIS Tuusula, you have the opportunity to grow and develop alongside our experienced professionals.

In addition to a competitive salary, we offer you, among other things, recreational benefits, comprehensive health care services, an extensive insurance package, access to our own gym, and the opportunity to take part in various leisure activities according to your interests.
