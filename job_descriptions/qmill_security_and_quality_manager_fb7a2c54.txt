Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Are you interested in quantum computing and information security? Would you like to be part of a team unlocking the quantum advantage? Are you willing to drive and shape what this technology becomes next?




About this position

QMill is developing quantum-powered security for critical infrastructure. Our Infosec product line, QMill Quantum Powered Security, combines standards-based post-quantum key establishment with verifiable quantum randomness and our own quantum-advantage key exchange. It is delivered as software into existing network equipment, and we have demonstrated it with Telia in a live mobile network. Get to know us further by visiting qmill.com.




We are now looking for a Security and Quality Manager to join our product management team in Espoo, Finland. You will own security and quality across our products, and make sure that what we ship is secure, correct and certifiable. You lead security engineering and systematic testing in a small product team, and work closely with our quantum algorithm and software developers, partners and customers.




What will you do

Own the product security and quality process, roadmap and targets of our quantum powered security products
Be accountable for threat models and security analyses, and for the review of our cryptographic implementations
Design, drive and implement the certification of the product, such as FIPS 140-3, NIST SP 800-90B, BSI AIS 31 and EUCC, with labs and auditors
Establish and govern secure development practices, including vulnerability handling and supply-chain security
Define the test strategy and own test automation and quality gates in our CI/CD pipelines
Design, plan and perform security, performance and interoperability testing, including field tests with operators
Own release quality from acceptance criteria to test reporting




We are looking for

Minimum of 7 years of experience in security engineering, security testing or software quality assurance, with ownership of product security or quality
Hands-on experience with applied cryptography and security protocols, such as TLS or IKEv2/IPsec
Experience in test automation and CI/CD for complex or distributed systems
Experience in leading security standards compliance, product certification or audits is a plus
Knowledge of post-quantum cryptography or random number generation is a plus
Good programming skills in Python and C, C++, Rust or Go, and experience with Linux and containers
Good working experience and skills with modern AI tools, such as AI coding assistants and agents, and using them effectively and securely in development, testing and security analysis
Experience in or understanding of quantum computing, or at least a keen interest in it
Master’s degree in computer science, information security, software engineering, mathematics or physics




What you need to succeed

Strong ownership, and good at leading, communicating and collaborating with people
Fluent in written and spoken English
Systematic and detail-oriented, with a healthy attacker’s mindset
Able to set priorities, make decisions independently and understand the big picture
Flexible and adaptive to handle the uncertainty of a new technology area




What we are offering

Visionary work in quantum security. Our research agenda is dedicated to finding quantum advantage algorithms that can solve real challenges in society and business, so your work will be groundbreaking with a real, positive impact.
Great career opportunities, and superb colleagues. Our personnel are highly educated professionals. Collaborating in a multi-disciplinary team with top talent is a source of learning, inspiration and fun.
Support for your well-being. Without our personnel we cannot succeed, so we take good care of them and enable a good work-life balance.
