About Newcode

We build AI tools for firms in legal and professional services: software people use for real work, not demos. We started a few months ago and already have paying clients who rely on what we ship. The team is small and moving fast.

The role

You'll own the infrastructure our AI products run on. That means the cloud environments, the deployment pipelines, and the Kubernetes setup that keeps everything serving reliably under load. A lot of the interesting work is specific to running AI in production: serving models cost-effectively, scaling for uneven demand, and keeping confidential client data properly isolated in a multi-tenant setup.

You're joining early enough to shape how we do this rather than inherit someone else's decisions. You'll set the practices, make the architecture calls, and live with them.

Requirements

Stack

Azure, Kubernetes (AKS), Terraform, Helm, GitHub Actions. Microservices, multi-tenant architecture, infrastructure-as-code throughout.

What The Job Involves


Building and running our cloud environments and CI/CD pipelines so deployments are fast, repeatable, and boring
Owning reliability: monitoring, alerting, and the kind of automation that means nobody gets paged for the same thing twice
Keeping security and tenant isolation tight, since clients trust us with confidential material
Managing the infrastructure behind model serving and AI workloads, including cost and scaling under uneven demand
Working with the engineers shipping features so the path to production stays smooth as the team grows


Who You Are


At least seven years running production infrastructure for web applications
You automate by default and treat manual, one-off fixes as a smell
You think about failure, blast radius, and recovery, not just the deploy that goes well
You take security and multi-tenant isolation seriously and can reason about where data is allowed to flow
You use AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) and use them well. Faster output, but you understand and stand behind every change
You're fine in a startup that changes direction. Decisions get made, then revisited


Experience we expect with the stack:


Terraform: 5+ years
Kubernetes (AKS): 5+ years
Azure: 3+ years
Helm: 3+ years
CI/CD, ideally GitHub Actions: 2+ years
Microservices and multi-tenant architecture: proven, hands-on track record


Nice to have:


Python, enough to write and maintain real tooling rather than just glue scripts
PostgreSQL administration: tuning, backups, replication
Comfortable scripting in Bash
You already work with AI agents day to day
Experience running GPU or model-inference workloads
Observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)
A security or compliance background relevant to handling sensitive data
