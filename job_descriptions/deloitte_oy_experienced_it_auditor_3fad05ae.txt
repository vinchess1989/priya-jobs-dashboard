Job description

In Deloitte, we want to make an impact that matters to the people around us, our clients, and society. Are you up for the task? Deloitte's IT, Data & Analytics (IDA) team is currently looking for an Experienced IT Auditor who thrives in a dynamic and engaging environment.

Who are we?

You will join Deloitte Audit & Assurance as a member of our IT, Data & Analytics (IDA) team. We help clients transform the ways in which they leverage people, technology, processes, and controls to address their financial and operational risks and drive performance within their business operations. IT audit work can include assignments relating to the provision of assurance or advice.

IT audit typically evaluates and reports on the procedures and control environment surrounding an organization's IT systems, aiming to improve the risk management. We do so in the following ways:

We identify risks and weaknesses, thus enabling the development of solutions for introducing controls over processes supported by IT.
We audit and advise on general IT controls and automated application controls within the area of IT audit and support financial auditing tasks.
We provide clients with assurance reports on several topics, including general IT controls in an outsourcing environment, and application and process controls.
We support clients in compliance with IT and cyber regulations.
We work across functions within Deloitte locally and globally to provide our clients with the right set of skills.

Qualifications

To be successful in this role, we expect that you:

Have graduated in relevant study field (e.g., audit, risk management, business, IT, accounting)
Have 1-4 years of full-time professional work experience, preferably with auditing information technology systems or any related field (IT controls, internal controls, GITC management, IT management & administration) gained from another consulting firm or large organization (private or public)
Have knowledge to identify and assess IT risks and controls to relate them to business processes and to express opinions in a clear manner.
Have solid teamwork skill and enthusiastic attitude to work with others, both with colleagues and customers
Have a keen interest in leveraging AI and emerging technologies to improve IT audit processes and ways of working
Are fluent in spoken and written English. Proficiency in Finnish is considered an advantage

In addition, we consider it an advantage if you have:

Hands-on experience related to SAP ERP products. E.g., System Configuration, User & Access Management, Change Management, Business process management.
Project management and coordinating skills
Knowledge of third-party assurance standards such as SOC 1/2/3, ISAE 3402, and ISAE 3000, as well as knowledge of some of the relevant regulatory and IT standards, frameworks, and best practices (e.g., DORA, NIS2, ISO27001, GDPR, COBIT, NIST).
Relevant certifications e.g,, CISA, CISSP, CISM
Experience of SAP systems from audit, system administration or configuration perspective

To be considered for this position, a prerequisite is that you are currently allowed to work in Finland, holding a valid residence permit if required.
