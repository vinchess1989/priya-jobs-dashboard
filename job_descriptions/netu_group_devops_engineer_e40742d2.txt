Join our experienced team and work with our Software Developers and Engineers to implement mission critical IT solutions for large size organizations in various sectors, including law enforcement and justice, asylum and migration, social security, lands and town planning, financial services, insurance, telco in Cyprus, Greece, Malta, Croatia and other regions.




Responsibilities

We are looking for a DevOps engineer who will be responsible for optimizing the software development lifecycle from deploying product updates, identifying production issues, implementing integrations, to maintenance and updates.




Qualifications

University or College graduate in Computer Science or in a related field
Strong experience with Git and branching strategies (e.g., Git Flow, trunk-based development)
Experience with the following tools: Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and Azure DevOps, Kubernetes (K8s)
Hands-on experience with Docker (creating, managing, and optimizing containers)
Proficiency in Bash, Python, Groovy (for Jenkins), or PowerShell
Experience writing scripts to automate builds, tests, and deployments
Knowledge of Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK Stack for monitoring CI/CD processes will be considered an advantage
Experience with Terraform, Ansible or Helm will be considered an advantage
Experience integrating observability into CI/CD pipelines will be considered an advantage
Familiarity with Azure or on-prem CI/CD solutions
Experience with cloud-native tools (Azure DevOps) will be considered an advantage

Skills & Competencies

Ability to create, maintain, and optimize CI/CD pipelines for automated builds, tests, and deployments
Ability to define and manage infrastructure using IaC principles will be considered an advantage
Can write clear CI/CD workflow documentation
Ability to troubleshoot build/deployment failures
Analytical mind and problem-solving aptitude
Attentive to detail, methodical, diligent
Ability to perform well in a fast-paced environment
Ability to work independently and as a member of a project team
Excellent knowledge of the English language
Continuous Improvement Mindset: remain updated with DevOps and CI/CD trends
